## 1. Main results and dependency ledger

Let $Z \sim \mathcal{CN}(0, 1)$ be a standard proper complex Gaussian ($\mathbb{E}|Z|^2 = 1$, density $\pi^{-1}e^{-|z|^2}$ on $\mathbb{C}$, $R_0 := \mathbb{E}|Z| = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} = 0.8862269254\dots$), and let $q \in \mathcal{H}_{2,1}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ ($n \ge 1$ arbitrary) be a unit-variance bidegree-$(2, 1)$ complex Wiener-chaos polynomial independent of $Z$. Put
$$U := |q|^2 \quad (\mathbb{E}[U] = 1), \qquad \chi := \mathbb{E}[U^2] - 2 \in [0, 42], \qquad \kappa_6^{\mathbb{C}}(q) := \mathbb{E}[U^3] - 6 - 9\chi \le C_6\,\chi^{3/2},$$
with $C_6 = \frac{4045313}{100000} = 40.45313$ (`STATUS.md` `3.4`, **Lean**: `Grothe/Lower/Moment.lean`, `Grothe/LowerAnalytic/Moments.lean`, `Grothe/LowerAnalytic/CircularFourth.lean`). Conditioning on $U = v \ge 0$ expresses the Gaussian-buffered first moment as
$$\mathbb{E}\big|Z + \sqrt{s}\,q\big| = \mathbb{E}\big[\rho(s U)\big], \qquad \rho(x) := \mathbb{E}\big|Z + \sqrt{x}\big| = R_0\,e^{-x/2}\Big[(1 + x)I_0\!\left(\tfrac{x}{2}\right) + x\,I_1\!\left(\tfrac{x}{2}\right)\Big] \qquad (x \ge 0). \tag{1}$$

In [`STATUS.md`](../../STATUS.md) `3.5a` (`buffered-rice-codex-a7c4/PROOF.md`, **P + C**, reviewed in `#735`), moment-only covers proved the sharp Gaussian inequality $\mathbb{E}|Z + \sqrt{s}\,q| \le R_0\sqrt{1 + s}$ for $0 \le s \le \frac{1}{6}$, yielding the exact remote cubic energy cap $\|P_{2,1}^{\mathrm{rem}} k\|_2^2 \le \frac{\pi}{4} - t^2$ down to $t \ge R_0\sqrt{6/7} = 0.8204869\dots$. That cutoff left `[0.751, 0.8205]` in the priced-peak consumer `8.13` (`#769`) using an inflated energy envelope, missed six rationalised degree-5 row amplitudes `0.70–0.80` in `#453` / `#751`, and did not reach the intermediate-$t$ window $t \in [\frac{1}{4}, \frac{13}{20}]$ of [`#628` `ATK P3`](../../../../higher-degree/sources/degree5-attack-2026-11-25/problems/P3-mid-t-direct-bound.md).

In the pure moment relaxation $(\mathbb{E}[U] = 1,\, \mathbb{E}[U^2] = 2 + \chi,\, \mathbb{E}[U^3] \le 6 + 9\chi + C_6\chi^{3/2})$, two-atom laws concentrating nearly all mass near $U \approx 1$ exceed $R_0\sqrt{1 + s}$ past $s \approx \frac{1}{6}$ (whereas actual cubics such as the single-coordinate Wick monomial $q_1(z) = z_1(|z_1|^2 - 2)/\sqrt{2}$ stay well below $R_0\sqrt{1 + s}$, e.g. $\mathbb{E}\rho(|q_1|^2) < 1.193 < \sqrt{\pi/2}$ at $s = 1$), while pure Stein bounds cannot reach $\chi \to 0^+$ alone (any nonzero Stein test $\phi$ couples $c_1 + c_2 - 3$ of norm $\Theta(\sqrt{\chi})$ against $O(1)$ test terms, paying $+c\sqrt{\chi} \gg \Theta(\chi)$ as $\chi \downarrow 0$). By combining a **deterministically centered two-step Ornstein–Uhlenbeck cumulant expansion at $V_L \equiv 1$** for small $\chi$ with a **pure-$v$ Level-2 Matrix Stein + 5-scalar Lean Gram LP certificate** against a **complete-monotonicity Hermite cubic Rice cap bank** for bulk and high $\chi$, we prove:

### Theorem 1 — Sharp Gaussian-buffered complex cubic radius through $s \le \frac{5}{8}$ (`STATUS.md` `3.5a`, **P + C, author only**)

*For every dimension $n \ge 1$, every $q \in \mathcal{H}_{2,1}(\mathbb{C}^n)$, and every $\sigma, \tau \ge 0$ with $0 \le s := \frac{\tau^2}{\sigma^2}\|q\|_2^2 \le \frac{5}{8} = 0.625$ (when $\sigma > 0$),*
$$\mathbb{E}\big|\sigma Z + \tau q\big| \le \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\sqrt{\sigma^2 + \tau^2\|q\|_2^2}, \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad \mathbb{E}\big|Z + \sqrt{s}\,q\big| \le \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\sqrt{1 + s} \quad \big(\|q\|_2 = 1,\; 0 \le s \le \tfrac{5}{8}\big). \tag{2}$$
*Equality holds along asymptotic Gaussian sequences $q_N = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\sum_{j=1}^N e_{2,1}(z_j)$ ($\chi(q_N) = \frac{42}{N} \to 0$), so the constant $\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\sqrt{1 + s}$ is sharp at every $s \in [0, \frac{5}{8}]$. Moreover, for every $0 < s \le \frac{5}{8}$ and every nonzero finite-dimensional $q \in \mathcal{H}_{2,1}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ ($\chi > 0$ by `STATUS.md` `6.3.32`), inequality `(2)` is strict.*

### Corollary 1a — Exact remote cubic energy frontier down to $\sqrt{2\pi/13} < 0.695215 < \frac{7}{10}$ (`STATUS.md` `3.5a`)

## 3. Regime B: Deterministically centered two-step Ornstein–Uhlenbeck remainder at $V_L \equiv 1$

### 3.0 Self-contained derivation and integrability of `(6)` from the reviewed smooth-test identity

Fix $s > 0$, put $N_0 := \sqrt{2}\operatorname{Re} Z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$, $F := \sqrt{2}\operatorname{Re} q \in \mathcal{H}_3(\mathbb{R}^{2n})$ ($\mathbb{E}[F^2] = 1$, $\kappa_4(F) = \frac{3}{2}\chi$, $\kappa_6(F) = \frac{5}{2}\kappa_6^{\mathbb{C}}(q)$ by circularity), $A_s := 1 + s$, and $B_{s,\theta} := 1 + s(1 - \theta)$ for $\theta \in [0, 1]$. By circularity of $Z + \sqrt{s}\,q$,
$$2\,\mathbb{E}\big|Z + \sqrt{s}\,q\big| = \pi\,\mathbb{E}\!\left|\frac{N_0}{\sqrt{2}} + \sqrt{\frac{s}{2}}\,F\right| = \mathbb{E}_F\big[h(F)\big], \qquad h(x) := \frac{\pi}{\sqrt{2}}\,\mathbb{E}_{N_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)}\big|N_0 + \sqrt{s}\,x\big|.$$
Differentiating in $x$ gives $h'(x) = \frac{\pi\sqrt{s}}{\sqrt{2}}(2\Phi(\sqrt{s}x) - 1)$ and $h''(x) = \sqrt{\pi}\,s\,e^{-sx^2/2}$, so $h \in C^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ is even of linear growth with bounded derivatives of all orders $\ge 1$ ($h^{(2+m)} \in \mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R})$ for all $m \ge 0$ — no random $V_L$ scale mixture or $\varepsilon \downarrow 0$ smoothing limit is needed). Applying the reviewed two-step Stein resolvent identity (`edgeworth-codex-a7c4/PROOF.md` §2 Eqs. `(6)` & `(9)`, **P**, reviewed by `claude-b9ee` in `#445`; `STATUS.md` `3.19`) directly to $h$ gives
$$2\,\mathbb{E}\big|Z + \sqrt{s}\,q\big| = \sqrt{\pi}\sqrt{1 + s} - \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{16}(1 + s)^{-3/2}s^2\chi + \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{96}(1 + s)^{-5/2}s^3\kappa_6^{\mathbb{C}}(q) + R_2(s, q), \tag{5}$$
where, with $U_{\mathbb{R}} := \|DF\|^2 - 3 = U_2 + U_4 \in \mathcal{H}_2 \oplus \mathcal{H}_4$, $v := \phi_h^{(5)} - \mathbb{E}_N[\phi_h^{(5)}(N)]$, and $R_\lambda f := -\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda u} P_u f\,du$,
$$R_2(s, q) = -\frac{1}{27}\sum_{k \in \{2, 4\}}\sum_{l \in \{0, 2, 4, 6, 8\}}\mathbb{E}_F\!\Big[\big(R_{2 - l/3} R_{4 - k/3} v\big)(F)\,U_{\mathbb{R}}\,\Pi_l(U_{\mathbb{R}} U_k)\Big].$$
To evaluate $R_{2-l/3} R_{4-k/3} v$ explicitly, apply the OU semigroup $P_t$ ($\theta_0 := e^{-2t} \in (0, 1]$) to the Gaussian $h''(x) = \sqrt{\pi}\,s\,e^{-sx^2/2}$:
$$(P_t h'')(x) = \sqrt{\pi}\,s\,B_{s,\theta_0}^{-1/2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{q_0}{2}x^2\right), \qquad q_0 := \frac{s\theta_0}{B_{s,\theta_0}}.$$
Since $\phi_h(x) = -\int_0^\infty e^{-t}(P_t h')(x)\,dt$ and $\frac{d}{dx}[e^{-t}(P_t h')(x)] = e^{-2t}(P_t h'')(x)$, differentiating four more times in $x$ via $\frac{d^4}{dx^4}e^{-q_0 x^2/2} = q_0^2\,h_4(q_0 x^2)$ with $h_4(w) := (w^2 - 6w + 3)e^{-w/2}$ and substituting $\theta_0 = e^{-2t}$ ($dt = \frac{d\theta_0}{2\theta_0}$) yields
$$\phi_h^{(5)}(x) = -\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\,s^3\int_0^1 \theta_0^2\,B_{s,\theta_0}^{-5/2}\,h_4\!\left(\frac{s\theta_0 x^2}{B_{s,\theta_0}}\right)d\theta_0.$$
For any $u \ge 0$, $P_u\big[B_{s,\theta_0}^{-5/2}h_4(\frac{s\theta_0(\cdot)^2}{B_{s,\theta_0}})\big](x) = B_{s,\theta_0 e^{-2u}}^{-5/2}h_4(\frac{s\theta_0 e^{-2u}x^2}{B_{s,\theta_0 e^{-2u}}})$, whose stationary Gaussian expectation at $N \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ is $B_{s,\theta}^{-5/2}\cdot 3(1 + \frac{s\theta}{B_{s,\theta}})^{-5/2} = 3 A_s^{-5/2}$. Substituting $\theta_1 = e^{-2u_1}, \theta_2 = e^{-2u_2} \in (0, 1]$ in $R_{2-\beta} R_{4-\alpha} v$ ($\alpha = k/3, \beta = l/3$) and changing variables from $\theta_0$ to $\theta := \theta_0\theta_1\theta_2 \in (0, 1)$ ($\theta_0^2 d\theta_0 = \frac{\theta^2}{\theta_1^3\theta_2^3}d\theta$ on $\theta < \theta_2 \le 1, \frac{\theta}{\theta_2} < \theta_1 \le 1$) evaluates the inner $(\theta_1, \theta_2)$-integral in closed form:
$$\frac{1}{4}\int_\theta^1 \theta_2^{-(\beta+6)/2}\left(\int_{\theta/\theta_2}^1 \theta_1^{-(\alpha+4)/2}\,d\theta_1\right)d\theta_2 = W_{\alpha,\beta}(\theta) := \frac{1}{d}\left(\frac{\theta^{-(\beta+4)/2} - 1}{\beta + 4} - \frac{\theta^{-(\alpha+2)/2} - 1}{\alpha + 2}\right) > 0 \qquad (d := \beta + 2 - \alpha > 0),$$
giving the exact representation
$$R_2(s, q) = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{54}\,s^3\sum_{k \in \{2, 4\}}\sum_{l \in \{0, 2, 4, 6, 8\}}\int_0^1 W_{k/3, l/3}(\theta)\,\theta^2\,\mathbb{E}_F\!\left[\left(B_{s,\theta}^{-5/2}h_4\!\left(\frac{s\theta F^2}{B_{s,\theta}}\right) - 3 A_s^{-5/2}\right)U_{\mathbb{R}}\,\Pi_l(U_{\mathbb{R}} U_k)\right]d\theta. \tag{6}$$
**Endpoint regularity and Fubini integrability on $(0, 1]$:** At the upper endpoint $\theta = 1$, $B_{s,1} = 1 > 0$ uniformly in $s \ge 0$ and $W_{k/3, l/3}(1) = 0$, so every integrand is continuous up to $\theta = 1$. As $\theta \downarrow 0$, $\theta^2 W_{k/3, l/3}(\theta) = O(\theta^{-l/6})$: for $l \in \{2, 4\}$ this is $O(\theta^{-2/3})$ with bounded expectation, hence integrable; for $l \in \{0, 6, 8\}$, Wiener-chaos orthogonality $U_{\mathbb{R}} \in \mathcal{H}_2 \oplus \mathcal{H}_4 \perp \mathcal{H}_l$ kills the deterministic scalar $3 B_{s,\theta}^{-5/2} - 3 A_s^{-5/2}$, leaving $B_{s,\theta}^{-5/2}(h_4(\frac{s\theta F^2}{B_{s,\theta}}) - 3)$ of size $O(\theta^{1/2}|F|)$ by Lemma 3.1(2) below and producing an integrable singularity **$O(\theta^{1/2 - l/6})$** ($O(\theta^{-1/2})$ at $l = 6$, $O(\theta^{-5/6})$ at $l = 8$).

### Lemma 3.1 — Asymmetric Hermite range, off-centre anchor, and chaos-wise $\|U_{\mathbb{R}}\|_4$ bound
