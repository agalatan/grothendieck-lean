\paragraph{Exact reductions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{One visible variable.} $\Lambda(G)=\E_T[\E(|\ell(T)+R_T|+|\ell(T)-R_T|)-\E|R_T|^2/\varepsilon]$, with $R_T=q+(T-1)r_A+\tfrac T{\sqrt2}r_B+\sqrt T(Y+W)$ and the
price exact.
\item \emph{The visible law is a Gaussian scale mixture.}
\item \emph{K for all $\varepsilon$} $\iff$ Gaussian domination on every variance sphere.
\item \emph{The remote part.} Exact remote K $\iff$ $\E|q|\le\frac{\sqrt\pi}2\|q\|_2$ on $\Hc21$ (PVD$_0$), which is implied by BNC (open).
\item \emph{Gaussian layer plus gap.} $\Lambda\le S-\alpha^2/10-\beta^2/4-\gamma^2/25+D$, with $D$ the non-Gaussian gap.
\end{enumerate}
\paragraph{Proved.}
\begin{itemize}
\item remote cubics ($\Lambda<S+10^{-5}$);
\item the diagonal (penalties $0.571$, $0.265$) and the even sectors;
\item the coherent one-driver Wick $A$ family, with \emph{exact} K;
\item the whole Gaussian covariance class, \emph{exact} K with penalties;
\item shared $A/B$ for unequal Wick sums with small coefficients;
\item $B$ only up to normalised fourth moment $3.757$;
\item independent blocks (weak K, $O(1/N)$);
\item remote cubics whose Hermite monomials all involve one coordinate: \emph{exact} K;
\item shared $A/B$ on arbitrary Gaussian-factor drivers $C^{\!\top}X+gU$: $\Lambda\le S+5\cdot10^{-5}-(\|\alpha\|^2+\|\beta\|^2)/200$, exact once the $A/B$ variance is $\ge0.01$.
\end{itemize}
\paragraph{False.} (T); equal-variance peeling; exact K-a from the scalar inputs; fibrewise domination; every single-scale strengthening (heat kernel, characteristic function, Laplace);
gluing by buffer doubling.
\paragraph{Open.} (1) The crux for $1.402$: $D\le$ penalties $+\eta_{AB}$ ($\eta_{AB}\approx9.06\cdot10^{-5}$) for cubics \emph{nonlinear in a driver shared with $A/B$}, e.g.\ $cH(X)$ with
$A$ and $B$ on $X$, then several drivers, then general coupled cubics. (2) Diagonal interactions and the row envelope, then the certificate at $\varepsilon\approx0.7564$. (3) PVD$_0$/BNC
(exactness only).

\section{The statement and what it buys}
Peak row $\varepsilon=0.7564$, $(\ell,\beta)=(0.8456,0.1432)$, i.e.\ $L=Z\,a(|Z|^2)$, $a(u)=\ell+\beta(u-2)=0.5592+0.1432u$. For $G\in\Hc21(\C^n)$,
\[
\Lambda(G)=\E|L+G|+\E|L-G|-\|G\|_2^2/\varepsilon,\qquad S=\sup_{\sigma\ge0}\{2\E|L+\sigma W|-\sigma^2/\varepsilon\}=1.37395353\ (\sigma^*=0.2009).
\]
\begin{conjecture}[K] $\Lambda(G)\le S$ for all $n$ and all $G\in\Hc21(\C^n)$; weak form $K_\eta$: $\Lambda(G)\le S+\eta$.\end{conjecture}
Exact gate-free row \st{P}: $\sup\Re\langle f,Tg\rangle\le t^2-2\ell t+2\beta^2/\varepsilon+\sup_G\Lambda(G)$. Under $K_\eta$ the certificate is rows $(\ell,\beta)$, the explicit potential
$\Phi_0$, the existing moment/Stein layer and the envelope: realistic cap $0.713160+\eta$ at $\varepsilon=0.7564$, so $K_\eta$ with $\eta\le1.0\cdot10^{-4}$ gives $K_G^{\C}>1.402$; $K$ itself
gives $1.4022$ (ceiling of the cubic witness $1.402258$). Decomposition of $G$ by its dependence on $Z$ (coefficients are chaos elements of the remote coordinates $w$):
\[
G=\underbrace{q_0}_{\Hc21(w)}+\underbrace{r_A}_{\Hc10}(T-1)+\underbrace{r_B}_{\Hc01}\tfrac{Z^2}{\sqrt2}+\underbrace{r_Y}_{\Hc11}Z+\underbrace{r_W}_{\Hc20}\bar Z+\nu\,\tfrac{Z(T-2)}{\sqrt2},\qquad T=|Z|^2 .
\]

\section{A proposal to finish, after the global $A$ theorem}\label{sec:proposal}
GPT's certificate works because, conditionally on the coefficient state, the residual is \emph{proper Gaussian} and the content is \emph{bilinear} ($p=\Re\langle S,\alpha\rangle$, centered), so a
supporting multiplier cancels it on averaging and a three-parameter dual can be covered by boxes. The uncovered case is the cubic self-interaction $q_0\ni cH(X)$ with $r_A=\alpha X$ on
the same driver. Two exact observations suggest how to close it.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The junta is the opposite conditional extreme, and it is still finite-dimensional.} Conditionally on $\rho=|X|$ (and on the remote coefficient state), $cH(X)+\alpha(T-1)X=
e^{i\theta}\rho\,[c(\rho^2-2)/\sqrt2+\alpha(T-1)]$: a \emph{ring} (deterministic modulus, uniform phase), not a Gaussian. With a proper-Gaussian buffer of variance $s$ the conditional
payoff is $2\,E_{T,\rho}\,\mathcal F_s\bigl(\sqrt Ta(T),\ \rho|c(\rho^2-2)/\sqrt2+\alpha(T-1)|\bigr)$, $\mathcal F_s(x,m)=\E_{\theta,G}|x+me^{i\theta}+\sqrt sG|$, i.e.\ one angular average of the
Rice function already enclosed in his certificate. The one-coordinate junta with shared $A$ (and $B$: $\beta\bar X Z^2/\sqrt2$ adds a second ring term with the opposite $Z$-frequency)
plus buffer is therefore a \emph{four-to-six-parameter} dual inequality $(c,s,|\alpha|,\arg\alpha,|\beta|,\arg\beta)$ over a compact set (the same Cauchy--Schwarz tail argument applies),
certifiable by exactly his box machinery. The right target is $\Lambda\le S-(1-\theta)d_q(c,s)$ with $d_q$ the junta-only deficit and $\theta\approx1/4$ (the sectors do recover up to $16\%$ of $d_q$, so one cannot subtract per-sector deficits as well; GPT, correcting a first formulation), or simply $\Lambda\le S+10^{-5}$; the boxes will be coarse. GPT also reports a $B$ case: when the shared coefficient has uniform phase given its magnitude, phase averaging turns the improper conditional Gaussian into a variance mixture covered by the $A$ theorem, allowing nonzero pseudocovariance. This would close
``cubic self-interaction'' for a single influential coordinate, and the Gaussian-factor theorem plus the profile-addition mechanism then covers everything riding on other, independent
drivers.
\item \textbf{The content is a pairing with the residual's own derivative, and only the second-chaos parts matter.} For $r_A=\sum p_j\bar w_j$-direction $p$ (unit vector),
Gaussian integration by parts gives the exact identity
\[
\mathrm{content}_A(p)=\langle p,\Pi_{1,0}F_A(q_0)\rangle=\E\bigl[F_{A,u}(q_0)\,D_pq_0\bigr]+\E\bigl[F_{A,\bar u}(q_0)\,\overline{\bar D_pq_0}\bigr],\qquad D_p=\sum_j\bar p_j\partial_j,
\]
and since $D_pq_0\in\Hc11(w)$, $\bar D_pq_0\in\Hc20(w)$ are pure second chaos, only $\Pi_{1,1}F_{A,u}(q_0)$ and $\Pi_{0,2}F_{A,\bar u}(q_0)$ enter. Hence content$\le\|\Pi_{1,1}F_{A,u}(q_0)\|\,\|D_pq_0\|+
\|\Pi_{0,2}F_{A,\bar u}(q_0)\|\,\|\bar D_pq_0\|$ with $\|D_pq_0\|^2\le\lambda_{\max}(\Gamma)\le0.866\sigma^2x$ (Theorem~5) and the second-chaos parts of the derivative profiles of size
$O(\sigma^2x)$ (they are $\approx F''$-weighted $U_2$): this is the third-order structure made explicit, and it shows what a sharp bound must control: the second-chaos content of
$g(|q_0|^2)$ for smooth $g$ in terms of $U_2,U_4$, i.e.\ in terms of the influence matrix, jointly with the influence in the direction $p$. For the polynomial model
$F=\gamma u+\delta u|u|^2$ this is L2 (now $\le2.2029\kappa^{3/2}$, kurtosis-dependent $\le\frac{32}{27}\kappa^{3/2}$ for $\kappa\ge1$); the bounded profile saturates beyond $|q_0|\approx0.5$.
\item \textbf{What is then left} is the reduction of a general cubic to ``one (or few) influential coordinates + Gaussian factors'' with an error paid by the deficit, i.e.\ a
quantitative invariance principle with deficit-weighted error; the influence threshold needed for a $10^{-5}$ error is far too small for brute force ($\sim1/\tau$ junta coordinates), so
this step needs the second observation (content controlled by the influence matrix, which also controls the deficit), not a cover.
\end{enumerate}
