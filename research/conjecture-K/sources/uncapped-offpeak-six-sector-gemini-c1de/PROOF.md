## 1. Main Theorems & Summary of Results

In `KGAS` (`STATUS.md` 7.5.13), global six-sector pointwise closure $F_{\mathrm{joint}}(c^*) \le 0$ on $\mathbb{C}^6$ at the Peak row used the A-tilt $s_A = 3/5$ and the quartic shell budget $B_P(w, A, B) = (p - 463/5000)S_r - (67/100)|R|^2 - (2/25)|U|^2 + (\frac{1}{2}w^2 + S_r)S_r$ of `7.4.10`. When averaged over a random response $G$, the quartic correction $+(\frac{1}{2}|q|^2 + S_r)S_r$ spends $\frac{1}{2}\mathbb{E}[|q|^2 S_r] + \mathbb{E}[S_r^2] \le 2 u V + 2 V^2$, restricting the six-sector retained bound $\widehat{\Lambda}(G)$ to the energy-capped domain $u \le 0.0463, u + V \le 0.080051$, and restricting fixed-scale or free-homothety Peak-ray consumers (`KAST`, `KHOM`) to $t \ge 0.8395$.

Here we eliminate the quartic correction $(\frac{1}{2}|q|^2 + S_r)S_r$ **identically** by moving to the symmetric A-tilt $s_A = 1/2$ and certifying the **purely quadratic** (zero-quartic) global six-sector closure $F_{\mathrm{joint}}^{(0, i)}(c^*) \le 0$ on all of $\mathbb{C}^6$ across three rows: the **Peak row** (`peak`, $\varepsilon = 1891/2500$) and the first two off-peak lower-central rows **`row7`** ($t \in [831/1000, 21/25] = [0.831, 0.840]$) and **`row6`** ($t \in [411/500, 831/1000] = [0.822, 0.831]$) of `8.13(ii)` ($\varepsilon = 94/125, p = 125/94$). We also prove four energy-cap-free all-$(V, W)$ Peak corollaries and certify lower-central obstructions on Rows 1–4 of `8.13(ii)` (genuine zero-core failure of $F_{\mathrm{joint}}^{(0, i)}(c^*) \le 0$ on Rows 1–2, $t \in [1503/2000, 157/200] = [0.7515, 0.785]$, and failure of the 2-sign secant-majorant certificate on Rows 3–4, $t \in [157/200, 203/250] = [0.785, 0.812]$).

### Theorem 1 (Energy-Cap-Free Global Pointwise Six-Sector Closure on $\mathbb{C}^6$)
For each $i \in \{\mathrm{peak}, \mathrm{row7}, \mathrm{row6}\}$ and **every** $(q, A, B, Q, P, \nu) \in \mathbb{C}^6$:
$$F_{\mathrm{joint}}^{(0, i)}(c^*)(q, A, B, Q, P, \nu) := \mathbb{E}_{z_1}\!\Bigl[|L_i + q + K_i + O'| + |L_i + q + K_i - O'| - 2|L_i + q| - 2\Re\bigl(K_i\overline{\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)}\bigr)\Bigr] + |c^*|^2 - B_P^{(0, i)}(A, B) - p_i\bigl(|Q|^2 + |P|^2 + |\nu|^2\bigr) - C_{\mathrm{inv}}^{(i)}(Q, \nu) \;\le\; 0,$$

## 2. Exact Retained Trial Cancellation, Alignment, & Peak Corollaries

### 2.1 Exact Retained Trial Cancellation at $s_A = 1/2$
In $\widehat{\Lambda}_i(G) = \min_{\xi \in H_{1,0}} \{\|\xi\|_2^2 + \mathbb{E}|L_i + G - \xi| + \mathbb{E}|L_i - G + \xi|\} - p_i\|G\|_2^2$, choose $\xi = -\frac{1}{2}(r_A + \rho_i r_B) + c^* z_1$ with $c^* = \alpha_{\nu, i}\nu$. Since $r_A + \rho_i r_B = R \in H_{1,0}^{\mathrm{rem}}$ is orthogonal to $z_1$, $\|\xi\|_2^2 = \frac{1}{4}\|R\|_2^2 + |c^*|^2$. Moreover,
$$(T - 1)r_A + \frac{z_1^2}{\sqrt{2}}\overline{r_B} - \left(-\frac{1}{2}R\right) = \left(T - \frac{1}{2}\right)r_A + \frac{\rho_i}{2}r_B + \frac{z_1^2}{\sqrt{2}}\overline{r_B} = K_i(z_1; r_A, r_B).$$
Using $\mathbb{E}C_{\mathrm{inv}}^{(i)}(Q, \nu) = 2\gamma_i\Re(\mathbb{E}[Q]\bar{\nu}) = 0$ (`\mathbb{E}Q = 0` for $Q \in H_{1,1}^{\mathrm{rem}}$ and deterministic $\nu \in \mathbb{C}$), adding and subtracting $2\mathbb{E}|L_i + q| + 2\Re\mathbb{E}[K_i\overline{\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)}]$ gives
$$\widehat{\Lambda}_i(G) \;\le\; \Lambda_i(q) + 2\Re\mathbb{E}\bigl[K_i\overline{\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)}\bigr] + \mathbb{E}\!\left[F_{\mathrm{joint}}^{(0, i)}(c^*)(q, r_A, r_B, Q, P, \nu)\right] + \mathbb{E}\!\left[\frac{1}{4}|R|^2 + B_P^{(0, i)}(r_A, r_B) - p_i S_r\right].$$
By definition of $B_P^{(0, i)}$, the bracketed quadratic shell term simplifies **identically** to
$$\frac{1}{4}|R|^2 + B_P^{(0, i)}(r_A, r_B) - p_i S_r = -c_{\mathrm{iso}, i}\bigl(|R|^2 + |U|^2\bigr) = -c_{\mathrm{iso}, i}(1 + \rho_i^2)\,S_r,$$
with **zero** $|q|^2 S_r$ or $S_r^2$ terms!

### 2.2 First-Order Linear Coupling & All-Row Centered Alignment (`7.1.6`)
By `STATUS.md` §0 and `7.1.6`, $\mathbb{E}_{z_1}[\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)] = q\,j^{(i)}(|q|^2)$, $\mathbb{E}_{z_1}[(T - 1)\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)] = q\,g^{(i)}(|q|^2) = -q\,k_A^{(i)}(|q|^2)$, and $\mathbb{E}_{z_1}[\frac{z_1^2}{\sqrt{2}}\overline{\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)}] = -q\,k_B^{(i)}(|q|^2)$. Writing $\mu_q := \Pi_1[q\,j^{(i)}(|q|^2)]$, $c_A^{(i)}(q) = \Pi_1[-q\,k_A^{(i)}(|q|^2)]$, $c_B^{(i)}(q) = \Pi_1[-q\,k_B^{(i)}(|q|^2)]$, $c_{1/2}^{(i)}(q) = c_A^{(i)}(q) + \frac{1}{2}\mu_q$, and $e^{(i)}(q) := c_B^{(i)}(q) - \rho_i c_A^{(i)}(q) = \Pi_1[q(\rho_i k_A^{(i)}(|q|^2) - k_B^{(i)}(|q|^2))]$, we have $\frac{\rho_i}{2}\mu_q + c_B^{(i)}(q) = \rho_i c_{1/2}^{(i)}(q) + e^{(i)}(q)$, hence
$$\mathbb{E}\!\left[2\Re\Bigl(\bigl((T - \tfrac{1}{2})r_A + \tfrac{\rho_i}{2}r_B\bigr)\overline{\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)} + \frac{z_1^2}{\sqrt{2}}\overline{r_B}\overline{\operatorname{ph}(L_i + q)}\Bigr)\right] = 2\Re\left\langle R, c_{1/2}^{(i)}(q)\right\rangle + 2\Re\left\langle r_B, e^{(i)}(q)\right\rangle,$$
whose Cauchy–Schwarz upper bound is $2(\|R\|_2\|c_{1/2}^{(i)}(q)\|_2 + \|r_B\|_2\|e^{(i)}(q)\|_2) \le 2(\sqrt{1 + \rho_i^2}\|c_{1/2}^{(i)}(q)\|_2 + \|e^{(i)}(q)\|_2)\sqrt{V}$ since $\|R\|_2 \le \sqrt{1 + \rho_i^2}\sqrt{V}$ and $\|r_B\|_2 \le \sqrt{V}$.

Furthermore, by `STATUS.md` `7.1.6` (**P**, A15T `ALIGNMENT.md`), for **every** $a_i, b_i > 0$, $k_A^{(i)}(v)$ and $k_B^{(i)}(v)$ share a common nonnegative spectral mixture with ratio $r(t) \in [k_B^{(i)}(0)/k_A^{(i)}(0),\, 1/\sqrt{2}]$. In `verify.py` (`check_target_foundations`), 220-bit interval enclosures of $J_0(a_i, b_i)$ certify $k_B^{(i)}(0)/k_A^{(i)}(0) > \rho_i$ on all three rows (`0.50534060 > 0.5053`, `0.50675337 > 0.5067`, `0.50928827 > 0.5092`). Hence $0 \le k_B^{(i)}(v) - \rho_i k_A^{(i)}(v) \le (1/\sqrt{2} - \rho_i)k_A^{(i)}(v) \le (1/\sqrt{2} - \rho_i)k_A^{(i)}(0)$ for all $v \ge 0$, so centering at the midpoint gives $\|e^{(i)}(q)\|_2 \le \frac{1}{2}(1/\sqrt{2} - \rho_i)k_A^{(i)}(0)\sqrt{u} \le 0.085\sqrt{u}$ on all three rows, with the last comparison strict when $u>0$ (sharpened at `peak` to $\frac{7}{2000}\sqrt{u}$ by the 79-cell cover of `7.1.6`).
