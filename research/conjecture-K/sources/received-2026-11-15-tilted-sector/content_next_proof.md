# A Gaussian-probe bound on tilted content

Agent ID: codex-6e2b. 15 November 2026.

**P+C:** at the exact peak row and tilt `s=3/5`, every finite-dimensional
remote cubic `q` with `E|q|² <= 463/10000` satisfies

\[
\boxed{\quad\|\Pi_1[q h_{3/5}(|q|^2)]\|_2\le\frac1{50}.\quad}  \tag{1}
\]

The argument in fact needs only `Pi_1 q=0` and the energy bound, on a
standard complex Gaussian probability space. It uses the Gaussian marginal
of a unit linear probe and does not require independence between that probe
and `q`. It is intended for amplitude localization. It does not itself
establish a content-to-deficit inequality or the full tilted-sector lemma.

The checker is `content_next_bound.py`. Its default invocation replays both
earlier scalar profile certificates, regenerates all profile enclosures,
and checks the dual envelope:

```
python3 -I -B content_next_bound.py
```

It requires python-flint. The generated table is
`content_next_profile_table.json`; the calculation transcript is
`content_next_results.txt`. The table is regenerated by default and is not
treated as a trusted cache. Development flag `--skip-dependency-replay`
explicitly labels its run conditional on the already proved scalar
dependencies; it is not the standalone verification command.

The computed upper bound is approximately
`0.019803664185096631`, strictly below `1/50`. Every acceptance comparison
uses Arb interval arithmetic. Floating point only prioritizes quadrature
subdivision. The checker has no numerical optimization in its trusted path.

## 1. Exact dual lemma — P

Write `h=h_(3/5)`. Let `ell` be a unit vector of the first holomorphic
Gaussian chaos. Thus `E|ell|²=1` and `|ell|²` has the exponential law of
mean one. For every real `k` and positive `lambda`, orthogonality gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
|\mathbb E\overline\ell\,q h(|q|^2)|
 &=|\mathbb E\overline\ell\,q(h(|q|^2)-k)|\\
 &\le \lambda u+
 \mathbb E\sup_{\rho\ge0}
  \left\{|\ell|\rho|h(\rho^2)-k|-\lambda\rho^2\right\}.
\end{aligned}
\]

No conditional independence was used. The bound is the same for every
unit `ell`, so taking the supremum yields

\[
C(q)\le\lambda u+\int_0^\infty e^{-T}\Omega(T)\,dT,\qquad
\Omega(T)=\sup_{\rho\ge0}
 \{\sqrt T\rho|h(\rho^2)-k|-\lambda\rho^2\}.                  \tag{2}
\]

The checker fixes the rational constants

\[
k=\frac{49}{500},\quad \lambda=\frac{13}{50},\quad
u_{max}=\frac{463}{10000}.
\]

It verifies the right side of (2) at `u_max`. Positivity of `lambda` then
covers every smaller energy, including zero.

## 3. Compact and infinite domains — P

For `0<=T<=16` and `rho>=3`,

\[
\sqrt T\rho|h(\rho^2)-k|-\lambda\rho^2
\le\rho(4H-3\lambda)<0.
\]

Since `rho=0` has value zero, the supremum on this `T` range is attained
inside `[0,3]`. For `T>16`, completing a scalar square gives

\[
\Omega(T)\le\frac{H^2}{4\lambda}T,
\quad
\int_{16}^\infty e^{-T}\Omega(T)dT
\le\frac{H^2}{4\lambda}\,17e^{-16}.                         \tag{4}
\]

The last expression is enclosed below `4.419441 * 10^-8`.

On `[0,3]`, take 300 consecutive exact rational cells of width `1/100`.
At every midpoint, `field_point` applies the previously audited
signed-density quadrature and its analytic tail, until the entire ball
for `h` has radius less than `1/10000`. Inflating by
`1/(200 sqrt(3))` encloses `h` throughout that cell. The checker then takes
an upper bound `H_j` for `|h-k|`, capped by the independent global bound
(3).

For a cell `[a_j,b_j]`, the required upper bound is therefore

\[
\max_{\rho\in[a_j,b_j]}
 \{\sqrt T H_j\rho-\lambda\rho^2\}.                         \tag{5}
\]

The function is concave quadratic. Certified derivative signs select an
endpoint when the vertex is outside the interval. Otherwise the
unrestricted quadratic maximum `T H_j²/(4 lambda)` is a safe upper bound.
If an interval comparison is undecidable, that unrestricted bound is also
the fallback. The maximum of these bounds and zero is denoted `U(T)`.

All cell endpoints are exact fractions. The wrapper verifies the first
endpoint is zero, the last is three, and successive endpoints match.

## 4. Exponential integration — P+C

Each maximized cell function in (5) is nondecreasing in `T`; its coefficient
of `sqrt(T)` is nonnegative. Thus their maximum is nondecreasing.
Partition `[0,16]` at exact fractions `j/200`, `0<=j<=3200`. A rigorous
upper sum is

\[
\int_0^{16}e^{-T}\Omega(T)dT
\le\sum_{j=1}^{3200}U(j/200)
       \big(e^{-(j-1)/200}-e^{-j/200}\big).                   \tag{6}
\]

Both the weights and the quadratic maxima are evaluated with outward
rounding. The verified quantities are approximately

\[
\lambda u_{max}=0.012038,
\qquad \text{compact sum}=0.007765619990694001,
\qquad \text{tail}=0.00000004419440262953212.
\]

Their sum is below `0.019803664185097`, proving (1).

The intermediate profile table serializes enclosing balls, not bare
floating-point centers. Reparsing a table bound gives a ball containing
the original exact Arb upper endpoint. The complete checker nevertheless
regenerates this table rather than relying on its saved copy.
