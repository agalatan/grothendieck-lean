# Conditional raw A-content near a core of specified real parity

Agent-ID: codex-a7c4. This extends the [complex-unitary neighbourhood, #673](https://github.com/agalatan/grothendieck-lean/pull/673) to a reflection of the underlying standardized **real** Gaussian space. Its application to trilinear projections is in [the coefficient-only theorem](PROOF.md). Write $P=\Re p$, $H=\Re h$ for $p,h\in H_{2,1}$. Assume $P$ and $H$ have opposite signs under a real orthogonal involution. Equivalently this proof works with a compact real orthogonal symmetry, a sign character $\chi$, and $H$ with zero $\chi$-projection. No action on $\Im p,\Im h$ is required. Joint circularity under *global* phase implies $\|p+h\|^2=2\mathbb E(P+H)^2=\|p\|^2+e$, with $e=\|h\|^2=2\mathbb EH^2$ by real character orthogonality.

The angular averaging in the [main proof](PROOF.md) gives $C(q)=t\|\Pi_{\mathbb R}m_A(\Re q)\|_2$ with $t=\pi/(2\sqrt2)$ and $m_A(x)=\mathbb E[(T-1)\operatorname{sign}(\Re L+x)]$. This is the [positive mixture, 6.3.43 §3.2](../raw-content-2026-11-17-gemini-5331/PROOF.md) of negative atoms $-x e^{-\lambda x^2}$ with measure $\mu_A(d\lambda)/(\sqrt\pi\sqrt\lambda)$. Its mass is $4M_0/\pi$ by [6.3.9b, equation (8)](../third-audit-2026-11-15-codex-a7c4/CONTENT_KERNELS.md) at zero, and its square-root moment is $1/(\sqrt\pi a^2)$ because $\int d\mu_A=f_A(0)=1/a^2$.

Subtracting a scalar linear map from $m_A$ leaves its first chaos after evaluating it on any real pure cubic. The centered derivative bound for $m_A$ is $(4/\pi)L$ for the same $L=(1+2e^{-3/2})M_0/2$ as in #673. The squared change of projection is consequently bounded by $t^2(4/\pi)^2L^2 (e/2)=L^2e$. Centering the radial multiplier separately gives $C(p)\le M_0\|p\|_2/2$.

Let $c=\Pi_{\mathbb R} m_A(P)$, of the same parity as $P$. The linear term in $\mathbb E[c\,m_A'(P)H]$ vanishes. If $c\ne0$, let $N=c/\|c\|_2$. In the real Hermite expansion of $H$ along $N$, the order-three term is excluded because $\operatorname{He}_3(N)$ has the same parity as $N$. Coefficients at orders $0,1,2$ belong to orthogonal chaoses of the other real coordinates. Their weighted squared norms $\mathbb E|N|\operatorname{He}_j(N)^2/j!$ are respectively $\sqrt{2/\pi}$, $2\sqrt{2/\pi}$ and $(5/2)\sqrt{2/\pi}$. The last follows by expanding $(N^2-1)^2$ and using $(\mathbb E|N|,\mathbb E|N|^3,\mathbb E|N|^5)=\sqrt{2/\pi}\,(1,2,8)$. Consequently $\mathbb E|N|H^2\le k_R e/2$, $k_R=(5/2)\sqrt{2/\pi}$.

The scalar atom Hessian bound $2\sqrt\lambda$ from the same 6.3.9b §5 gives $H_R=2/(\sqrt\pi a^2)$ for $m_A$. Thus the Taylor remainder in the cross term costs at most $t^2 H_R\|c\|_2 k_R e/2=(5/(4a^2))C(p)e$, with exactly the same coefficient as for the complex-unitary result. This is zero when $c=0$.

The payoff equals $\mathbb E f(P)$, where $f(x)=\pi\mathbb E|\Re L+x|$ is even, and $\mathbb E[f'(P)H]=0$. By the visible variance mixture, $f''(x)\le 2\pi f_{\Re L}(0)=2\sqrt\pi\,\mathbb E V^{-1/2}=4\phi'(0)$, since $\phi'(0)=\tfrac12\mathbb E|L|^{-1}=(\sqrt\pi/2)\mathbb E V^{-1/2}$. The quadratic error is therefore at most $2d\mathbb EH^2=d e$. The benchmark derivative lower bound is unchanged.

The adaptive criterion and all nine rational budgets of #673 follow verbatim. In particular a core of the [real-carrier class](https://github.com/agalatan/grothendieck-lean/pull/675) at $47/200$ admits arbitrary opposite-parity real parts of bidegree-(2,1) cubics at $e\le D(p)/167$ on peak and flank or $D(p)/160$ on upper, when the core and total energies are in the required bands. Another possible symmetry is coordinatewise complex conjugation: a cubic with real Wick coefficients has even real part, and $i$ times any cubic with real Wick coefficients has odd real part. This gives the same perturbation criterion whenever the first cubic meets a deficit/content bound. The argument uses no complex linearity of the involution.

The same statement holds for scalar real third-chaos pieces $P+H=\Re q$ whether or not $P,H$ lift separately. In this case define $C_0=t\|\Pi_{\mathbb R}m_A(P)\|_2$, $D_0=B(2\mathbb EP^2)-\mathbb E f(P)$ with $f(x)=\pi\mathbb E|\Re L+x|$, $e=2\mathbb EH^2$. The proof uses only these scalar variables and their real sign projections. Compact groups with a sign character give the two cancellations by Haar averaging. For the denominators in each core regime, run the [exact #673 replay](../../code/mixed-driver-sign-perturbation-codex-a7c4/verify.py); for its premises, see the [original proof](../mixed-driver-sign-perturbation-codex-a7c4/PROOF.md).
