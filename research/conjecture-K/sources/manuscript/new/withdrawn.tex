\section{Withdrawn and corrected claims (index)}\label{sec:ms-withdrawn}
Claims that were made in our files and later withdrawn or corrected. The chapter where the correction is recorded is
given; keep the corrected form when quoting.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{``All Krivine schemes stop at $1/\gd$; going below needs a non-Krivine scheme.''} Withdrawn (22 October, Step 18):
  the defect identity concerns one chosen inverse series; the negativity of its coefficients is checked only to a finite
  order; no barrier at $1/\gd$ for general mixed schemes is proved. What remains is the screening statement of
  \S\ref{sec:ms-alternating} \st{F}.
\item \textbf{``The upper bound has converged to the truth.''} Overclaim withdrawn (\autoref{ch:ms-gap}).
\item \textbf{Domination statement $D_\eta$},
  \[\|P_{H_{10}+H_{21}}F(p)\|^2\le|\langle F(p),p\rangle|^2/\|p\|^2+\eta\quad\text{(equality at Gaussian $p$)}:\]
  \textbf{false} for $\eta=0$, exact counterexample $p=H(w_1)$, $F=p|p|^2$ (128 vs 1936)
  (\S\ref{sec:ms-gate-analysis}). Step 21's $\Delta_{21}$ omitted a law-linear $q$-term; corrected in Step 22.
\item \textbf{Sixth-moment hypothesis \code{h6}} for general real \code{Chaos3} pairs: false
  ($\mathrm{He}_3(x_0)/\sqrt{12},\mathrm{He}_3(x_1)/\sqrt{12}$ gives $\E U^3=13506>12622.55$); it needs circularity, now
  proved in Lean for circular cubics (\autoref{ch:certified}).
\item \textbf{Step 16 numbers} (degree-5 shared-driver model norm $0.712464$) were off by $3.5\cdot10^{-5}$ (plain
  Gauss--Hermite on a phase singularity); corrected value $0.7124294$ with the polar-grid engine.
\item \textbf{Hypothesis (T)} of the weighted-content programme: false (\autoref{ch:weighted}, \autoref{ch:kjoint}).
\item \textbf{Frozen-table ceiling $\approx1.4017236$} applies to the cert-0745b tables only; not recomputed for later tables
  (\autoref{ch:common-law-refinement}).
\item \textbf{Grid artefacts} in the two-coordinate $W$ search (tensor-grid maxima up to $+1.8\cdot10^{-4}$) were rotations
  inflated by grid bias; resolved in Step~20 (\S\ref{sec:ms-alternating}).
\end{enumerate}
