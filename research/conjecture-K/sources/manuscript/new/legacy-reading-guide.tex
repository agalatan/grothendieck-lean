\paragraph{Status labels.} Every claim carries one of the following labels, which must be preserved when the claim is
quoted elsewhere.
\begin{description}[style=nextline]
\item[\st{L}] proved in Lean 4 with Mathlib in the repository \pathref{\~{}/workspaces/grothe} (GitHub \code{agalatan/grothendieck-lean}, private), with the axioms listed in \pathref{scratch/Axioms.lean}.
\item[\st{P}] pen-and-paper proof exists (in our notes, in the source archives, or in the literature), not formalized.
\item[\st{C}] computer-assisted external certificate (Arb ball arithmetic or exact rational arithmetic), not replayed in Lean.
\item[\st{C$_{\rm float}$}] reported padded floating-point validation; this is distinct from an outward-rounded certificate and requires its own roundoff justification.
\item[\st{F}] float numerics only (grids and solvers stated where known). Never a bound.
\item[\st{?}] claimed somewhere in our files or by a collaborator, not verified by anyone here.
\end{description}
