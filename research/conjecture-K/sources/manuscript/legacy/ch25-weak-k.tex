\section{Original-response geometry and weaker sufficient targets for the lower bound}\label{ch:weakk}
\chapsummary{Status: Current (22 October; \st{P,C}; no new endpoint).
The exact target is the norm objective on the projections of bounded responses,
not Gaussian domination of every abstract cubic. This chapter keeps the two
negative projection squares, derives joint feasibility constraints, and proves
dimension-free regional caps with all visible sectors and phases. The scalar
moment-free region is $e+(191/500)t^2\le9/40$; together with the anisotropic
certificate it covers $0\le t\le0.851$, $e\le0.0148$. A further moment-compression cell, \autoref{thm:response-moment-slab},
certifies a cap below $0.7124$ on a shared-projection box across $0.84\le t\le0.851$;
its energy budget is for the full $F$. The weighted full-energy theorem,
\autoref{thm:weighted-full-energy-response}, additionally gives cap
$0.71321135$ for $0\le t\le0.851$, $\mathcal V_*\le0.0325$, and
cap $0.71325087$ at the peak when $\mathcal V_*\le0.03744$, without
any projection-box restriction. A projected-price refinement extends the
cutoffs to $0.03255$ and $0.03748$, respectively, below $0.713260$.
The exact joint-response defect has a rational lower formula retaining
at least the sharp fraction $81/250$ of its full value; the hierarchy
in \S\ref{sec:joint-dual-descent} improves this to more than $99.99\%$
after 32 rational steps and gives an explicit descent for the joint dual.
Whole tensor-subspace concentration is now covered as well:
\autoref{thm:whole-slice-contact} handles $90\%$ of the full cubic energy
in one holomorphic slice, or $98.5\%$ in an arbitrary-rank quadratic
block with one orthogonal antiholomorphic factor, throughout the central
interval. The remainder may be fully coupled.
\autoref{thm:two-slice-contact} extends this to $97.5\%$ of the full
energy in the sum of two orthogonal holomorphic slices, with cap
$0.7132$. A common three-profile reduction covers any number of such
slices analytically, while explaining its loss of phase information
in the diffuse limit. The sharp phase-preserving comparison in
\autoref{thm:sharp-decoupled-support} repairs that loss for orthogonal
holomorphic/antiholomorphic input blocks. Its response certificates
cover up to 24 holomorphic inputs, with arbitrary antiholomorphic rank
and a coupled remainder, below $0.71325$.
The spherical reduction in \autoref{thm:spherical-core-contact}
now covers concentration in the entire cubic space on two, three,
four, six or eight remote coordinates, allowing arbitrary input overlap.
It closes the previously uncovered three-cycle core and has an invariant
entrance condition using the tail of the combined influence spectrum.
The rank-free theorem \autoref{thm:conditional-response-contact}
now covers $99.8\%$ of the full energy in a remote exactly-one-leg
linear--quadratic module, with arbitrary ranks, both Gaussian covariance
channels, and a fully dependent remainder. Six scalar profiles and two
analytic outer intervals give cap $0.71325$ over the entire $t$ range.
Radial unimodality and an exact projection-price transfer supply its
short analytic proof. The remaining complement and its full
same-$\varepsilon$ cover are open. The earlier rotation
and smoothing arguments remain proof tools with their own hypotheses.}

\paragraph{Scope and accounting.}
Here $F=\varepsilon Qk$ is an actual projection, and its remote variance
$e=\|\E[F\mid X]\|^2$ is unnormalised. Raw-K theorems for arbitrary cubics
are separate sufficient inputs. A projection square used inside a weighted
Gram bound cannot also be subtracted as an independent penalty; separate
valid upper bounds may always be combined by taking their minimum.
No claim here changes the certified endpoint in \autoref{ch:certified}.
