\subsection{The row inequality of the current certificate}\label{sec:nearmax-row}
Notation of \autoref{ch:certified} and \autoref{ch:pinned}: $h=(f+g)/2$, $k=(f-g)/2$, $Ph=tZ$, $c_1,c_A,c_B$ the coefficients of $k$ against $1,|Z|^2-1,Z^2/\sqrt2$,
$\xi=(c_1,c_A,\overline{c_B})$, $p=Q_wc_1$, $\tau=\norm p_2$, $U=|p|^2/\tau^2$, $x^2=\E U^2-2$, $X=Q_w\xi$ with $X_1=p$, $H_\beta=Qh+\beta v/\varepsilon$ with
$v=Z(|Z|^2-2)$, $D_1=\operatorname{diag}(-1,\varepsilon,\varepsilon)$. A row (or a combined profile, \S\ref{sec:nearmax-mix}) has a gate matrix $E$ (real symmetric $3\times3$), an $R$
block, $\ell,\beta$, and the coupling $A=\delta(e_1+b)$. The $k$-side gate inequality splits $\E_w[\xi^*E\xi]$ by remote chaos level. On the remote first chaos, with
$x_\bullet=\Pi^{\rm rem}_{1,0}\xi=(x_0,x_A,x_B)$ ($x_0=P_{\rm rem}Pk$, and $r_A=\varepsilon x_A$, $\overline{r_B}=\varepsilon x_B$ in the notation of \autoref{ch:weakk}),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nm-level1}
-\norm{x_0}^2+\varepsilon\norm{x_A}^2+\varepsilon\norm{x_B}^2=\langle x_\bullet,D_1x_\bullet\rangle\le\langle x_\bullet,Ex_\bullet\rangle\qquad(E\succeq D_1);
\end{equation}
on the levels $0$ and $\ge2$ other than the cubic one it needs $E\succeq0$, and on the cubic level the pinned completion uses $X_1=p$ exactly. Taking the fibre supremum yields
the shared row (Lean \code{RowShared}, \pathref{Grothe/Lower/Boxes.lean})
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nm-row}
V\le t^2-2\ell t+\frac{2\beta^2}\varepsilon+\E\Psi_E(\delta\tau\sqrt U)-\delta\tau^2-\E[X^*NX+2\Re(\bar p\,g\cdot X)]-\varepsilon\norm{H_\beta}^2 ,
\end{equation}
and the box value $\max_t(t^2-2\ell t)+2\beta^2/\varepsilon+\max_x\max_\tau(L(\tau,x)-D_{\rm pin}\tau^2)+10^{-11}$ of \code{combined.py} drops the retained square ($-\varepsilon\norm{H}^2\le0$)
after it has served the mixing identity. The gate matrix enters twice: in the potential $\Psi_E(s)=\sup_m\int(|L+B\cdot(m+sa)|+|L-B\cdot(m+sa)|)\,d\gamma-m^{\mathsf T}M^{-1}m$ with
$M=E\oplus E\oplus R\oplus R\oplus\varepsilon I_2$, which \emph{decreases} when $E$ decreases, and in the pinned price $D_{\rm pin}=e-\varepsilon+2A_0-(n+A_R)^{\mathsf T}C^{-1}(n+A_R)$
\eqref{eq:pinned4}, which never increases when $E$ decreases.

\subsection{Cut (a): rotation stationarity}\label{sec:nearmax-rot}
\paragraph{Hypothesis.} \textbf{(H$_{\rm rot}$)} $(f,g)$ is a global maximiser of $\Re\langle f,Tg\rangle$ over disc-valued pairs on $\C^n$ for a fixed $n$, made unimodular by phase
replacements, with $Z$ chosen after them. Such a pair exists in every dimension (\autoref{ch:weakk}, \S\ref{sec:rotgate-reading}), and $\sup_n$ of these maxima is the norm,
so a cap valid at every finite-dimensional maximiser bounds the scalar cap of \code{TransferInputsL2}\stale{22 Oct}{proved in Lean: \code{transferInputs\_of\_witnessCap}, \code{witnessCap\_of\_reduction}, \code{lower\_bound\_of\_reduction}}. No $\delta$-near version is needed. (b) below needs a different hypothesis.

\paragraph{The constraint.} Under (H$_{\rm rot}$), Ward's identity $d+\varepsilon t x_0+\varepsilon J_G^*H_\beta=0$ holds for \emph{every} $\beta$: the $\beta$-terms cancel because
$J_G^*B=-d$. With the remote-sensitive tensor bound \eqref{eq:remote-rotation-omega} and the bounded-response budget \eqref{eq:rotgate-clipping} at $\lambda=3/2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nm-ward}
\varepsilon\norm{H_\beta}^2\ \ge\ \frac{\norm{v_\beta(t)\cdot x_\bullet}^2}{\varepsilon F(t,\tau,x)},\qquad v_\beta(t)=(t,2\beta,\sqrt2\beta),\quad
F=9\gamma(t)-\bigl(9-3\min\{3,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2x\}\bigr)\tau^2,\quad\gamma(t)=1+K_+-3t .
\end{equation}
Here $\tau$ and $x$ are \emph{exactly} the certificate's: the remote cubic of $G=\varepsilon Qk$ is $\varepsilon p$ with $p=P^{\rm rem}_{2,1}k=Q_wc_1$, so $v_0=\varepsilon^2\tau^2$ and
$\kappa_q=\varepsilon^4\tau^4x^2$. The bound is decreasing in $\tau$ and increasing in $x$, so on a box it holds with $(\tau_-,x_+)$, and $7/8>\sqrt3/2$ gives a rational version
(\S\ref{sec:remote-sensitive-rotation}). Inputs: Ward identity and $\norm{D_pG}^2\le\Omega$ \st{P} (finite audits \st{C}); influence bound $I_p(q)\le\min\{3v_0,\sqrt{3\kappa_q}/2\}$
\st{P} (its ingredient $4\norm{\Pi_{1,1}|q|^2}^2\le3\kappa$ is \st{L}, \code{Influence.lean}; $\operatorname{Tr}M_q^2=\norm{\Pi_{1,1}|q|^2}^2$ is \st{P}); $K(3/2)<K_+$ \st{C}.

\paragraph{How it enters the row: not as a box term.} The right side of \eqref{eq:nm-ward} depends on $x_\bullet$, the remote first-chaos part of $\xi$. In \eqref{eq:nm-row} this
vector no longer appears: it was absorbed by the level-one inequality \eqref{eq:nm-level1} (whose slack $\langle x_\bullet,(E-D_1)x_\bullet\rangle\ge0$ is discarded) and by the fibre
supremum. Adding \eqref{eq:nm-ward} to \eqref{eq:nm-row} therefore gives $V\le(\ref{eq:nm-row})-\norm{v\cdot x_\bullet}^2/(\varepsilon F)$ with $x_\bullet$ free, whose supremum over
$x_\bullet$ is the old bound. \textbf{With the potential tables fixed, the cut is worth exactly zero} \st{P}. It pays only through the gate: spend a fraction $\mu\in[0,1]$ of the retained
square on level one,
\[
-\norm{x_0}^2+\varepsilon\norm{x_A}^2+\varepsilon\norm{x_B}^2-\mu\varepsilon\norm{H_\beta}^2\le\Bigl\langle x_\bullet,\Bigl(D_1-\frac{\mu}{\varepsilon F}v_\beta v_\beta^{\mathsf T}\Bigr)x_\bullet\Bigr\rangle ,
\]
so the gate may be lowered to any $E'$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nm-gate}
E'\succeq D_1-\frac{\mu}{\varepsilon F(t,\tau,x)}v_\beta(t)v_\beta(t)^{\mathsf T},\qquad E'\succ0,\qquad C'=E'_{RR}\succ0 ,
\end{equation}
($E'\succeq0$ on the other levels; $E'\succ0$ and $C'\succ0$ for the inverse forms of the potential and of the pinned price). The \emph{rotation row} is then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nm-rotrow}
V\le t^2-2\ell t+\frac{2\beta^2}\varepsilon+\E\Psi_{E'}(\delta\tau\sqrt U)-D_{\rm pin}(E')\tau^2-\E\norm{Y+C'^{-1}(n'+A_R)p}^2_{C'}-(1-\mu)\varepsilon\norm{H_\beta}^2 .
\end{equation}
So the ``extra term'' is a change of the gate matrix with multiplier $\mu$ (in practice $\mu=1$; then nothing is left for the $\beta$-variance mixing identity), and the gain is
$\E[\Psi_E-\Psi_{E'}](\delta\tau\sqrt U)$ under the worst law, minus any loss of price, $(D_{\rm pin}(E)-D_{\rm pin}(E'))\tau^2\ge0$. The loss is zero for $E'=E-\Delta$ with
$\Delta\succeq0$ and $\Delta w=0$, $w=(1,-C^{-1}(n+A_R))$ (\autoref{ch:rotation-gate-refinement}, \S6). Since $\Psi_{E'}\le\Psi_E$, old caps stay valid fallbacks; new caps need
recertification (anchor rebasing, re-run of the localised S-lemma pieces, \S\ref{sec:nearmax-build}).

\paragraph{Compatibility with the shared residual.}\label{sec:nearmax-mix} (i) One law: \eqref{eq:nm-ward} is a statement about the same pair as the reduction, so the same
$(\Omega,\mu,U,\tau,X)$ serve all rows and all rotation profiles; nothing about the residual law is assumed beyond $\tau,x$, which are already certificate variables. (ii) Mixtures:
freeze the mixture, then spend the square once. For a profile with components $(r,w_r)$ and a common $\Delta$, the combined gate is $\bar E-\Delta=\sum w_r(E_r-\Delta)$ with
$\bar\beta=\sum w_r\beta_r$ and the convexity bound $\Psi_{\bar E-\Delta}^{\rm comb}(s)\le\sum_rw_r\Psi_{E_r-\Delta}(\delta_rs)$ \eqref{eq:rotgate-potential-mixture}; only the
\emph{combined} gate \eqref{eq:nm-gate} with $\bar\beta$ is required, the components need not satisfy any gate. (iii) Joint mixtures of two rotation batches are one flattened
profile (components with their own $\Delta_k$; $\sum_k\lambda_k\sum_rw_{kr}(E_r-\Delta_k)$) whose gate must be re-checked: \eqref{eq:nm-gate} is not convex in $\beta$ because of
$v_\beta v_\beta^{\mathsf T}$. (iv) A rotation profile with $\mu=1$ cannot be mixed with an ordinary row by the square identity; doing so loses
$\theta(1-\theta)\,2(\beta_1-\beta_2)^2/\varepsilon$. (v) Domain: \eqref{eq:nm-gate} holds only at the actual $(t,\tau,x)$, so a rotation profile may be used only in boxes inside
$t\in[t_-,t_+]$, $\tau\ge\tau_-$, $x\le x_+$. Its node certificates and chord segments need \emph{not} lie in that domain: they bound $\E\Psi_{E'}(\tau_j\sqrt U)$ for a fixed function
$\Psi_{E'}$, and the gate is used only in \eqref{eq:nm-rotrow} at the true point. This is weaker than the ``every interpolation segment inside the box'' obligation of
\S\ref{sec:remote-sensitive-rotation}.

\paragraph{Lean form.} Modelled on \code{RotShared} (\code{b78baab}, \pathref{Grothe/Lower/Boxes.lean}): for each rotation profile $p$ (components, rebased component tables,
$\Delta$, gate domain), \code{SharedReduction} gains
$\forall p\in\text{profiles},\ p.t_-\le t\le p.t_+\to p.\tau_-\le\tau\to x\le p.x_+\to\code{RotShared}\,p\ldots$, where \code{RotShared} is \eqref{eq:nm-rotrow} with $\mu=1$ (no retained
square), the component potentials with \code{CapsHold} for the rebased tables, and the pinned gate term of $E'$. The gate check \eqref{eq:nm-gate} is part of the paper
justification of the hypothesis (exact checker in Python, seven principal minors over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$), as on \code{b78baab}. The $\tau,x$ conditions are the only additions to
\code{RotShared}; with $\tau_-=0$, $x_+=31/4$ they disappear and the $\gamma$-only gate of \autoref{ch:rotation-gate-refinement} is recovered.

\subsection{Cut (b): the fresh second-chaos phase lift}\label{sec:nearmax-lift}
\paragraph{Hypothesis.} \textbf{(H$_\delta$)} $J_0=\Re\langle f,Tg\rangle\ge M-\delta$ with $M=\sup_n\norm{T_n}$ (all dimensions). A fixed-dimensional maximiser need not satisfy the
cut (adjoining the fresh mode may raise its value); the $n$-dimensional maximiser satisfies (H$_\delta$) with $\delta_n=M-M_n\to0$, so (a) and (b) can be combined along that
sequence, the bound becoming $V\le B+O(\sqrt{\delta_n})\to B$.

\paragraph{The scalar instance in unused directions.} Take an independent remote first-chaos Gaussian $X$, $A=\Re(\bar X\psi)$, $B=-A$, $\psi$ a function of the old coordinates.
Then $P(fA)=\tfrac X2\E[f\bar\psi]$ and, with $a=\Re(\bar fTg)$, $b=\Re(\bar gTf)$,
\[
H(A,-A)=\tfrac12\E[(a+b)|\psi|^2]-\tfrac\varepsilon2\bigl(|\E h\bar\psi|^2-|\E k\bar\psi|^2\bigr).
\]
With separate scalings of $\psi_A,\psi_B$ and Cauchy--Schwarz, at an attained (dimension-free) maximiser with phase best responses ($a=|Tg|$, $b=|Tf|$) the cut
$H\ge0$ for all such multipliers is equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nm-scalar}
\varepsilon^2\,\E\frac1{|Tg|}\,\E\frac1{|Tf|}\le4 ,
\end{equation}
which is \eqref{eq:fresh-matrix} in one unused direction ($M_f=\tfrac12\E|Tg|^{-1}$); at the diffuse Gaussian critical response $\E|Tg|^{-1}=2/\varepsilon$ and it is an equality
\st{P}. For near-maximizers one must clip ($|A|\le K$) and use the error $10K^2\sqrt\delta$.

\paragraph{It is not expressible in $(t,\tau,x,X)$.} The cut involves the pointwise weights $|Tf|,|Tg|$ with $Tg=tZ-Pk-\varepsilon Qh+G$ and first-chaos projections of $fA$. In the
reduction, $Qh$ survives only through the retained square (it was eliminated by Young's inequality), the visible response only inside the fibre supremum $\Psi$, and $\psi$ would have
to be a function of both. A Lagrangian $J_0+\lambda(H+e)$ would need (i) a new fibre potential with $\psi$ and the weights built in (new Gaussian integrations), (ii) a gate for the convex
term $+\tfrac{\varepsilon\lambda}2|\E k\bar\psi|^2$, and (iii) a pointwise treatment of $\Re(\bar h\,Qh)|\psi|^2$. None of these is a term in $(t,\tau,x,X)$; the box formula cannot carry
the cut \st{P}.

\paragraph{It is inactive at the binding configuration \st{F}.} At the relaxation's fibre maximiser (where $Tf,Tg$ are replaced by $L\pm B\cdot(m^*+sa)$) for row $0.85$, under the
worst law of batch \code{d2\_a112\_x72} at $(\tau,x)=(0.245,0.70)$ (LP support of 12 atoms, value reproducing the exact witness to $3\cdot10^{-7}$):
$\E|L-p|^{-1}=\E|L+p|^{-1}=2.6494$ and $\varepsilon^2\cdot2.6494^2=3.896<4$. Without residual ($s=0$) the profile violates \eqref{eq:nm-scalar} ($4.512$); the residual acts as the buffer.
With the residual entering as a pure shift $\varepsilon\tau\sqrt U$ instead, $3.932<4$. So even an implemented version of the unused-direction cut would not cut the binding
configuration; other multipliers ($\psi$ correlated with the response) are untested. \textbf{Float gain of (b): $0$.}

\subsection{Float measurements}\label{sec:nearmax-measure}
\paragraph{Method \st{F}.} For a joint batch (rows, weights) of cert-combined-e at a binding $(\tau,x)$: the moment/Stein LP of \code{gen\_nodes.py} in float (dual form, the batch's own cut
banks near $\tau$), which reproduces the stored exact witness values to $3\cdot10^{-7}$; its primal worst law; the true potentials $\Psi_{E_r}$ and $\Psi_{E_r-\Delta}$ by a float fibre
optimiser (polar Gauss--Legendre $500\times160$, L-BFGS, warm starts; the copy of \code{psi\_float.py} with $E$ as argument) on a $z$-grid, interpolated, subtracted from the exact mixed
table; the LP re-solved; the price recomputed. $\Delta$ is chosen by a $3\times3$ SDP maximising the first-order gain $\operatorname{tr}(W\Delta)$, $W=\sum$ (law weight)
$E^{-1}m^*m^{*\mathsf T}E^{-1}$ over the support, subject to $\Delta\succeq0$, $\Delta w=0$, the gate \eqref{eq:nm-gate} at both ends of the $t$-interval with the box's $(\tau_-,x_+)$,
and $E_r-\Delta\succeq0.02I$. Four variants: per-row gates without rotation ($E_r-\Delta\succeq D_1$ for each row), mixed gate without rotation ($\bar E-\Delta\succeq D_1$), rotation
with $F=9\gamma$, rotation with the law-sensitive $F$.

\subsection{Status and build order}\label{sec:nearmax-build}
\paragraph{Not built.} The threshold ($2\cdot10^{-7}$ in $B$) is passed by the hypothesis-free mixed-gate decrease, not by the near-maximizer constraint: on the present slab the latter
gains nothing in $B$, and its law-sensitive refinement is worth $\le2.8\cdot10^{-7}$ in $V$ before the envelope cap. A certificate built now around (H$_{\rm rot}$) would attribute to
a new hypothesis a gain that needs none. Recommended order:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Profile gates without rotation.} Combined profiles $(\{(r,w_r)\},\Delta)$ with $\bar E-\Delta\succeq D_1$, $\Delta w=0$ (or an exact price recomputation). Hypothesis: the
Gaussian reduction for a combined profile (same paper argument as \code{SharedReduction}\stale{22 Oct}{proved in Lean: \code{sharedReduction\_frame}, \code{reductionHyp\_of\_nodeCaps}; only \code{NodeCaps} remains}; no maximiser). Caps for $E_r-\Delta$ of rows $0.84$--$0.86$: exact anchor rebasing (cheap, done
above for one row), re-run of the localised S-lemma pieces of \pathref{cert-0745d/midcaps} and \pathref{cert-0745e/midcaps} with $Q'=M'^{-1}$ (the integrals do not depend on $E$;
$K'=Q'-H^*+\sum\lambda vv^{\mathsf T}\succeq K\succ0$ automatically; about $2\cdot10^4$ pieces, roughly an hour on 6 cores), and the price layer beyond $s=0.42$. A common $\Delta$ for all
profiles makes this a one-off per row. Then regenerate the joint batches on the new mixed tables, rebuild the cover, and extend the slab below $0.847$.
\item \emph{Rotation increment.} Same tables with a larger $\Delta$ allowed by \eqref{eq:nm-gate} with $9\gamma$; hypothesis \code{RotShared} with $t$-domain. Worth $\approx1\cdot10^{-6}$ in
$B$ once the envelope no longer binds.
\item \emph{Law-sensitive denominator.} Adds the $(\tau_-,x_+)$ domain to each rotation profile and to the cover checker; $\approx2\cdot10^{-7}$.
\end{enumerate}
\paragraph{Side observation.} The ``per-row'' column is not always zero: for rows $0.84/0.85$ a price-neutral decrease satisfying both rows' own gates exists ($4.57\cdot10^{-6}$). The
rows were optimised for the old price $\kappa$, not for $D_{\rm pin}$; re-optimising single rows in the $\delta$-free pinned parameterisation of \S\ref{sec:pinned-delta} is therefore
also open \st{F}.

\paragraph{Open issues.} (1) The measurements use float potentials and the existing cut banks; the certified gain depends on recertifying caps at $s\in[0.1,0.9]$ for $E-\Delta$.
(2) The binding set after the improvement is unknown; four batches were measured, the envelope bound is exact. (3) (b) was tested only in unused directions and only at the
relaxation's fibre maximiser, which is not a response pair of the original problem. (4) $\operatorname{Tr}M_q^2=\norm{\Pi_{1,1}|q|^2}^2$ and the Ward identity are paper proofs.
