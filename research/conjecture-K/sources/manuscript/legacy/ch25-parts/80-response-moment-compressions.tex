\subsubsection{Two explicit first/cubic matrix inequalities}
For a bounded response $f$, $|f|\le1$, on standard complex Gaussian space,
write the unnormalised Wick moments
\[
 p_i=\E[\bar Z_i f],\qquad
 m_{ij;k}=\E[\overline{Z_iZ_j\bar Z_k-\delta_{ik}Z_j-\delta_{jk}Z_i}\,f].
\]
Thus $m_{ij;k}=m_{ji;k}$ and
$\|Qf\|^2=\sum_{i\le j,k}|m_{ij;k}|^2/(1+\delta_{ij})$.
Define
\[
 A_{(ij),k}=m_{ij;k}+\delta_{ik}p_j+\delta_{jk}p_i\quad(i\le j),
 \qquad B_{i,0}=p_i,\quad B_{i,(j,k)}=m_{ik;j}+\delta_{ij}p_k.
\]
With $D=\operatorname{diag}(1+\delta_{ij})$, every such response satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:response-compression-lmis}
 \boxed{\quad
 \begin{pmatrix}D&A\\A^*&I\end{pmatrix}\succeq0,
 \qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}I&B\\B^*&I\end{pmatrix}\succeq0.
 \quad}
\end{equation}
\begin{proof}
Multiplication by $f$ is a contraction on Gaussian $L^2$.
Compress it from $\operatorname{span}\{Z_k\}$ to
$\operatorname{span}\{Z_iZ_j:i\le j\}$; their Gram matrices are $I,D$,
and Wick expansion gives the matrix $A$.
Compress from $\operatorname{span}\{1,Z_j\bar Z_k-\delta_{jk}\}$ to
$\operatorname{span}\{Z_i\}$; both bases are orthonormal, and expansion
gives $B$. The Schur forms of these contraction inequalities are
\eqref{eq:response-compression-lmis}.
The argument is dimension-free. No residual independence or moment bound
beyond Gaussian polynomial integrability is used.
\end{proof}
For charge-one $f$, the other nonzero blocks on quadratic test polynomials
are transposes of these blocks after permutations. Charge averaging is
contractive and preserves $p,m$, so no charge assumption on $f$ is needed.
The checker separately audits 2,140 exact polynomial and Gram identities
in dimensions one through five; the preceding argument supplies the
arbitrary-dimension proof.

Apply both inequalities separately to $f_+$ and $f_-$, substituting
\[
 p^\pm=te_0\pm Pk,\qquad m^\pm=m(Qh)\pm m(F)/\varepsilon,
 \quad h=(f_++f_-)/2,\ k=(f_+-f_-)/2,\ F=\varepsilon Qk.
\]
Together with Parseval, this is a finite convex relaxation for the minimum
of the existing retained penalty
$J=\|Pk\|^2+\varepsilon\|Qh\|^2$ at a prescribed $t,F$.
Its lower bound on $J$ replaces another bound for these same squares;
it must not be added to one.

\subsubsection{A certified central slab with arbitrary additional coordinates}
Use all Wick polynomials of degree at most three as multiplication tests.
Their charge-one blocks involve first, third, and fifth response moments.
Keep the fifth moments as free variables, with their Parseval inequality.
All entries in the unnormalised basis are integers and the Gram matrices
are diagonal factorials. Rational positive semidefinite dual multipliers
and positive Parseval multipliers give a quadratic Lagrangian; exact
completion of squares supplies a certified lower bound on $J$.

\begin{theorem}[\st{P,C}: a response slab below the $1.402$ cap]
\label{thm:response-moment-slab}
Let $\varepsilon=1891/2500$, $Z,X$ be any two orthonormal complex Gaussian
coordinates, $|f_\pm|\le1$, and
\[
 Ph=tZ,\qquad F=\varepsilon Qk,\qquad
 21/25\le t\le851/1000,\qquad \|F\|^2\le1/20.
\]
For the six two-coordinate cubic moments put
\[
 u_{ab;cd}=\E[\overline{H_{a,c}(Z)H_{b,d}(X)}\,Qk],
 \quad a+b=c+d+1,\quad a+b+c+d=3,
\]
where $H$ denotes the unnormalised complex Wick polynomial.
Suppose their real parts lie within $1/200$ of the center
\[
 u^0_{02;01}=1/8,\qquad u^0_{11;10}=1/8,\qquad
 u^0_{20;01}=1/4,
\]
with the other three centers zero. Their imaginary parts and every
coefficient involving further coordinates are unrestricted, subject to
the displayed total energy bound. Then
\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \operatorname{Re}\langle f_-,(P-\varepsilon Q)f_+\rangle
 <1781/2500=0.7124<500/701.
 \quad}
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}[Certificate and dimension-free extension]
For every feasible response, with $M_{s,b}$ its multiplication blocks
and $P_s$ its retained Parseval sum, the dual Lagrangian satisfies
\[
 \mathcal L=J-\sum_{s,b}\operatorname{tr}(Y_{s,b}M_{s,b})
              +\sum_s\lambda_s(P_s-1)\le J.
\]
The exact dual has twelve rational positive semidefinite matrices and
two positive Parseval multipliers. Rational LDL verifies every matrix.
The degree-three multiplication tables are regenerated by exact Wick
expansion; the fifth moments are not set to zero. Completing all 32
real unknown coefficient squares, with outward rational evaluation on
the displayed box, gives
\[
 J>0.0779336632133.
\]
The exact original-response identity is
\[
 \operatorname{Re}\langle f_-,(P-\varepsilon Q)f_+\rangle
 =t^2+\|F\|^2/\varepsilon-J.
\]
The rational checker bounds its right side by
$0.7123699280082\ldots<0.7124$; the decimal is explanatory, not proof
arithmetic. Real dual multipliers use only real parts of the prescribed
moments. Discarded imaginary contributions are nonnegative squares.
Conditioning on $(Z,X)$ preserves boundedness and these moments; the
full penalty dominates the penalty on that two-coordinate projection.
Thus arbitrary additional coordinates and dependent sectors are allowed.
\end{proof}

At the center the indicated projection of $F$ is
\[
 F_0=\frac\varepsilon{16}X(|X|^2-2)
 +\frac\varepsilon8(|Z|^2-1)X+\frac\varepsilon8Z^2\bar X,
 \qquad \|F_0\|^2=0.03128895875.
\]
This leaves about $0.01871$ for additional energy. It does not assert
that this exact projection is attained by a bounded response pair.
Three narrower boxes in the package permit total energies
$0.067676$, $0.069794$, and $0.070125$, with caps below $0.7132$.
A separate exact dual excludes the center with $u^0_{02;01}=3/8$

\paragraph{The remaining cover problem.}
The variance bound above concerns the full $F$, not the remote variance
$e$. These are genuine dimension-free response cells, without a fresh
buffer, conditional Gaussianity, or stationarity assumption. An arbitrary
high-payoff tensor need not admit a remote direction with its projected
moments in these cells. Covering those other projections, especially
diffuse tensors, and assembling all rows at one common $\varepsilon$
remain necessary before claiming a new endpoint.
