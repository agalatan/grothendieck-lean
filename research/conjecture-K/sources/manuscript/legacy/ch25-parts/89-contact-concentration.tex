\subsection{An exact contact certificate for a dominant Wick direction}
\label{sec:contact-concentration-cone}
\paragraph{Status and scope.}
The analytic argument is \st{P}; the three support integrals and the
finite comparisons are \st{C}, freshly replayable with Python's standard
library. This is a new region for actual responses, not a proof of raw K
and not a new global endpoint. It implements the joint-support programme
without approximating the unknown cubic's law. No independence assumption
is imposed on its remainder, and no moment bound or fresh buffer is used.

Put $\epsilon=1891/2500$, $p=1/\epsilon$, $h=(f_++f_-)/2$,
$k=(f_+-f_-)/2$, $Ph=tZ$, and $F=\epsilon Qk$, where $|f_\pm|\le1$.
Let $V=\|F\|^2$ and
\[
 \mathcal N=t^2+pV-\|Pk\|^2-\epsilon\|Qh\|^2.
\]
For any standard complex Gaussian linear form $X$ orthogonal to $Z$, set
\[
 H_X=X(|X|^2-2)/\sqrt2,\qquad
 c_X=|\langle H_X,F\rangle|.
\]
The supremum below is over all such directions in the actual Gaussian
space, not over a fixed finite list.

\begin{theorem}[\st{P,C}: four-fifths concentration alternative]
\label{thm:contact-concentration-cone}
In every finite dimension, if
\[
 \frac{21}{25}\le t\le\frac{851}{1000},\qquad
 c_X^2\ge\frac45\|F\|^2\quad\hbox{for some }X\perp Z,
\]
then
\[
 \boxed{\quad\mathcal N<\frac{14263}{20000}=0.71315
            <\frac{35663}{50000}<\frac{500}{701}.\quad}
\]
Equivalently, the theorem allows $F=\zeta H_X+R$ with
$\langle H_X,R\rangle=0$ and $\|R\|\le|\zeta|/2$.
The remainder may use $X,Z$ and arbitrarily many further coordinates,
with all phases and visible sectors present. There is no amplitude
restriction on $\zeta$ and no separate bound on $V$.
\end{theorem}

\subsubsection{The joint support constraint pays the existing penalty}
Write $\psi_X=\sqrt2H_X$ and rotate the phase of $X$ so that
$u=\langle\psi_X,F\rangle=\sqrt2c_X\ge0$ is real. This rotation does
not affect its joint Gaussian law with $Z$. For rational $\ell,b,d\ge0$
with $\ell\ge2b$, put
\[
 L=Z\{\ell+b(|Z|^2-2)\},\qquad D=d\psi_X,\qquad
 I(\ell,b,d)=\E|L+D|.
\]
The two signs have the same expectation by $X\mapsto-X$.
For every actual response, the pointwise support inequality gives
\[
 2\Re\E(\bar Lh+\bar Dk)\le\E(|L+D|+|L-D|).
\]
With $m=\Re\langle Z(|Z|^2-2),h\rangle$, this becomes
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contact-concentration-support}
 \boxed{\quad \ell t+bm+pdu\le I(\ell,b,d).\quad}
\end{equation}
The test $D$ is cubic, so its pairing with $k$ is exactly its pairing
with $pF$; unspecified coefficients do not enter. If $I\le H$ and
$b>0$, the same response therefore satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contact-concentration-penalty}
 \|Pk\|^2+\epsilon\|Qh\|^2
 \ge\frac{[\ell t+pdu-H]_+^2}{2pb^2}.
\end{equation}
Indeed $\|H_Z\|=1$ and a positive bracket forces $m$ to be negative.
This is also the exact minimization over a common positive scale of
$(L,D)$ in the canonical dual. It does not add a second charge for
$Pk$ or $Qh$; it is a new complete lower bound for their retained cost.
For $b=0$, \eqref{eq:contact-concentration-support} is a feasibility cut.

The fresh integral certificates are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 \ell&b&d&I(\ell,b,d)<H\\\hline
 1&0&7/25&46999/50000\\
 3/5&18/125&111/1000&489287/1000000\\
 39/50&37/250&37/250&32621/50000
\end{array}
\]
The default directed upper values are respectively less than
$0.939978898$, $0.489286432$, and $0.652402237$.
These decimals are explanatory; the rational entries are the witnesses.

\subsubsection{Reduction of the entire region to seven rational endpoints}
Here is the complete finite part of the proof. Write $\eta=4/5$,
$u_0=9/40$, $H_0=46999/50000$, and $d_0=7/25$. Concentration and the
first support cut imply
\[
 V\le u^2/(2\eta),\qquad u\le U(t):=(H_0-t)/(pd_0).
\]
If $u\le u_0$, use the independent visible support inequality
\[
 J:=\|Pk\|^2+\epsilon\|Qh\|^2
 \ge\frac1{2p}\left(\frac{19}{10}t-\frac{124233393}{10^8}\right)^2.
\]
The bracket is positive on the central interval. The resulting cap is
increasing there and at $t=851/1000$ is below $0.712970185$.
For completeness, its sole integral constant is checked afresh from
\[
 \E\sqrt T\,|T-1/10|
 =\frac75\frac{\sqrt\pi}{2}
 +2(1/10)^{5/2}\sum_{n\ge0}
 \frac{(-1/10)^n}{n!(n+3/2)(n+5/2)}
 <\frac{124233393}{10^8}.
\]
Thus this branch does not inherit the earlier eight-integral certificate.

For $u\ge u_0$, use the second row on $t\in[.84,.849]$ and the third
row on $t\in[.849,.851]$. For the selected $(\ell,b,d,H)$ define
\[
 C(t,u)=t^2+\frac{pu^2}{2\eta}
           -\frac{(\ell t+pdu-H)^2}{2pb^2}.
\]
The bracket is positive. This quadratic is convex in $u$, since
$\frac p{2\eta}-\frac{pd^2}{2b^2}>0$. It suffices to consider
$u=u_0$ and $u=U(t)$. The first endpoint is increasing in $t$ on its
assigned interval: its second derivative is negative and its first
derivative remains positive at the right endpoint. The second endpoint
is convex in $t$, with quadratic coefficient
\[
 1+\frac1{2\eta p d_0^2}
       -\frac{(\ell-d/d_0)^2}{2pb^2}>0.
\]
Consequently only three endpoint comparisons per row remain. Their
rational values are bounded above, respectively, by
\[
 (.711144674,\ .712003698,\ .713147927),\qquad
 (.713068636,\ .712850849,\ .713070930).
\]
Together with the small-$u$ branch these prove the theorem. The checker
verifies every sign and endpoint with fractions, including the strict
comparison with $14263/20000$.

\subsubsection{A derivative-free integral certificate}
Let $M(a,r)=\E_\theta|a+re^{i\theta}|$. This function is convex in each
nonnegative amplitude. In the radial variables $x=|Z|$, $y=|X|$,
which have density $2xe^{-x^2}$, the amplitudes are
\[
 A(x)=(\ell-2b)x+bx^3,\qquad B(y)=dy|y^2-2|.
\]
Partition $[0,6]$ into rational cells. On each cell replace its amplitude
law by the two endpoint atoms at the amplitude's minimum and maximum,
with the same mass and first moment. Convexity makes this an upper
cubature for $M$. Apply the two replacements successively; the test
variables $Z,X$ are independent, although the unknown remainder is not.
The extremum at $y=\sqrt{2/3}$ and zero at $y=\sqrt2$ are included
with directed root bounds, so monotonicity is not assumed across them.

Masses are differences of $e^{-x^2}$. First moments reduce to
$\int2x^2e^{-x^2}dx$ and $\int2x^4e^{-x^2}dx$, evaluated with outward
integer intervals using the entire alternating series for
$\int_0^x e^{-s^2}ds$ and exact recurrences. With $M_*=\max(a,r)$ and
$z=(\min(a,r)/M_*)^2$, the angular mean has the positive series
\[
 M(a,r)=M_*\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n,\quad
 a_0=1,\quad a_n=a_{n-1}\frac{(2n-3)^2}{4n^2},\quad
 \sum a_n=4/\pi.
\]
Hence its upper enclosure is
\[
 M_*\left(\sum_{n=0}^Na_nz^n+
            \left(\frac4\pi-\sum_{n=0}^Na_n\right)z^{N+1}\right).
\]
The value at $a=r=0$ is zero. Machin's formula encloses $\pi$.
The discarded radial tails use $M(a,r)\le a+r$ and
\[
 \int_6^\infty2x^2e^{-x^2}dx\le(6+1/12)e^{-36},\qquad
 \int_6^\infty2x^4e^{-x^2}dx\le(216+9+1/8)e^{-36}.
\]
Every bound holds at norm zeros and on the entire unbounded domain.
No quadrature derivative or smoothness assumption is required.

\paragraph{A second regional consequence.}
The middle trial at fixed scale also proves
\[
 .84\le t\le .84625,\quad c_X\ge71/400,\quad V\le1/25
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mathcal N<.71325.
\]
This consequence and the concentration theorem are separate sufficient
conditions; neither assumes that every survivor has a large Wick
projection.

\paragraph{The new necessary condition, and its limit.}
A central-row response exceeding the design cap must now have
\[
 \boxed{\quad\sup_{X\perp Z}|\langle H_X,F\rangle|^2
                     <\frac45\|F\|^2.\quad}
\]
This removes a dimension-free concentrated branch without a fresh-buffer
hypothesis. It is not a bound on maximal directional influence: for the
unit coupled monomial $X_1^2\bar X_2/\sqrt2$, the largest squared
projection onto any $H_X$ is only $4/27$, since
$4/27-r^2(1-r)=(r-2/3)^2(r+1/3)$ on $[0,1]$.
Diffuse tensors and such nondiagonal concentrated tensors still need
certificates, as do the flank rows outside the central interval.
The manuscript's best complete numerical endpoint remains that of
\S\ref{sec:finer-spectral-certificate}; this regional proof changes none
of its integration or transfer status.
