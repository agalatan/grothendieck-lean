\subsection{Statement}
Let $(Z,W)$ and the coordinates of $X\in\C^n$ be independent standard proper complex Gaussians, $L=Z(\ell+\beta(|Z|^2-2))=Z(a+b|Z|^2)$ with $a=699/1250$, $b=179/1250$, $H(Z)=Z(|Z|^2-2)/\sqrt2$,
$S=\sup_{v\ge0}\{2\E|L+\sqrt vW|-v/\varepsilon\}$.
\begin{theorem}[all remote cubics, \st{P} GPT + \st{C}]\label{thm:rescub}
For every $n\ge1$, $q\in\Hc21(\C^n)$ and $g\ge0$, with $Q=q(X)+gW$,
\[
2\E|L+Q|-\frac{\E|Q|^2}\varepsilon<\frac{54959}{40000}=1.373975,\qquad\text{hence}\qquad\Lambda(Q)<S+\frac{11}{500000}=S+2.2\cdot10^{-5},
\]
since the certificate also proves $S>1.373953525$. No kurtosis, amplitude or dimension restriction; $\E|L-Q|=\E|L+Q|$ by circularity and independence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[global diagonal removal, \st{P} GPT + \st{C}]\label{thm:rescub-diag}
For every complex random variable $Q$ independent of $Z$ with finite second moment and every $\nu\in\C$,
\[
\Lambda(Q+\nu H)\le\Lambda(Q)-\frac{20899}{1891000}|\nu|^2 .
\]
In particular Theorem~\ref{thm:rescub} holds for $q(X)+gW+\nu H(Z)$ with this strict deficit.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Moment input: the published constant $50$ suffices}
For $v=\E|Q|^2>0$ put $U=|Q|^2/v$, $x^2=\E U^2-2$. Only
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescub3}
\E U=1,\qquad\E U^2=2+x^2,\qquad\E U^3\le6+9x^2+50x^3,\qquad0\le x\le\tfrac{31}4
\end{equation}
is used (HJM, eq.~(7.3) and \S8.3; their $\E U^2\le62$ even gives $x\le\sqrt{60}$). The project's sharper $C_6=40.45313$ (a scalar consequence formalized under a cumulant tensor hypothesis), the bound on $\E\sqrt U$ and all Stein cuts are \emph{not} needed.
The buffer preserves \eqref{eq:rescub3}: with $u=\E|q|^2$ the circular cumulants $\kappa_4(q)=\E|q|^4-2u^2$ and $\kappa_6(q)=\E|q|^6-9u\E|q|^4+12u^3$ are unchanged by adding $gW$, so
$\kappa_6(Q)\le50\kappa_4(Q)^{3/2}$ and $\kappa_4(Q)\le60u^2\le60v^2$; expanding gives \eqref{eq:rescub3} for $Q$. The case $v=0$ follows by continuity.

\subsection{Derivative signs of the radial potential}
Let $f(u)=2\E|L+\sqrt u|$. Needed: $f'\ge0$, $f''\le0$, $f'''\ge0$, $f^{(4)}\le0$. With $H(r)=r(a+br)^2$, $h=H^{-1}$ and $T=|Z|^2\sim{\rm Exp}(1)$, $\mathbb P(|L|^2>u)=e^{-h(u)}$ and $h'(u)=R(h(u))$,
$R(r)=1/[(a+br)(a+3br)]$, which is completely monotone; with $R_j=(-1)^jR^{(j)}\ge0$ and $D_uR_j(h)=-R_0(h)R_{j+1}(h)$, induction gives $(-1)^{k-1}h^{(k)}\ge0$ and then $e^{-h}$ completely monotone,
so $L\overset d=\sqrt R\,G$ for a positive mixing variable $R$ (the mixture theorem of \S\ref{sec:kfull-global}). Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescub4}
f(u)=\frac1{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^\infty\Bigl[1-\E\frac{e^{-su/(1+sR)}}{1+sR}\Bigr]s^{-3/2}\,ds.
\end{equation}
For every integer $k\ge1$, differentiation gives
\[
(-1)^{k-1}f^{(k)}(u)=\frac1{\sqrt\pi}\int_0^\infty
\E\left[\frac{s^k e^{-su/(1+sR)}}{(1+sR)^{k+1}}\right]s^{-3/2}\,ds\ge0.
\]
The integrands are nonnegative, so Tonelli applies. At $u=0$ their integral equals
$\Gamma(k-\tfrac12)\E R^{1/2-k}/(2k!)$, which is finite: the exponential-mixture rate $R^{-1}$ has all integer moments, since the survival function $e^{-h}$ is analytic at zero; the fractional moments follow by domination by $1+R^{-k}$. This also supplies an integrable dominating function for every $u\ge0$ and justifies differentiation up to the endpoint.
\paragraph{Correction (15 October, 21:45; GPT's \code{weak-k} package, patch applied and verified by us, \autoref{ch:weakk}).} An earlier version appended the equality $f(u)=\sqrt\pi\,\E\sqrt{R+u}$ to \eqref{eq:rescub4}. That equality is false for a fixed shift $\sqrt u$: it describes adding an independent proper Gaussian of variance $u$. The correct Laplace formula above still proves all derivative signs, so the two-node theorem and certificates are unchanged. For example, when $R=r$ is deterministic, the fixed-shift function has $f''(0)=-\sqrt\pi/(8r^{3/2})$, whereas the Gaussian-variance formula has second derivative $-\sqrt\pi/(4r^{3/2})$.

\subsection{The two-node moment theorem}
\begin{lemma}[\st{P}]\label{lem:twonode}
Let $m>1$, $M>m^2$, and let $U\ge0$ satisfy $\E U=1$, $\E U^2\ge m$, $\E U^3\le M$. Put $A=\frac{M-m}{m-1}$, $B=\frac{M-m^2}{m-1}$, $r_+=\frac{A+\sqrt{A^2-4B}}2$, $r_-=B/r_+$, $p=\frac{1-r_-}{r_+-r_-}$.
Then $0<r_-<1<r_+$, $0<p<1$, and for every $v\ge0$
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescub6}
\E f(vU)\le(1-p)f(vr_-)+pf(vr_+).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $Y\in\{r_-,r_+\}$ with $\mathbb P(Y=r_+)=p$; the root equation gives $\E Y=1$, $\E Y^2=m$, $\E Y^3=M$. Let $P(t)=c_0+c_1t+c_2t^2+c_3t^3$ be the cubic Hermite interpolant of $f_v(t)=f(vt)$ at the two
nodes (values and first derivatives). The interpolation remainder is $f_v^{(4)}(\xi)(t-r_-)^2(t-r_+)^2/4!\le0$, so $P\ge f_v$ on all of $t\ge0$, outside the node interval included. The divided
difference $c_3=f_v[r_-,r_-,r_+,r_+]\ge0$ ($f'''\ge0$), and $c_2=f_v[r_-,r_-,r_+]-(2r_-+r_+)c_3\le0$ because $f_v$ is concave. Hence
$\E f_v(U)\le\E P(U)=c_0+c_1\E U+c_2\E U^2+c_3\E U^3\le c_0+c_1+c_2m+c_3M=\E P(Y)=\E f_v(Y)$. For exact second moment $m$ and third-moment bound $M$ the bound is attained by $Y$: it is the exact
value of the relaxed moment problem.
\end{proof}
At $x=0$ ($m=2$, $M=6$) the nodes are $2\pm\sqrt2$ with upper probability $(2-\sqrt2)/4$.

\subsection{Certifying every kurtosis and every variance}
Partition $0\le x\le31/4$ into 162 rational intervals $[x_-,x_+]$ (\code{residual\_certificate.json}); on each use $m=2+x_-^2$, $M=6+9x_+^2+50x_+^3$ ($M>m^2$ since $2+5x^2+x^3(50-x)>0$). The lemma
bounds the residual objective by $B_{m,M}(v)=(1-p)f(vr_-)+pf(vr_+)-v/\varepsilon$, concave on $v\ge0$. For $v_0>2d>0$ let $B_0,\ldots,B_3$ be its values at $v_0-2d,v_0-d,v_0+d,v_0+2d$; if $B_1>B_0$
and $B_3<B_2$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescub8}
\sup_{v\ge0}B_{m,M}(v)\le\max\{4B_1-3B_0,\ 4B_2-3B_3\}
\end{equation}
(the two outer secant lines of a concave function bound it on the two unbounded sides and, crossing, on the middle). Both slope signs and both bounds are checked in Arb on every band: all
accepted upper endpoints are $<54959/40000$ (largest $\approx1.373974765716$). The floating optimiser only proposes the partition and the four test points.

\subsection{Rigorous radial integration}
With $r=|Z|$, $X(r)=r(a+br^2)$, $s=\sqrt u$, the angular mean is $g_s(x)=2\E_\theta|xe^{i\theta}+s|=\frac4\pi(x+s)E\bigl(\frac{4xs}{(x+s)^2}\bigr)$, convex in $x\ge0$. On a radial cell $[l,h]$ with exact
Gaussian mass $p_c$ and first $X$-moment $m_c$, Jensen gives the lower bound $p_cg_s(m_c/p_c)$ and the chord between $X(l)$, $X(h)$ the upper bound; the moment uses
$\int_r^\infty2t^2(a+bt^2)e^{-t^2}dt=e^{-r^2}[ar+b(r^3+3r/2)]+\sqrt\pi(a/2+3b/4)\operatorname{erfc}(r)$. Adaptive subdivision until upper minus lower $<10^{-8}$ (7,522,878 cells in all); tail $r\ge6$ by
$g_s(X(r))\le2(X(r)+s)$; near the elliptic endpoint $1\le E(m)\le1+\sqrt{1-m}$. The benchmark uses the exact Rice mean at $\sigma=10043/50000$ with tolerance $10^{-9}$: $S>1.373953525$.

\subsection{What this corrects, and the remaining budget}\label{sec:rescub-budget}
The exact two-node optimum at $x=0$ is $S+9.77\cdot10^{-6}$ (floating), not the $6.3\cdot10^{-5}$ of \autoref{ch:certs}: a feasible dual gives an upper bound, not the size of the loss
(see the reading note). With $C_6=50$ a second maximum appears near $x=0.775$, $v=0.0444$, at $\approx1.37396805$; the certificate bounds both by $1.373975$.
\emph{Remaining obligation.} With $G=q(X)+(|Z|^2-1)A(X)+\frac{Z^2}{\sqrt2}B(X)+ZY(X)+\bar ZW(X)+\nu H(Z)$, the theorem allows arbitrary dependence inside $q$ and removes $\nu$ over a remote
baseline; the even-sector theorem of \S\ref{sec:profileslack} covers $q+ZY+\bar ZW$ on its box; open: the dependence with $A,B$ (and $Y,W$ on top of $A,B$, and $\nu$ on top of $A,B$). \emph{Later:} \autoref{ch:shared-sector-refinements} removes $A$ under the symmetry
$(Q,A)\overset d=(Q,iA)$ and $B$ under $\E[B\mid Q]=0$ at every amplitude; the coherent one-driver case is subsequently closed in \autoref{ch:complete-coherent-driver}. General coupled cubics remain outside these family theorems. With
$C=54959/40000$ and $\mathcal Q(v,x)$ the two-node bound at exact moments $m=2+x^2$, $M=6+9x^2+50x^3$, a sufficient coupled estimate is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescub9}
\Lambda_P(q+H)-\Lambda(q)\le C-\mathcal Q(v,x)+78\cdot10^{-6},
\end{equation}
$H$ the visible sectors and $\Lambda_P$ the first-chaos dual of \S\ref{sec:pinned-kprime}. Then $V\le(t-\ell)^2+(C-\ell^2+2\beta^2/\varepsilon)+78\cdot10^{-6}$, with
$C-\ell^2+2\beta^2/\varepsilon=33714462381/47275000000=0.7131580$, which is $\le0.713265624009<500/701$ for every $t\in[0.84,0.851]$ (exact arithmetic): the \emph{central-row budget} for $1.402$,
conditional on \eqref{eq:rescub9}; the other rows at $\varepsilon=0.7564$ and the envelope remain necessary (the $0.745$ rows cannot be substituted).

\subsection{The global diagonal theorem}
\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:rescub-diag}]
$Z\mapsto-Z$ in the second sign gives $\E[|L+Q+\nu H|+|L-Q-\nu H|]=\E[|L+Q+\nu H|+|L+Q-\nu H|]$, and $|w+y|+|w-y|\le2\sqrt{|w|^2+|y|^2}\le2|w|+|y|^2/|w|$ reduces the claim to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rescub10}
\sup_{s\in\C}\E_Z\frac{|H(Z)|^2}{|L(Z)+s|}<\frac{1311}{1000}.
\end{equation}
The push-forward of $|H|^2d\gamma$ under $Z\mapsto L$ has radial density $\rho(X(t))=t(t-2)^2e^{-t}/[2\pi(a+bt)(a+3bt)]$, $X(t)=\sqrt t(a+bt)$. Majorise $\rho$ by a radial nonincreasing
$\tilde\rho$: the convolution of two radial nonincreasing functions is maximal at the origin (write both as superpositions of centred disc indicators; the intersection area of two discs is
largest at zero displacement; truncate the locally integrable kernel $1/|z|$), so $\sup_s\int\rho(|z|)/|z+s|\,d^2z\le2\pi\int_0^\infty\tilde\rho(r)\,dr$. The certificate builds $\tilde\rho$ from
32,000 Arb upper bounds of $\pi\rho$ on $t\in[0,16]$ (step $1/2000$), each replaced by the maximum over all later intervals (a nonincreasing step function since $X$ is increasing); for $t\ge16$
the density itself is decreasing (its logarithmic derivative $1/t+2/(t-2)-1-b/(a+bt)-3b/(a+3bt)<0$ for $t\ge6$) with tail $\le e^{-16}(16^2+2\cdot16+2)/(8b)$. The outward-rounded sum is
$[1.31072839989\pm4.8\cdot10^{-26}]<1.311<1/\varepsilon$. Finally $\E|Q+\nu H|^2=\E|Q|^2+|\nu|^2$, and $1/\varepsilon-1311/1000=20899/1891000>0.011$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{IDE reading note}\label{sec:rescub-reading}
\paragraph{(a) The derivative signs.} Proved, not merely checked: by the Gaussian-mixture theorem for $L$ (GPT, \S\ref{sec:kfull-global}, complete monotonicity of $e^{-h}$; the same
induction as for the positive-Laplace profiles of \S\ref{sec:kweighted}) the nonnegative Laplace derivative formula in \eqref{eq:rescub4} proves that $f$ is a Bernstein function of $u$, so \emph{all} derivatives alternate; the Hermite
argument needs only $f''\le0$, $f'''\ge0$, $f^{(4)}\le0$. Our finite differences confirm $f'\ge0$, $f''\le0$ (the third and fourth differences of quadrature data are noise-dominated and
inconclusive, which is why the proof must be analytic). \textbf{(b) The two-node lemma} is correct: the cubic Hermite interpolant majorises $f_v$ globally because the remainder carries the sign of
$f^{(4)}\le0$; with $c_3\ge0$ and $c_2\le0$ the moment inequalities go the right way, and the two-point law $Y$ attains the bound, so the relaxed moment problem (first moment, second moment,
third-moment upper bound) is solved \emph{exactly} in closed form. \emph{Reconciliation with \autoref{ch:certs}, Task~B}: our dual LP there reported $\Lambda_q\le S+6.3\cdot10^{-5}$ at
$\sigma=0.2$, $x\to0$ (``$d<0$''). We re-checked: the Hermite cubic at the nodes $2\pm\sqrt2$ is dual-feasible for our $\Phi_0$ (minimum gap $-9\cdot10^{-9}$, finite-difference noise) with value
$S+9.2\cdot10^{-6}$ at $v=0.04$, and the exact two-node optimum over $v$ is $S+9.768\cdot10^{-6}$ at $x=0$ (GPT: $9.77\cdot10^{-6}$); so \textbf{the HiGHS solution of our LP was suboptimal by
$5.4\cdot10^{-5}$} (ill-scaled $v^6$ column), the $6.3\cdot10^{-5}$ was an artefact, and \autoref{ch:certs} is corrected accordingly. What survives of Task~B: the moment relaxation alone does
exceed $S$ near $\sigma^*$ (by $9.8\cdot10^{-6}$, the ``genuine loss $\approx10^{-5}$'' of the K report), so exact K-a is not provable from radial moments (\autoref{ch:spectral-review}), but
$K_\eta$ with $\eta\approx10^{-5}$ is, and this theorem certifies $\eta=2.2\cdot10^{-5}$ uniformly. Consistent with \autoref{ch:spectral-review}: the artificial laws exceed $S$ by
$<4.65\cdot10^{-6}\le2.2\cdot10^{-5}$. \emph{Our constant}: with the mathematically proved $C_6=40.45313$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:sixth-tensor-bridge-audit}) in place of $50$ the second maximum near $x=0.775$ disappears and the two-node supremum over all
