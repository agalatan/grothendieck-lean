\subsection{Enforce the known coordinate in the gate square}\label{sec:pinned-gate}
Notation of the shared-residual reduction (\autoref{ch:certified}, \eqref{eq:row}, and \autoref{ch:shared-residual-review}): for an admissible pair $f,g$ put $k=(f-g)/2$,
$c_1(w)=\E_zk(z,w)$, $p=Q_wc_1$, $\tau=\norm p_2$, $\xi=(c_1,c_A,\overline{c_B})$ and $X=Q_w\xi$. Then, by the definitions (HJM (8.6)),
\[
X_1=p .
\]
This is not an assumption that different extremisers agree; it is the first coordinate of $X$. The shared-residual certificate kept the common gate square but bounded it below by zero, letting
its first coordinate vary freely.
\begin{lemma}[\st{P}, checked exactly here]\label{lem:pinned}
For $N\succ0$, a vector $g$ and $X_1=p$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned1}
\norm{X+N^{-1}gp}_N^2\ \ge\ \frac{|1+(N^{-1}g)_1|^2}{(N^{-1})_{11}}\,|p|^2 ,
\end{equation}
with equality attainable under the single-coordinate constraint.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}With $Y=X+N^{-1}gp$, Cauchy--Schwarz in the $N$-inner product gives $|Y_1|^2\le(N^{-1})_{11}\norm Y_N^2$, and $Y_1=(1+(N^{-1}g)_1)p$. Equality holds for $Y=N^{-1}e_1\,Y_1/(N^{-1})_{11}$.\end{proof}
For a row mixture put $N=\sum_i\lambda_iN_i$, $g=\sum_i\lambda_i\delta_ib_i$, $\bar\delta=\sum_i\lambda_i\delta_i$. The old shared-gate price and its replacement are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned2}
D_{\rm old}=\bar\delta-g^*N^{-1}g,\qquad D_{\rm pin}=D_{\rm old}+\frac{|1+(N^{-1}g)_1|^2}{(N^{-1})_{11}}\ \ge D_{\rm old},
\end{equation}
and the strengthened mixed row is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned3}
V\le t^2-2\ell_\lambda t+\frac{2\beta_\lambda^2}\varepsilon+\sum_i\lambda_i\E\Psi_i(\delta_i\tau\sqrt U)-D_{\rm pin}\tau^2 .
\end{equation}
All potential bounds and all 402 boxes of the shared-residual certificate are reused; the price only increases, so the outer envelope stays valid. Orientation: for row $0.85$ the first
component of $N^{-1}g$ is $\approx-0.99625761$ and the correction $\approx1.538\cdot10^{-7}$ per unit $\tau^2$; for row $0.855$ it is $\approx-0.99979327$; the optimised profiles nearly satisfy
$1+(N^{-1}g)_1=0$, which is why the gain is small. Exact result \st{C}: 402 boxes pass, 1,788 outer cells pass, interior maximum $0.7134626228693718\ldots$, outer maximum
$0.7134617364535627\ldots$, cap $B=71346262287/10^{11}=0.71346262287$, $1/B=1.4016151203231426\ldots$; the verifier checks the old bounds first and checks \eqref{eq:pinned2} against the
independent block-Schur formula \eqref{eq:pinned4} in every box; the final rounding is integer arithmetic. As in \autoref{ch:shared-residual-review}, this does not follow from the
abstract $\forall i\,\exists q_i$ interface: the Gaussian reduction must expose the common $p$, the common $X$ and $X_1=p$, which follow from the definitions and must be proved, not obtained by
swapping quantifiers.

\subsection{Remove $\delta$ from the profile optimisation}\label{sec:pinned-delta}
For the real symmetric profiles write
\[
E=\begin{pmatrix}e&n^{\mathsf T}\\ n&C\end{pmatrix},\quad C\succ0,\qquad a=\delta(e_1+b)=\begin{pmatrix}a_0\\a_R\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The rescaled potential $\widetilde\Psi_a(s)=\Psi_\theta(\delta s)$ depends on $(E,R,\ell,\beta,a)$ and \emph{not separately on $\delta$}: its residual coupling is $2s\Re(a\cdot\xi)$. The corrected
price is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned4}
D_{\rm pin}=e-\varepsilon+2a_0-(n+a_R)^{\mathsf T}C^{-1}(n+a_R).
\end{equation}
\begin{proposition}[$\delta$-free row, \st{P}; identity checked exactly here]\label{prop:deltafree}
With $N=E-(\varepsilon-\delta)e_1e_1^*$ the pre-square gate inequality of \autoref{ch:certified},
$\varepsilon\norm{Qk}^2-\norm{Pk}^2\le\E(\xi^*E\xi+\zeta^*R\zeta+\varepsilon|d|^2)+\delta\norm p^2-\langle X,NX\rangle$, has, because $X_1=p$,
\[
\delta\norm p^2-\langle X,NX\rangle=\varepsilon\norm p^2-\langle X,EX\rangle ,
\]
so $\delta$ cancels; and with $X=(p,Y)$ (replacing $\xi$ by $X$ in the pairing with $p$ by projection orthogonality), completing the square in $Y$ only,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned5}
\varepsilon\norm p^2-\langle X,EX\rangle=2\Re\,\E\,\bar p\,(a\cdot\xi)-D_{\rm pin}\norm p^2-\norm{Y+C^{-1}(n+a_R)p}_C^2 ,
\end{equation}
which proves the row \eqref{eq:pinned3} directly.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}Expand: $\langle X,EX\rangle=e|p|^2+2\Re(\bar p\,n^{\mathsf T}Y)+Y^*CY$ and $2\Re\bar p(a\cdot X)=2a_0|p|^2+2\Re(\bar p\,a_R^{\mathsf T}Y)$; with $h=n+a_R$,
$\norm{Y+C^{-1}hp}_C^2=Y^*CY+2\Re(\bar p\,h^{\mathsf T}Y)+h^{\mathsf T}C^{-1}h|p|^2$. Substituting \eqref{eq:pinned4} the right side of \eqref{eq:pinned5} collapses to
$(\varepsilon-e)|p|^2-2\Re(\bar p\,n^{\mathsf T}Y)-Y^*CY$, which is the left side. (Verified on 200 random rational instances together with \eqref{eq:pinned1}--\eqref{eq:pinned2} and the
equality $D_{\rm pin}=D_{\rm old}+$gain.)\end{proof}
Consequences for the next row optimiser: search over $(E,R,a,\ell,\beta)$, 14 real parameters instead of 15, keeping the $E,R$ gate conditions; use $D_{\rm pin}$ and $\tau=\norm p$; no separate
$N\succ0$ gate is needed (the completion uses $C\succ0$); mixing is a $2\times2$ computation with retained slack $\sum_i\lambda_ih_i^{\mathsf T}C_i^{-1}h_i-\bar h^{\mathsf T}\bar C^{-1}\bar h\ge0$,
$h_i=n_i+a_{R,i}$; existing node caps stay usable after rescaling their abscissae $r\mapsto r/\delta_i$. The row is valid also when $D_{\rm pin}\le0$; positivity is needed only by the present
concave-quadratic box maximiser, and all 402 prices are checked positive. This is a reduction of the gate search, not a claim that it reaches $1.402$. (The same simplification appears as
``$A=\delta(e_1+b)$'' in \autoref{ch:shared-residual-review}.)

\subsection{Optimise the mixed potential against one law: a local certificate}\label{sec:pinned-local}
The shared-residual certificate adds separately certified bounds on $\E\Psi_i(\delta_i\tau\sqrt U)$, which may be supported by different worst laws even at common $(\tau,x)$. For the mixture
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned6}
\Phi_\theta(s)=(1-\theta)\Psi_{0.85}(\delta_{0.85}s)+\theta\Psi_{0.855}(\delta_{0.855}s)
\end{equation}
rescale the source cap tables to the $s$-variable and add them with weights; the breakpoint union gives a piecewise-affine upper envelope with the source padding added once (no new
Gaussian integration). New moment/Stein dual coefficients were optimised by LP and then \emph{certified} by the unchanged source verifier (rational Bernstein subdivision, exact Taylor tail):
three nodes at $\theta=14/125$, $\tau\in\{241/1000,\ 121/500,\ 243/1000\}$, with the global inequality $y_0+y_4v+y_1v^2+y_2v^4+y_3v^6-\widehat\Phi_\theta(\tau v)-\sum_j\mu_jG_j(v^2)\ge0$
($y_3,y_4,\mu_j\ge0$) for all $v\ge0$; the floating proposals needed upward repairs of $y_0$ by $1.73$, $2.85$, $2.69\cdot10^{-7}$, which are included. With the pinned price this gives the
exact \emph{local} result \st{C}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned7}
t\in[0.851,0.8512],\ \tau\in[0.241,0.243],\ x\in[0.69,0.71]\ \Longrightarrow\ V\le0.7134591683137567\ldots<0.713459169 ,
\end{equation}
a local gain of several $10^{-6}$. \textbf{Not a global bound}: the box does not cover the domain and its reciprocal must not be quoted. The global version of this idea, with three mixtures,
36 nodes and an 863-box partition, is \autoref{ch:shared-residual-review}'s $1.401616861$.

\subsection{A valid first-chaos dual for K$'$}\label{sec:pinned-kprime}
Two corrections to our own record first. (i) \autoref{ch:K} calls the first-chaos penalty experiment untested, but \code{kattack-2026-10-15/kprime.py} and \code{kprime.log} exist and
\S\ref{sec:kprime-result} reports them (raw penalty $16$--$31\%$ of the mixed-gain allowance on the junta family). (ii) \code{kprime.py} applies a $C/s$ tail extrapolation to all three profiles,
including $F_0(s)=\E_z\ph(L(z)+s)$, whereas $F_0(s)\to1$ as $s\to\infty$ (dominated convergence); the note's diagnostic evaluates the angular mean directly. The effect on the $16$--$31\%$
figures is not quantified \st{F}. (iii) The logical correction already made in \autoref{ch:crossbound}: subtracting $\norm{Pk_G}^2$ at the unpenalised best response does not bound the
penalised supremum, since the maximisers readjust.

\begin{proposition}[first-chaos dual, \st{P}]\label{prop:LambdaP}
With $\mathcal F(H)=\E|L+H|+\E|L-H|$ define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned8}
\Lambda_P(G)=\inf_{a\in\Hc10}\bigl\{\norm a^2+\mathcal F(G-a)\bigr\}-\frac{\norm G^2}\varepsilon .
\end{equation}
Then for the constrained functional $\Lambda'$ of \autoref{ch:K}: $\Lambda'(G)\le\Lambda_P(G)\le\Lambda(G)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}For every $a\in\Hc10$, $-\norm{Pk}^2\le\norm a^2-2\Re\langle a,k\rangle$. Inserting this into the row, the coefficients of $f,g$ become $L+G-a$ and $L-G+a$, whose pointwise suprema over
unimodular $f,g$ are the two moduli in \eqref{eq:pinned8}; infimise over $a$. Taking $a=0$ gives the second inequality. No exchange of $\sup$ and $\inf$ is used.\end{proof}
So $\sup_G\Lambda_P(G)\le S+\eta$ is an intermediate target sufficient for K$'$ and weaker than K. \emph{Relation to \autoref{ch:crossbound}}: $\Lambda_P$ is Proposition~\ref{prop:retained-dual}'s
dual $\mathcal D_G(v)$ restricted to first-chaos trial multipliers $v=a\in\operatorname{ran}P$ (then $Qv=0$ and $\mathcal D_G(a)=\mathcal F(G-a)+\norm a^2-\norm G^2/\varepsilon$); the two statements agree,
\autoref{ch:crossbound} being more general (cubic-consistency directions). The matrix bound \eqref{eq:pinned11} below is \autoref{ch:crossbound}'s first-chaos-only correction
$\Lambda'\le\Lambda-\langle Pk_0,(I+K_{PP})^{-1}Pk_0\rangle$. \emph{New} is the explicit $q$-only penalty \eqref{eq:pinned10}. (\autoref{ch:common-law-refinement}, \S\ref{sec:pinned-exact-dual}, later upgrades these duals to equalities in fixed finite dimension.)

\begin{theorem}[quantitative penalty for the $q$-only part, \st{P}]\label{thm:qpenalty}
Let $G=q(w)$ be independent of the visible coordinate, $L(z)=z(a+b|z|^2)$ with $a>0$, $b\ge0$, $F_0(u)=\E_z\ph(L(z)+u)$ and
\[
H_0=\E\frac1{|L|}=2\int_0^\infty\frac{e^{-r^2}}{a+br^2}\,dr\qquad\Bigl(=\frac{\pi e^{a/b}\operatorname{erfc}(\sqrt{a/b})}{\sqrt{ab}}\ \text{for }b>0\Bigr).
\]
Then for every square-integrable $q$, with the projection in the remote variables,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned10}
\Lambda_P(q)\le\Lambda_q-\frac{\norm{\Pc10F_0(q)}^2}{1+H_0/2}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}With $\rho=r(a+br^2)$ the planar density of $L$ is $\omega(\rho)=e^{-r^2}/[\pi(a+br^2)(a+3br^2)]$, radially nonincreasing. Hence $h(u)=\E|L+u|^{-1}$, its convolution with
$1/|u|$, is radial and nonincreasing (write both radial functions as positive superpositions of disc indicators; the intersection area of two discs decreases with their separation;
truncate to handle the singularity and the infinite support). Let $g(u)=\E|L+u|$: radial, convex, $\Delta g=h$, and
\[
\frac{g'(r)}r=\frac1{r^2}\int_0^rsh(s)\,ds\le\frac{H_0}2,\qquad g''(r)=h(r)-\frac{g'(r)}r\le\frac{h(r)}2\le\frac{H_0}2 ,
\]
which bound the tangential and radial Hessian eigenvalues; convexity makes them nonnegative, and the limit at $0$ is $H_0/2$. So $\nabla g=F_0$ is globally $H_0/2$-Lipschitz and
$2g(q-a)\le2g(q)-2\Re(\overline{F_0(q)}a)+\frac{H_0}2|a|^2$. Take $a=\Pc10F_0(q)/(1+H_0/2)$ in \eqref{eq:pinned8} and integrate.\end{proof}
At the peak profile ($a=0.5592$, $b=0.1432$) $H_0/2\approx1.4319737913$, so the guaranteed multiplier on the squared content is $\approx0.4112$ rather than $1$ \st{F}. For
$q=0.2\,w(|w|^2-2)/\sqrt2$ the note's one-parameter dual gives: raw squared first-chaos content $9.30685\cdot10^{-4}$, reduction guaranteed by \eqref{eq:pinned10} $3.82687\cdot10^{-4}$, reduction
from the one-parameter dual $4.18182\cdot10^{-4}$ (quadrature-stable to $8\cdot10^{-8}$) \st{F}. Read against \S\ref{sec:kprime-result}: the usable K$'$ relief is $0.41$--$0.45$ of the raw
penalty, i.e.\ roughly $7$--$14\%$ of the mixed-gain allowance on the junta family, not $16$--$31\%$.

\paragraph{Matrix version for general $G$ (\st{P}).} Put $w_\pm=L\pm G$, $k_0=(\ph w_+-\ph w_-)/2$, $c=Pk_0$, and on a finite-dimensional first-chaos subspace define $A_G$ by
$\langle a,A_Ga\rangle=\frac12\E|a|^2(|w_+|^{-1}+|w_-|^{-1})$. When the entries are finite and $w_\pm\ne0$ a.s.,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pinned11}
\Lambda_P(G)\le\Lambda(G)-c^*(I+A_G)^{-1}c ,
\end{equation}
by $|w-a|\le|w|-\Re(a\,\overline{\ph w})+|a|^2/(2|w|)$ (from $\sqrt s\le(1+s)/2$) applied to both moduli and minimisation of the positive quadratic. \eqref{eq:pinned10} is stronger in its
$q$-only setting because it uses the averaged Hessian; \eqref{eq:pinned8} remains valid when the reciprocal-modulus integrals diverge. The $q$-only penalty does not transfer unchanged to coupled $A/B$, even or diagonal sectors, and neither these measurements nor this dual proves the mixed-sector inequality. Later family theorems are listed in \autoref{ch:K-state}.
