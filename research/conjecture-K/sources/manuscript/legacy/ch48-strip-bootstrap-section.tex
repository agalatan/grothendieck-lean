\subsection{Wider global strips by retaining the directional A penalty}
\label{sec:milestone-global-strips}
\paragraph{Status \textup{[P,C]}.}
At the peak parameters, let \(Z,X,U\) be independent standard proper
complex Gaussians, and put
\[
G=cH(X)+gU+\alpha(|Z|^2-1)X+\beta Z^2\overline X/\sqrt2,
\qquad H(X)=X(|X|^2-2)/\sqrt2 .
\]
The new conclusions, valid for every \(c,g\ge0\) and every
\(\beta\in\mathbb C\), are
\[
\boxed{|\alpha|\le1/100\ \Longrightarrow\ \Lambda(G)\le S,}
\qquad
\boxed{|\alpha|\le1/84\ \Longrightarrow\ \Lambda(G)<S+1/12500.}
\]
They enlarge the incoming exact strip \(1/128\) and its slack strip
\(1/100\), respectively. No Grothendieck endpoint is asserted.

Let \(q=cH(X)+gU\), \(r=|\alpha|\), and let \(d_A,d_B\ge0\) denote
the independent-baseline contents. The accepted directional A cost
and actual-content transport from \autoref{ch:radial-directional-bootstrap} give
\[
\Lambda(q+\alpha(T-1)X)
\le\Lambda(q)+2d_A r-\frac{19}{100}r^2+10^{-11},
\qquad |d_B^A|\le d_B+\frac45r.
\]
A fresh scalar replay extends the shifted B cost \(7/20\) to
\(r\le1/80\), with error below \(10^{-12}\).
Completing the complex B square therefore gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:milestone-strip-envelope}
\Lambda(G)-S\le\Lambda(q)-S+2d_A r-\frac{19}{100}r^2+
\frac{20}{7}\left(d_B+\frac45r\right)^2+\frac{11}{10^{12}}.
\end{equation}
This uses the actual A-coupled content before transport. Dependence
of \(q\) on \(X\) is unrestricted. Since
\(-19/100+(20/7)(4/5)^2>0\), the collected right side is increasing
in \(r\ge0\).

\paragraph{The new shifted B-cost check.}
More generally let \(q,r_A,R\) be remote and independent of \(Z\),
with \(q\) square integrable and \(r_A,\overline R\) Gaussian linear
forms. They may share all their drivers. For \(\|r_A\|_2\le1/80\),
\[
\Lambda(q+(T-1)r_A+Z^2R/\sqrt2)
\le\Lambda(q+(T-1)r_A)+2\Re\mathcal B_A
-\frac7{20}\|R\|_2^2+10^{-12}.
\]
The error vanishes with a fresh buffer \(g\ge1/32768\).
For the accepted radial-shift proof, write \(k_0=\sqrt{3ab}\),
\[
K_j=\int_0^{20}\frac{t^je^{-t}}{\sqrt{1-\sqrt t/(80k_0)}}\,dt,
\quad C=\frac{953}{1000}\max(K_0,K_1),\quad
\delta=p-C-\frac7{20}.
\]
Outward integer cells of width \(1/1024\), with exact exponential
masses, give \(C<0.969639190321\), \(\delta>0\), and
\[
\frac{2[(\sqrt{20}+1/(2\sqrt{20}))^2+1]e^{-40}}{\delta}
<10^{-12},\qquad
C+21\cdot32768e^{-20}<p-\frac7{20}.
\]
Only the accepted scalar layer bound \(C_B<953/1000\) is inherited.

\paragraph{Continuum certificate and completion.}
Insert the incoming saturating positive-content bounds and its
nonperturbative Bernstein deficit into
\eqref{eq:milestone-strip-envelope}.
The new replay recomputes all scalar moments, positive Gaussian/Radau
quadrature bounds, transform tables and \(S-F(0)\).
It assumes no numerical value of \(S\) and reads no stored coefficients.
The \(1200\) cells in \(c\in[0,3/10]\) and \(512\) cells in
\(v=g^2\in[0,(41/250)^2]\) give \(614400\) parameter rectangles.
Outward-integer comparisons prove
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
r\text{ bound}&\text{computed maximum, display only}&
\text{certified conclusion}\\\hline
1/100&-7.222147640457037\cdot10^{-7}&\Lambda<S-1/2000000\\
1/84& 7.926772948363997\cdot10^{-5}&\Lambda<S+1/12500.
\end{array}
\]
The usual monotonic endpoint choices enclose each parameter cell,
and monotonicity in \(r\) covers the complete complex disks.
\autoref{ch:buffer-bootstrap} supplies exact K when \(g\ge41/250\).
\autoref{ch:zero-buffer-bootstrap} supplies a strict deficit when \(c\ge34/125\), hence when
\(c\ge3/10\). These two accepted continuation theorems complete the
global strips; they are not reproved in this package.
