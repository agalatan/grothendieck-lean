\subsection{The target and retained deficit}
Put
\[
 \epsilon=\frac{1891}{2500},\qquad p=\frac{2500}{1891},\qquad
 a=\frac{699}{1250},\qquad b=\frac{179}{1250},
\]
\[
 L=Z(a+b|Z|^2),\qquad H(X)=\frac{X(|X|^2-2)}{\sqrt2}.
\]
All named standard complex Gaussians are proper and have second moment
one. For independent $Z,X,U$, define
\[
 Q_0=cH(X)+\alpha(|Z|^2-1)X+
                         \beta Z^2\overline X/\sqrt2,
\]
\[
 \Lambda(G)=\mathbb E|L+G|+\mathbb E|L-G|-p\mathbb E|G|^2,
 \qquad
 S=\sup_{v\ge0}\{2\mathbb E|L+\sqrt vU|-pv\}.
\]
The complete comparison is
\[
 \boxed{\Lambda(Q_0+gU)\le S-\Delta(c,\alpha,\beta)
       \quad(c,g\ge0,\ \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb C),}
 \tag{OD}
\]
where
\[
 \boxed{\Delta(c,\alpha,\beta)=
 \min\left\{\frac3{100000},
 \frac{\min(c^4,c^2)}{32}
       +\frac{|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2}{2000}\right\}.}
\]
Thus every nonzero coherent Wick or A/B coefficient has a retained
deficit. The Gaussian-only family is intentionally permitted to attain
the benchmark. On the entire small-buffer half-line the
stronger conclusion is
\[
 0\le g\le\frac16
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \Lambda(Q_0+gU)<S-\frac3{100000}.
 \tag{SB}
\]
This model excludes additional even or diagonal terms and arbitrary
coupled cubic coefficient fields. A proper independent Gaussian buffer
is a distributional comparison variable; it need not itself lie in the
third chaos of the original Gaussian space.

\subsection{A global quadratic on every integration cell [P]}
Rotate out the phase of $Z$. Write $r=|Z|$, $X=x+iy$, and
\[
 \ell=ar+br^3,\qquad
 H=(x+iy)(x^2+y^2-2)/\sqrt2,\qquad
 A=(r^2-1)(x+iy),\qquad B=r^2(x-iy)/\sqrt2.
\]
The density is $2r e^{-r^2-x^2-y^2}/\pi$.
The simultaneous sign change $(X,U)\mapsto(-X,-U)$ gives
\[
 \Lambda(Q_0+gU)
 =2\mathbb E\rho_g(|\ell+cH+\alpha A+\beta B|^2)
       -p(c^2+g^2+|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2),
\]
where $\rho_g(u)=\mathbb E|\sqrt u+gU|$.
For $g>0$,
\[
 K_g(u)=2\rho_g'(u)
 =\frac1{g\sqrt\pi}\int_0^1 e^{-ut/g^2}
                         \sqrt{\frac{1-t}{t}}\,dt .
\]
Thus $\rho_g$ is concave. At $g=0$ this is square-root concavity.

On a cell $D_j$, let $\mathbb E_j$ denote its normalized Gaussian
expectation. Choose an anchor $(c_0,\alpha_0,0)$ and set
$q_j=\mathbb E_j|\ell+c_0H+\alpha_0A|^2>0$.
Jensen's inequality and the supporting line at $q_j$ imply
\[
 2\mathbb E_j\rho_g(|\ell+cH+\alpha A+\beta B|^2)
 \le2\rho_g(q_j)+K_g(q_j)
 \bigl(\mathbb E_j|\ell+cH+\alpha A+\beta B|^2-q_j\bigr).
 \tag{T}
\]
This is global in the target coefficients. It is not a local
Taylor approximation. All polynomial moments have degree at most six.

The bounded domain uses $256$ positive radial cells and $32$ cells
in each Cartesian coordinate, with all four sign choices:
$4\cdot256\cdot32^2=1,048,576$ cells on $[0,6]^3$.
The unequal resolutions reduce the visible-variable Jensen error
without increasing the cell count of the previous $64^3$ construction.

Let $D$ be the bounded domain, $P=\mathbb P(D^c)$,
$L_T=\mathbb E[\ell^2 1_{D^c}]$, and
$T_D=\mathbb E[(|H|^2+|A|^2+|B|^2)1_{D^c}]$.
Symmetry, Cauchy--Schwarz, and $2|w|\le1+|w|^2$ give
\[
 2\mathbb E[|\ell+cH+\alpha A+\beta B+gU|1_{D^c}]
 \le P+L_T+g^2P+(c^2+|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2)T_D.
\]
The full trace used here is exactly three. In particular no
unbounded-amplitude tail is discarded.

\subsection{Preserving phases and covering the continuum [P,C]}
Summing (T), its tail, and the exact price gives a Hermitian majorant
\[
 C+2\Re(b_Hc+b_A\alpha+b_B\beta)
 +(c,\alpha,\beta)^*M(c,\alpha,\beta).
\]
The code encloses complex off-diagonal entries. When
$k=-M_{BB}>0$, the complete B contribution is bounded by
\[
 \frac{|b_B+cM_{HB}+\overline\alpha M_{AB}|^2}{k}.
 \tag{Schur}
\]
This leaves a real quadratic in $c,\Re\alpha,\Im\alpha$.
Conjugation symmetry supplies reflected anchors and restricts the
cover to $\Im\alpha\ge0$ without removing any relative phase.

The direct buffer nodes are
\[
 0,\quad \frac1{25},\quad\frac2{25},\quad\frac3{25},
 \quad\frac{71}{500},\quad\frac{41}{250}.
\]
Thirty-one primary anchors and three supplemental anchors each
supply six matrices. Four additional matrices are computed directly

Every paired anchor at $g=.142,.164$ also gives a valid midpoint
quadratic by averaging and adding $p(.011)^2$ to its constant.

For fixed target coefficients, $\Lambda(Q_0+gU)+pg^2$ is convex in
$g$. Hence for $g=(1-u)g_0+ug_1$,
\[
 \Lambda(g)\le(1-u)\Lambda(g_0)+u\Lambda(g_1)
                    +pu(1-u)(g_1-g_0)^2.
 \tag{I}
\]
The checker maximizes this scalar quadratic exactly on $0\le u\le1$.
Different valid anchors may be used at the two endpoints.

The exact spatial cover recursively bisects
$[0,.19]\times[-.4,.4]\times[0,.4]$.
A box is discarded only when it does not intersect the A disk.
At each buffer node its cap is the minimum of valid quadratic
upper bounds. Multilinear interpolation is exact for cross terms;
negative pure quadratic curvatures cost at most

 \max(0,-q_{cc,\mathrm{low}})(\Delta c)^2+

    ((\Delta\Re\alpha)^2+(\Delta\Im\alpha)^2)\right].
\]
The coefficient units are $10^{-12}$ and spatial subdivision units
are $1/16000$. These are exact outward rational operations.
A failed box is subdivided; exhaustion of the subdivision resolution
