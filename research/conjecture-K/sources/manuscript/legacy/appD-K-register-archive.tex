\paragraph{Independent cold-start check (20 Oct).} We gave a second GPT instance only the bare statement of K, with none of this context. It independently re-derived the
sector decomposition, the Gaussian-scale-mixture form of $L$, and these results (Node exact-rational certificate, replayed by the IDE: PASS; archived in
\pathref{gpt-reviews/cold-start-20261020/}):
\begin{itemize}
\item K with margin, $\Lambda\le2\E|L|-0.128\E|G|^2$, for every $G$ vanishing on $\{z_1=0\}$, i.e.\ with no remote cubic and no $A$ sector;
\item the case $n=1$ on the whole box;
\item a dimension-free \emph{local} theorem: at the origin, independence maximises the second variation, $Q(G)\le\frac{I_0}2\|G\|^2$, for every $a>0$, $b\ge0$, including the coupled
$A/B$ block, via the $2\times2$ determinant $x(1-\tfrac78x)\ge0$. This is the $v\to0$ companion of the profile signs of \autoref{ch:gpt-averaged}.
\end{itemize}
It named as the gap exactly our crux: coupled components on top of a nonzero independent cubic. A second cold-start round (\code{conjecture-k-progress.zip}, both Node
verifiers replayed: PASS) proved:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{directional stability at the Gaussian optimum} for every $H\in\Hc21$, including $z_1$-dependent ones. With $A=\E[H\mid z_2,\dots]$ and $R=H-A$:
$\Lambda(X_t)-S\le-0.011t^2\|R\|^2+O_H(t^4)$ if $R\ne0$, and $=\frac{h''(v_*)}4(\E|H|^4-2\sigma^4)t^4<0$ if $R=0$. It uses a bound uniform over every smoothing variance,
$Q_v(H)\le h'(v)\|A\|^2+1.311\|R\|^2$. The neighbourhood depends on $H$;
\item \emph{A finite variance-preserving comparison for remote directions}:
for a unit circular remote $H$, independent of the visible variable and of
the Gaussian buffer, put $\kappa=\E|H|^4-2>0$ and $m_6=\E|H|^6$.
The comparison holds when the cubic variance fraction is at most
$6\kappa/(m_6+6\kappa)$. The particular number $576/16937$ applies to
$H=z[(|z|^2-2)+i(|w|^2-1)]/\sqrt3$ on remote coordinates, for which
$\kappa=64/3$ and $m_6=32722/9$; it is not a uniform fraction for all cubic
directions and does not cover shared visible drivers. (scope corrected by GPT, \autoref{ch:global-local-coupled-K});
\item an explicit, rigorously certified counterexample to Laplace domination: $H=z[(|z|^2-2)+i(|w|^2-1)]/\sqrt3$ has $\E e^{-30|H|^2}<1/31$. This is the same no-go as
\autoref{ch:stronger}, now with an exact certificate.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Independent cold-start check by a third model (Opus in a browser, bare statement only; bundle archived in \pathref{gpt-reviews/cold-start-20261020/opus-browser/}).}
It reproduced the sector reduction and the local block structure (worst block $-0.19$) with an Arb certificate, and reached the same no-go facts (covariance-only bounds stop at
$\eta\approx1.9\cdot10^{-3}$, via ring-phase laws; no pointwise-in-$r$ domination). Three items are new to us:
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Gaussianisation is not monotone, by a lot.} For $q=-c{:}z_2^2\bar z_2{:}$ with the self-interacting $A$ coefficient $r_A=c(2-\kappa')z_2$ plus Gaussian bulk, $\Lambda(G)-\Lambda(\hat G)$
reaches $+1.2\cdot10^{-2}$ (at $c=0.2$, $\kappa'=4.9$), with $\hat G$ the matched conditional Gaussian. That is the gap $D$ of \autoref{ch:polarized-profile-certificate}, while $\Lambda(G)\le S-7.5\cdot10^{-4}$
still holds. \textbf{This does not falsify the target} $D\le\alpha^2/10+\dots$. We checked the logged family: the worst ratio $D/(\alpha^2/10)$ is $0.29$ (at $c=0.2$, $\kappa'=4.9$,
$v=v^*$), so the certified $A$ penalty absorbs it with room. It does show that $D$ is not small, so any proof must use the $A$ cost.
\item \emph{A proposed route that is false.} It suggested the convex order $|A|^2\ge_{\rm cx}\|A\|^2\mathrm{Exp}(1)$ on pure $\Hc21$. The certified Laplace counterexample above ($\E e^{-30|H|^2}<1/31$) refutes it,
since convex order implies Laplace domination.
\item \emph{The benchmark degenerates in a corner of the row box.} Where $\E[1/(2|L|)]\le1/\varepsilon$, the optimal variance is $v^*=0$ and $S=2\E|L|$. This happens around
$\varepsilon\approx0.74$--$0.75$, $\ell\gtrsim0.85$, $\beta\approx0.12$--$0.13$; for example $(0.74,0.90,0.12)$ gives $1.244<1.351$. We checked it independently; at
$(0.74,0.86,0.13)$, $v^*$ is $3\cdot10^{-4}$. Arguments that assume $v^*>0$ must treat this corner separately.
\end{itemize}
