\subsection{Setting}
With $T\sim\mathrm{Exp}(1)$ independent of $X$, $\ell(T)=\sqrt T(a+bT)$ and $m(s)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}|1+\sqrt se^{i\theta}|\,d\theta$, the visible-phase average is exact
for $G=q+(T-1)r$:
\[
\Lambda(q,r)=2\E\bigl[\ell(T)\,m\bigl(|q+(T-1)r|^2/\ell(T)^2\bigr)\bigr]-p(u+w).
\]
Only orthogonality is used: $\E|q+(t-1)r|^2=u+(t-1)^2w$, because $q\in\Hc21$ is orthogonal to the first chaos.

\subsection{An unconditional bound}
The earlier coefficient $3/10$ follows from the sharper global theorem
\ref{thm:sharp-circular-audit}, proved below. Its two redundant polynomial
proofs have been removed; the historical integral bounds and strip
certificate are retained.

With $K=\E[1/\ell]\le\frac{5578637500}{3451269861}\sqrt\pi$ and $J=\E[(T-1)^2/\ell]\le\frac{991820528125}{875535147441}\sqrt\pi$ (from the elementary
$\int\frac{d\nu}{a+bt}\ge2\int f\,d\nu-\int(a+bt)f^2\,d\nu$ with an optimised affine $f$, all moments rational multiples of $\sqrt\pi$):
\[
\boxed{\Lambda(q,r)\le B_0+\bigl(\tfrac35K-p\bigr)u+\bigl(\tfrac35J-p\bigr)w\le B_0+\tfrac25u-\tfrac1{10}w.}
\]
Since $S>B_0+0.002$ (certified by monotone rectangles at $v=1/25$): $u\le0.00525+\frac w4\Rightarrow\Lambda<S+10^{-4}$, and $u\le0.005+\frac w4\Rightarrow\Lambda<S$.

\subsection{A full strip of the response-feasible rectangle}
Let $g(s)=\min\{1+\frac{3s}{10},\sqrt{1+s}\}\ge m(s)$. It is increasing and concave with $0\le g'\le\frac3{10}$. Conditional Jensen gives
$\Lambda\le V(u,w):=2\E[\ell\,g((u+(T-1)^2w)/\ell^2)]-p(u+w)$ and $\partial_wV\le\frac35J-p\le-\frac1{10}$. A fixed-cutoff upper bound on $V(u,0.024)$ is
certified increasing in $u$ on $[0,0.0463]$, with endpoint below $B_0+0.0019$. Hence
\[
\boxed{u\le0.0463,\quad w\ge0.024\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Lambda(q,r)<S-10^{-4},}
\]
for every coupled cubic. With the response-feasible ceiling $w<0.0328$ (\autoref{ch:K-state}), this covers $0.024\le w<0.0328$ entirely.

\subsection{A cumulant criterion}\label{sec:sar-cumulant}
With $\eta=(\E|q|^4-2u^2)/u^2$, the AI proves (Stein comparison, with the dependence of $q$ and $r$ controlled by
$\E\langle DQ,DR\rangle^2\le\frac w4\sqrt{3\kappa/8}$, where $Q=\Re q$, $R=\Re r$, $\kappa=\E|q|^4-2u^2$):
\[
\Lambda(q,r)\le S-\frac w{10}+\sqrt{\tfrac{\pi u}3}\sqrt\eta+\tfrac{8\sqrt\pi}{3e}\bigl(\tfrac38\bigr)^{1/4}\eta^{1/4}\sqrt w,
\]
so $\Lambda\le S+5\sqrt\eta$ for $u\le0.0463$. It proves the $10^{-4}$ target only for $\eta\le4\cdot10^{-10}$, i.e. essentially Gaussian $q$.

\subsection{A sharper unconditional region and an analytic majorant (second AI)}\label{sec:sar-second}
A second context-free AI given the same prompt (\code{gaussian-domination-partial-proof.zip}, archived in
\pathref{gpt-reviews/shared-a-regions-20261021/second-ai/}; Node certificate with only \code{node:assert} PASSES in 2\,s) proves, with sharper certified constants
$\E[1/\ell]<2.865$, $\E[(T-1)^2/\ell]<2.01$ and $S-2\E\ell>0.0020476$ (Jensen on finitely many radial and angular cells),
\[
\boxed{\Lambda(q,r)-S\le0.397v-0.116w-0.0020476,}
\]
so $v\le0.00541+0.292w\Rightarrow\Lambda\le S+10^{-4}$, and $v\le0.00515\Rightarrow\Lambda\le S$ for every $r$. It also gives the independent-Gaussian penalty $\partial_wF\le\frac35\E[(T-1)^2/\ell]-p<-0.116$ via
$\partial_sR(l,s)=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4\sqrt s}e^{-x}I_0(x)<\frac3{10l}$, and a cumulant criterion $\Lambda\le S+3\sqrt\delta$ ($v\le0.0463$, $\delta=\kappa/v^2$) of the same
practical scope as \S\ref{sec:sar-cumulant}. It has no strip result.

\subsection{Remaining shared-A rectangle}
Combining the sharper unconditional bound with the new strip, the remaining
part of $u\le0.0463$, $w\le0.0328$ is contained in
