\subsection{Setting and the target}
Use the peak parameters
\[
 p=\frac{2500}{1891},\quad
 a=\frac{699}{1250},\quad b=\frac{179}{1250},\quad
 L=Z(a+b|Z|^2),
\]
\[
 \Lambda(G)=\E|L+G|+\E|L-G|-p\E|G|^2,\qquad
 S=\sup_{v\ge0}\{2\E|L+\sqrt vU|-pv\}.
\]
Let \(Z,X,V_1,\ldots,V_m,U\) be independent proper standard complex
Gaussians. For an arbitrary
\(F\in H_{2,1}(Z,X,V_1,\ldots,V_m)\), put
\[
 Q=\E[F\mid Z,X],\qquad J=F-Q.
\]
The conditional projection preserves bidegree:
\(Q\in H_{2,1}(\C^2)\) and \(\E[J\mid Z,X]=0\).
Write
\[
 Q=cH(X)+A(T-1)X+\frac{BZ^2\bar X}{\sqrt2}
       +Zy(t-1)+\bar ZwX^2+\nu H(Z),
\]
where \(T=|Z|^2,\ t=|X|^2\) and
\(H(x)=x(|x|^2-2)/\sqrt2\).
Rotation of \(X\) makes \(c\ge0\), and conjugation permits
\(\Im A\ge0\). All other coefficients are unrestricted complex numbers.
The quantity controlled by the new certificate is
\[
 R^2=\|J\|_2^2+g^2,\qquad g\ge0,
\]
for the Gaussian-buffer extension \(F+gU\).
The fresh \(U\) is independent of the original coordinates.
The case \(g=0\) is included.

In an adapted coordinate system \(z_1=Z,z_2=X\), write
\[
 F=\sum_{i,j,k}\tau_{ij,k}:z_i z_j\bar z_k:,\qquad
 \tau_{ij,k}=\tau_{ji,k}.
\]
The Wick isometry gives the directly computable criterion
\[
 \|J\|_2^2=2\sum_{(i,j,k)\notin\{1,2\}^3}|\tau_{ij,k}|^2.
\]
The two holomorphic indices are both summed as ordered indices.

\subsection{Certified neighborhood and deficit [P,C]}
Put \(r=\|J\|_2^2+g^2\). The completed certificate proves
\[
\boxed{\sqrt r\le\frac{31}{1000}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Lambda(F+gU)<S-\frac1{50000}.}
\tag{54.0}
\]
This raises the general conditional-complement radius from
\(7/2000=0.0035\) to \(0.031\): a factor \(62/7\) in norm and
\(3844/49>78\) in allowed variance.
No independence of \(J\) from the retained coordinates is assumed.
The six coefficients of the core are unrestricted.

In particular \(1/15000<\eta_{AB}\).

A useful quantitative consequence is
\[
\boxed{\Lambda(F+gU)<
S-\frac{11}{8000}+\frac{1355}{961}r
\quad\left(0\le r\le\frac{961}{1000000}\right).}
\tag{54.0a}
\]
Indeed the certificate upper function is convex in \(r\):
each box/vertex bound is affine plus a nonnegative multiple of
\(\max(0,b_0+14r/5)\), and the outside-profile bound is constant.
All exclusions remain valid below the largest checked radius.
The chord of the two certified endpoint bounds has slope
\[
\frac{11/8000-1/50000}{961/1000000}
=\frac{1355}{961},
\]
so the boxed affine bound gives exactly the deficit $1/50000$ at $r=961/10^6$. (The package text rounded the
slope up to $141/100$, which alone gives only $1999/10^8$ there; the endpoint certificate (54.0) is unaffected.)
This is a statement about the explicit certificate function.
Convexity of the actual variance-constrained optimum is not assumed.

\paragraph{A genuinely mixed zero-buffer example.}
For any \(Q\in H_{2,1}(Z,X)\), take
\[
F=Q+\frac{X^2\bar V}{40\sqrt2}
       +\frac{(|Z|^2-1)V}{100}
       +\frac{Z^2\bar V}{100\sqrt2}.
\]
The three added terms are orthogonal, have total variance
\(33/40000\), and have conditional mean zero given \(Z,X\).
Consequently
\[
\Lambda(F)<S-\frac{11}{40000}.
\]
Here the purely mixed estimate below is used.
All three coefficient phases may vary arbitrarily.
The term \(X^2\bar V\) couples a retained driver to an external one.

\paragraph{Purely mixed improvement.}
If also \(\E[J\mid V_1,\ldots,V_m]=0\), and \(g=0\), then
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|J\|_2\le 4/125&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Lambda(F)<S-1/100000,\\
\|J\|_2\le 33/1000&\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Lambda(F)<S+1/12500.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here \(1/12500<\eta_{AB}\). Both conditional-centering conditions
are needed; a purely external cubic or fresh buffer is not included
in this improved branch.
The quantitative version, for \(\|J\|_2\le4/125\), is
\[
\Lambda(F)<S-\frac{11}{8000}+\frac43\|J\|_2^2.
\]
Indeed the endpoint chord slope is \(1365/1024<4/3\).
The displayed mixed example has \(\E[J\mid V]=0\), so this
gives its stated deficit.

\subsection{The tangent is taken at the A-exact core [P]}
Put
\[
 Q_A=cH(X)+A(T-1)X,\qquad D=Q-Q_A,
\]
and choose the exact binary rational used by the existing integrator:
\[
 k=\frac{7378697629483821}{18446744073709551616}
       \ge\frac1{2500}.
\]
Define
\[
 \omega=\frac12\left(
 \frac1{\sqrt{|L+Q_A|^2+k}}+
 \frac1{\sqrt{|L-Q_A|^2+k}}\right).
\]
It is invariant under simultaneous phase rotation of \(Z,X\)
and is even under \(X\mapsto-X\).
The elementary tangent inequality
\[
 |f+h|\le\sqrt{|f|^2+k}
 +\frac{2\Re(\bar f h)+|h|^2-k}{2\sqrt{|f|^2+k}}
\]
applied to both norm signs shows that the actual-law upper bound is
the existing regularized core tangent plus
\[
 \E[\omega|J|^2]-p\|J\|_2^2
       +g^2(\E\omega-p)-k\E\omega.
 \tag{54.1}
\]
There are no cross terms between \(J\) and \(D,L,Q_A\):
the coefficients are measurable in \(Z,X\) and
\(\E[J\mid Z,X]=0\).
Cross terms involving \(U\) vanish by its independence and centering.
The subtraction \(-k\E\omega\) is essential.
It is absent when one bounds a regularized target; here the target is
the original unregularized norm.

For the two core norm signs, the B--even quadratic terms and
the even-sector linear terms cancel by \(X\mapsto-X\).
This is exactly the parity calculation used by the old slab certificate.
It remains valid in the presence of \(J\), because its additional
terms have already been removed by conditional expectation.

\subsection{The five finite Gram blocks [P]}
Expand \(J\) in the normalized complex Hermite basis of the external
coordinates:
\[
 J=\sum_{\mu,\nu:\,|\mu|+|\nu|>0}
       f_{\mu,\nu}(Z,X)e_{\mu,\nu}(V).
\]
If \(|\mu|=r,\ |\nu|=s\), then
\(f_{\mu,\nu}\in H_{2-r,1-s}(\C^2)\).
The possible local spaces are precisely
\[
 H_{1,1},\quad H_{2,0},\quad H_{1,0},\quad
 H_{0,1},\quad H_{0,0}.
 \tag{54.2}
\]
Orthogonality over \(V\), at each fixed \((Z,X)\), proves
\[
 \E[\omega|J|^2]
 =\sum_{\mu,\nu}\E[\omega|f_{\mu,\nu}|^2].
 \tag{54.3}
\]
This step is exact in every finite external dimension.
It does not assert that \(J\) is independent of \(Z\) or \(X\).

\paragraph{Any finite core [P].}
For a retained Gaussian subspace of complex dimension \(d\), the same
identity holds with local spaces
\(H_{1,1}(\C^d),H_{2,0}(\C^d),H_{1,0}(\C^d),H_{0,1}(\C^d),H_{0,0}\).
Their dimensions are \(d^2,d(d+1)/2,d,d,1\).
For any bounded nonnegative core-measurable weight, its largest
Gram eigenvalue over these five spaces bounds
\(\E[\omega|F-\E(F\mid\mathrm{core})|^2]\) divided by the
conditional-complement variance. No symmetry of the weight is needed.
Thus a visible coordinate and two remote drivers require matrices
of sizes \(9,6,3,3,1\), regardless of the external dimension.
This is a proved extension mechanism; no numerical certificate
for that larger core is asserted here.

\paragraph{Sharpness of this reduction [P].}
The maximum Gram eigenvalue is the exact optimal constant for
the weighted second-moment inequality, already with one external
coordinate \(V\). Multiply an eigenvector in a maximizing local space
by the corresponding normalized external polynomial
\[
 V,\quad\bar V,\quad |V|^2-1,\quad V^2/\sqrt2,\quad H(V).
\]
The complementary bidegrees make each product a cubic of bidegree
\((2,1)\), its conditional expectation on the core is zero, and
its weighted Rayleigh quotient is exactly the local eigenvalue.
This is sharpness of the weighted norm step, not an assertion
that these products maximize the original nonlinear payoff.

\paragraph{Other pure bidegrees [P].}
For \(F\in H_{p,q}\), \(p+q>0\), the identical proof takes the
maximum over
\[
 H_{i,j}(\C^d),\quad
 0\le i\le p,\quad 0\le j\le q,\quad (i,j)\ne(p,q).
\]
The dimensions are
\(\binom{d+i-1}{i}\binom{d+j-1}{j}\).
A single external Hermite polynomial of complementary bidegree
proves sharpness. In particular, pure \(H_{3,2}\) with a
two-coordinate core gives eleven blocks, of maximum size nine.
This algebraic extension is not a numerical certificate for
a higher-degree witness.

Set
\[
 M_{ij}=\E[\omega T^it^j]\quad(i+j\le2),\qquad
 \zeta=\E[\omega\bar Z^2X^2].
 \tag{54.4}
\]
There are six real radial moments and one complex harmonic.
In the normalized \(H_{1,1}\) basis
\((T-1,t-1,Z\bar X,\bar ZX)\), its Gram matrix splits as
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}
 M_{20}-2M_{10}+M_{00}&M_{11}-M_{10}-M_{01}+M_{00}\\
 M_{11}-M_{10}-M_{01}+M_{00}&M_{02}-2M_{01}+M_{00}
 \end{pmatrix}
 \ \oplus\
 \begin{pmatrix}M_{11}&\zeta\\\bar\zeta&M_{11}\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{54.5}
\]
In \(H_{2,0}\), the even basis \(Z^2/\sqrt2,X^2/\sqrt2\)
has matrix
\[
 \frac12\begin{pmatrix}M_{20}&\zeta\\\bar\zeta&M_{02}\end{pmatrix},
 \tag{54.6}
\]
and the odd basis vector \(ZX\) contributes \(M_{11}\).
Both linear spaces have matrix \(\operatorname{diag}(M_{10},M_{01})\);
the constant space contributes \(M_{00}\).
The exact symbolic checker verifies all \(34\) entries and their
unweighted Gaussian normalization.

Let \(K\) bound all these largest eigenvalues.
Then (54.3) gives
\[
 \E[\omega|J|^2]\le K\|J\|_2^2,\qquad M_{00}\le K.
 \tag{54.7}
\]
Thus (54.1), uniformly for \(\|J\|_2^2+g^2\le R^2\), is at most
\[
 (K-p)_+R^2-kM_{00}.
 \tag{54.8}
\]
The positive part is required: the constraint is a ball,
not a fixed variance sphere.
The universal bound \(K\le50\) is still valid, but the new
directed integrals give much smaller bounds near the binding cells.

\paragraph{Purely mixed remainders [P].}
If also \(\E[J\mid V_1,\ldots,V_m]=0\), then the constant local
block is absent. The largest eigenvalue of the other four blocks
suffices. This restricted version permits no fresh buffer \(gU\).
It excludes both core-only and purely external terms, while allowing
arbitrary cubic terms mixing the two sets of coordinates.
The separate \texttt{mixed} check uses precisely this smaller operator.

\subsection{How the eight moments are integrated [P,C]}
After removing the visible phase, let \(Z=\sqrt T\),
\(X=\sqrt t\,e^{i\psi}\), and put
\[
 \ell=\sqrt T(a+bT),\qquad
 m=c(t-2)/\sqrt2+(T-1)A.
\]
The one-sign denominator is
\[
 \sqrt{|\ell+\sqrt t\,m e^{i\psi}|^2+k}.
\]
For even angular test functions, averaging this denominator gives
the same expectation as the paired weight \(\omega\).
The previously audited arithmetic--geometric mean formula provides
\[
 J_0=\E_\psi\frac1{\sqrt{|\ell+\sqrt t\,m e^{i\psi}|^2+k}},
\]
and the second Fourier coefficient is
\[
 \E_\psi\frac{e^{2i\psi}}
 {\sqrt{|\ell+\sqrt t\,m e^{i\psi}|^2+k}}
 =\left(\frac{\bar m}{|m|}\right)^2 J_2.
 \tag{54.9}
\]
At \(m=0\) the coefficient is zero.
The six \(M_{ij}\) integrate \(T^it^jJ_0\);
\(\zeta\) integrates \(Tt\) times (54.9).

The scalar second harmonic can be checked without an additional
special-function identity. For \(G^2=p_0+q_0\cos\theta\),
put \(j_1=\E[\cos\theta/G]/q_0\) and
\(J_2=\E[\cos(2\theta)/G]\).
Integrating \((\sin\theta\,G)'\) gives
\[
0=p_0q_0j_1+\frac{q_0}{4}J_0+\frac{3q_0}{4}J_2,
\qquad J_2=-\frac{J_0+4p_0j_1}{3}.
\]
At \(q_0=0\), use the continuous limit.
Similarly \(\E[(\sin\theta\,G^{-3})']=0\) gives
\[
\E G^{-5}=\frac{4p_0\E G^{-3}-\E G^{-1}}
 {3(p_0^2-q_0^2)}.
\]
The code uses \(p_0=d+q_0\), so the denominator is \(3d(d+2q_0)\).

Use radial variables \(u=\sqrt T,\ v=\sqrt t\), with density
\(4uv e^{-u^2-v^2}\), on \([0,6]^2\).
Each cell uses the smaller of a full interval enclosure and a midpoint
rule with an explicit second-derivative remainder.
The derivatives are bounded directly in the angular representation;
the angular numerator has modulus at most one.
Consequently derivatives of a chosen phase branch are never estimated
separately near \(m=0\).
The same audited geometry and inverse-denominator estimates are reused
from the old sector integrator.

All new polynomial factors are dominated by the old tail majorant
\[
 \frac4{\sqrt k}(1+u)^6(1+v)^4
       (2ue^{-u^2})(2ve^{-v^2}).
\]
Its explicit integrable tail bound covers the complement of
\([0,6]^2\). There is no omitted Gaussian tail.
All final moment endpoints are widened outward.
The adaptive choices use floating scores only to choose a partition;
the final interval sums and error enclosures are the proof data.

The proposed numerical \(K\) is rounded up to a rational grid
of spacing \(10^{-8}\). The independent Python checker then
verifies its validity using only rational diagonal and determinant
inequalities for the \(2\times2\) matrices.
It does not trust a floating eigenvalue calculation.
Every record is bound to the source hashes of the integrator.

\subsection{The enlarged profile budget and the continuum cover [P,C]}
Let \(r=\|J\|_2^2+g^2\le R^2\).
The exact radial variance profile is the same five-profile expression
as in the existing full-cubic bound. The remote constant-profile
energy is at most \(c^2+R^2\); all other new profile energies
are nonnegative.
This remains true for the independent buffer, whose variance simply
adds to the constant profile.

If total variance is at least \(13/20\), the existing profile cutoff
already supplies a stronger bound.
The affine exclusion also applies when
\[
 |A|^2\ge\frac1{12}+\frac{14}{5}(c^2+R^2).
 \tag{54.10}
\]
In the remaining domain,
\[
 |B|^2+|y|^2+2|w|^2+|\nu|^2
 \le\frac1{12}+\frac{14}{5}(c^2+R^2)-|A|^2.
 \tag{54.11}
\]
Indeed the full affine threshold is
\(V<1/12+(19/5)u\).
Subtract the core \(c^2+|A|^2\), and use the fact that the
remote part of \(J\) has variance at most its total variance \(r\).
This yields the coefficient \(19/5-1=14/5\).

For a parameter box \(\mathcal B\) in
\((c,\Re A,\Im A)\), fix
\[
 B_{\mathcal B}(R^2)=
 \max\left\{0,\frac1{12}+\frac{14}{5}
          (c_{\max}^2+R^2)-\min_{\mathcal B}|A|^2\right\}.
\]
At each vertex \(v\), let \(C_v,G_v,\lambda_v\) be the existing
upper bounds for the regularized core, completed B square, and
nonnegative even-sector cost. Let \(K_v\) and \(m_v\) be the new
operator upper bound and \(M_{00}\) lower bound.
The lifted vertex upper bound is
\[
 C_v+G_v+\lambda_v B_{\mathcal B}(R^2)
              +(K_v-p)_+R^2-km_v.
 \tag{54.12}
\]
The feasible sets for the even coefficients and for \(J,g\) are
fixed throughout the box.
For each fixed choice, adding \(p(c^2+|A|^2)\) to the actual
payoff makes it convex in these three real parameters.
Taking the supremum preserves convexity.
If \(\rho_{\mathcal B}\) is the box circumradius, the continuum bound is
\[
 \max_{v\text{ a vertex of }\mathcal B}\!
 \left(C_v+G_v+\lambda_v B_{\mathcal B}(R^2)
              +(K_v-p)_+R^2-km_v\right)+p\rho_{\mathcal B}^2.
 \tag{54.13}
\]
The radius budget is therefore checked in the full continuum cover,
not merely at individual anchors.

The exact checker validates the root grid, every subdivision,

\subsection{Scope and next obstruction}
The result allows, for example, terms proportional to
\(X^2\bar V\), \(ZX\bar V\), \(V(|X|^2-1)\), and
\(\bar ZXV\), as long as the combined conditional-complement norm
satisfies the certified bound.
These terms are not independent blocks relative to the core.
All six core sectors remain unrestricted.

The norm condition is absolute and must not be reported as a
fraction of total variance.
The theorem does not control a second influential block of comparable
size to the first, nor establish the full K or a new endpoint.
Its contribution is to complete the eight-moment computation left open
in \autoref{ch:two-coordinate-slab-gluing} and enlarge the rigorously controlled coupled neighborhood.
