\subsection{The block-addition theorem [P,C]}
Let \(Z,X,U\) be independent proper standard complex Gaussians and put
\[
T=|Z|^2,\quad a=\frac{699}{1250},\quad b=\frac{179}{1250},
\quad p=\frac{2500}{1891},\quad L=Z(a+bT),
\]
\[
H(X)=\frac{X(|X|^2-2)}{\sqrt2},\qquad
\Lambda(G)=\mathbb E|L+G|+\mathbb E|L-G|-p\mathbb E|G|^2,
\]
\[
S=\sup_{v\ge0}\{2\mathbb E|L+\sqrt vU|-pv\},\qquad
Q_0=cH(X)+\alpha(T-1)X+\frac{\beta Z^2\bar X}{\sqrt2}.
\]
Let \(q,A,B,Y,W\) be square-integrable, centered, pairwise
Hermitian-orthogonal random variables, jointly independent of \((Z,X,U)\).
There is no assumption of independence or properness within this five-tuple.
Define
\[
J=q+(T-1)A+\frac{Z^2B}{\sqrt2}+ZY+\bar ZW,
\]
\[
x=\mathbb E|q|^2,\quad y=\mathbb E|A|^2,\quad
z=\mathbb E|B|^2,\quad e=\mathbb E|Y|^2+\mathbb E|W|^2.
\]
Then, for
\[
c\ge0,\qquad 0\le g\le\frac{41}{250},
\qquad \alpha,\beta\in\mathbb C,
\]
we have
\[
\boxed{\Lambda(Q_0+J+gU)
<S-\frac3{100000}
+\frac{38}{25}x+\frac{33}{50}y-\frac{13}{25}z-\frac9{100}e.}
\tag{B}
\]
In fact the absolute constant \(8587/6250=1.37392\) can replace
\(S-3/100000\).

For the Gaussian-chaos application the external fields may all be functions
of the same independent Gaussian block \(V\), with respective bidegrees
\[
(2,1),\quad(1,0),\quad(0,1),\quad(1,1),\quad(2,0).
\]
Distinct bidegrees ensure precisely the orthogonality assumed above.
The dimension of \(V\) and the dependence within its fields are unrestricted.

\subsection{Conditional variance is enough for the lifting [P]}
Conditioning on \((Z,X)\), centering and Hermitian orthogonality give
\[
\mathbb E[J\mid Z,X]=0,\qquad
\mathbb E[|J|^2\mid Z,X]
=x+y(T-1)^2+zT^2/2+eT.
\tag{V}
\]
No assumption is made on \(\mathbb E[J^2\mid Z,X]\). In particular the
conditional law can be improper.

Let \(\rho_g(u)=\mathbb E|\sqrt u+gU|\) and \(K_g=2\rho_g'\).
The function \(\rho_g\) is concave. At \(g=0\), it is \(\sqrt u\).
For \(g>0\),
\[
K_g(u)=\frac1{g\sqrt\pi}\int_0^1
e^{-ut/g^2}\sqrt{\frac{1-t}{t}}\,dt.
\]
Fix a bounded core cell \(D\) and its positive anchor mean \(q_D\).
The tangent used by the coherent-core certificate gives
\[
\begin{split}
2\mathbb E_D\rho_g(|F+J|^2)
\le{}&2\rho_g(q_D)
+K_g(q_D)(\mathbb E_D|F|^2-q_D)\\
&+K_g(q_D)\mathbb E_D\mathbb E[|J|^2\mid Z,X].
\end{split}
\tag{T}
\]
This is global in the target amplitudes, with no Taylor remainder.
The cross term with \(J\) vanishes by conditional centering.

On the spatial tail use the same global inequality as in the original
cell certificate, \(2|w|\le1+|w|^2\). The added tail cost is exactly
\[
x\mathbb P(D^c)+y\mathbb E[(T-1)^2 1_{D^c}]
+z\mathbb E[T^2 1_{D^c}/2]+e\mathbb E[T1_{D^c}].
\]
The original core tail bound is left intact. Finally the price subtracts
\(p(x+y+z+e)\), since the core and added block are orthogonal.

Thus, with the piecewise weight
\[
w=K_g(q_D)\text{ on each bounded cell},\qquad w=1\text{ on the tail},
\]
the additional costs are
\[
W_0=\mathbb Ew-p,\quad W_A=\mathbb E[w(T-1)^2]-p,\quad
W_B=\mathbb E[wT^2/2]-p,\quad W_E=\mathbb E[wT]-p.
\tag{W}
\]

\paragraph{Both norm signs and all phases.}
The even fields do not change sign upon negating their underlying Gaussian
coordinates. Accordingly, apply (T) separately to the two norms and reflect
only the coherent core variable \(X\) for the second norm.
The reflected core cell means agree, and (V) is unchanged. Each one-norm
tangent contributes half of (W), giving exactly the asserted total.
No sign symmetry of the external joint law is needed.

If \(Z=\sqrt T e^{i\theta}\), rotate \(X\) to \(e^{-i\theta}X\) and the norm
argument by \(e^{-i\theta}\). The five external coefficients become
\[
e^{-i\theta}q,\quad e^{-i\theta}A,\quad e^{i\theta}B,\quad
Y,\quad e^{-2i\theta}W.
\]
Conditionally on \(\theta\), their means and Hermitian orthogonality persist,
and their variances do not change. The rotated \(X\) is still independent of
that conditional external law. This justifies using the core engine without
assuming phase invariance of arbitrary external fields.

\subsection{Remaining obstruction and replay}
The result proves K for an internally coupled external block when its
variance costs are paid by the core deficit and the negative B/even terms.
It includes non-Gaussian self-interaction on a second coordinate and all
relative phases.

General two-driver K remains \textbf{[?]}. A second cubic with insufficient
B/even energy has a positive cost in this argument. Cubic monomials mixing
the coherent coordinate \(X\) and external coordinates are excluded by the
independence hypothesis. A diagonal term \(\nu H(Z)\) is not appended.
The new bound holds for all \(c\) at \(g\le41/250\), and additionally for
\(c^2+g^2\ge(24/25)^2\) at every buffer. The separate all-buffer coherent-core
theorem does not, by itself, transfer these signed costs through the remaining
buffer region.
