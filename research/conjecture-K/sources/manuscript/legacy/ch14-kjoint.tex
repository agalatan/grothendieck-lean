\subsection{The three theorems}\label{sec:kjoint-thms}
\begin{theorem}[shared Wick junta with simultaneous $A$ and $B$, \st{P} GPT, integer replay]\label{thm:kjoint-junta}
For $Q=cH(X)+\alpha(T-1)X+\beta Z^2\bar X/\sqrt2+gG$ with $c,g\ge0$ and arbitrary $\alpha,\beta\in\C$ (and, by conditioning, jointly random coefficients $(C,\mathcal A,\mathcal B,V_0)$
independent of $(Z,X,G)$ with finite second moment, buffer $\sqrt{V_0}G$):
\[
\Lambda(Q)\le S+10^{-4}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Proof architecture: remove the visible phase and condition on the two radii $T=|Z|^2$, $R=|X|^2$; the residual is the ellipse $me^{i\psi}+ne^{-i\psi}$ with
$m=\sqrt R[c(R-2)/\sqrt2+\alpha(T-1)]$, $n=\beta T\sqrt R/\sqrt2$ (the $B$ term has the opposite $Z$-frequency, \S\ref{sec:freqrule}). The \emph{ellipse phase theorem}
$J(x;m,n,g)\le J(x;|m|,-|n|,g)$ (perpendicular major axis maximises the Gaussian-smoothed mean norm, including $g=0$) removes the relative phase and leaves five real parameters
$(c,\Re\alpha,\Im\alpha,|\beta|,g)$; the exact envelope is jointly convex in them (a supremum of expectations of norms of affine functions), so vertex interpolation with the
allowance $\sum h_j^2/(4\varepsilon)$ is valid; positive cubic Bernstein integration in $r=\sqrt T$, $s=\sqrt R$ on five grids (up to $256\times128$ nonuniform radial cells, 64 angular);
one Rice evaluation per angular cell by conditional Jensen (concavity of $F_w(u)=\E|\sqrt u+\sqrt wG|$ in $u$); parameter tail $V=c^2+|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2+g^2\ge1$ by
$\Lambda\le2\sqrt{M+V}-V/\varepsilon<4/3$, radial tail $r,s\le6$ costing $2^{-23}$; all arithmetic in signed 128-bit integers on a $2^{-46}$ lattice, floating values used only as
guesses that are corrected by integer inequalities. Cover: root box $[0,1]\times[-1,1]\times[0,1]^3$, \textbf{26,446 boxes, 79,159 vertices, 1,508 analytic tail boxes, 27,953 splits};
largest accepted upper $96690404106435/2^{46}$; internal Gaussian benchmark lower bound $B=96683367401312/2^{46}=1.3739532875\ldots\le S$ (our float $S=1.37395353$); the
difference is exactly $7036705123/2^{46}=0.99998\cdot10^{-4}$. \emph{So the certified slack is the whole $\eta$ budget of \autoref{ch:attemptK}}, and it is not the truth: on this family
the true margin $S-\Lambda$ is positive everywhere tested (J1 scan, \S\ref{sec:kjoint-j1}); the slack is intrinsic to the phase relaxation (\S\ref{sec:kjoint-obstr}).
Not covered: several junta coordinates, even sectors, coefficients depending on the drivers, other profiles (only the exact scaling ray $\lambda L$, $\lambda\varepsilon$).

\begin{theorem}[joint $A{+}B$ on one Gaussian factor with buffer, arbitrary phases, \st{P} GPT, binary64 interval replay]\label{thm:kjoint-complex}
Let $(S_0,\alpha,\beta,g)$ be jointly random (complex, complex, complex, $g\ge0$), independent of $(X,Z,G)$, with $\E(|S_0|^2+|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2+g^2)<\infty$ and
\[
\E\Re(S_0\bar\alpha)=0,\qquad\E\Re(S_0\beta)=0 .
\]
Then $Q=\{S_0+\alpha(T-1)\}X+\beta Z^2\bar X/\sqrt2+gG$ satisfies $\Lambda(Q)\le S+1/12500=S+8\cdot10^{-5}$.
\end{theorem}
Deterministic $\alpha,\beta$ with centered $S_0$ satisfy both cancellations automatically; the buffer may be correlated with the coefficients; neither the relative phase of
$\alpha,\beta$ nor $\alpha\beta$ is restricted. Mechanism: conditionally on the remote state the residual is an \emph{improper} Gaussian with variance $v=|A|^2+|B|^2+z^2$ and
pseudocovariance $C=2AB$ ($A=r+\alpha'(T-1)$, $B=\beta'T/\sqrt2$ after the gauge $S_0=r\ge0$); the pointwise dual adds $\frac75ra_r+\frac57rb_r$ (which average to zero under the
two hypotheses) and is covered over six real parameters $(r,z,a_r,a_i,b_r,b_i)$ on $[0,13/4]^2\times[-13/4,13/4]\times[0,13/4]\times[-13/4,13/4]^2$ with a positive
log-Laplace Rice oracle, four nonuniform Bernstein partitions of $[0,8]$ (52--144 cells), and the Cauchy--Schwarz shortcut $\sqrt{\ell^2+v}$ for $\ell\ge12$. Cover:
\textbf{236,786 boxes, 898,217 vertices, 1,027,305 norm calls, 132 tail boxes}; largest accepted upper $1.374033275968777$ below the cap $1.374033284570177=B'+1/12500$
with the separately verified benchmark lower bound $B'=96683367193980/2^{46}=1.373953284570177$. \emph{Vector corollary}: for $Q=\sum_jX_j\{S_j+\alpha_j(T-1)\}+\frac{Z^2}{\sqrt2}\sum_j\beta_j\bar X_j+gG$
in any dimension with $\E\Re\sum S_j\bar\alpha_j=0$, $\E\Re\sum S_j\beta_j=0$ and \textbf{collinear directions} $\alpha=\kappa\bar\beta$, the same bound: a unitary $U\beta=\|\beta\|e_1$
puts both sectors on the first driver and the orthogonal proper residual is conditionally exactly a buffer $z_0G_0$, $z_0^2=\|S\|^2-|s|^2+g^2$.

\begin{theorem}[$B$ on any conditionally proper Gaussian-factor residual, with deficit, \st{P} GPT, binary64 interval replay]\label{thm:kjoint-B}
In any dimension, for $(S,\beta,g)$ jointly random and independent of the drivers, finite second moment, $\E\Re\sum_jS_j\beta_j=0$:
\[
Q=\sum_jS_jX_j+\frac{Z^2}{\sqrt2}\sum_j\beta_j\bar X_j+gG\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\Lambda(Q)\le S+10^{-5}-\frac{\E\|\beta\|^2}{200}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Four real parameters after the reduction of Theorem~\ref{thm:kjoint-complex} with $\alpha=0$; bonus $|\beta|^2/200$ added to the left side of the pointwise dual, tail $V\ge9/4$
exact. Cover: \textbf{24,967 boxes, 58,879 vertices, 62,987 norm calls, 1 tail box}; largest upper $1.373963280269784$ below the cap $1.373963284570177$. It strengthens (ii) of
\code{junta-and-b} (one driver, no buffer) to arbitrary dimension with a proper buffer and the same deficit $1/200$. The residual must be conditionally \emph{proper}: an
antiholomorphic part $\sum_jP_j\bar X_j$ on the same drivers (present in a general $q_0\in\Hc21$) is outside the theorem because it cannot be absorbed into the buffer.

\paragraph{Which hypotheses are restrictive.} (1) \emph{Independence of the remote state from the drivers} (all three theorems): in a general cubic the coefficient $S_j$ of
$X_j$ is a chaos element of the \emph{other} coordinates $X_k$, which are themselves drivers of other terms; the theorems cover ``driver coordinates $\sqcup$ coefficient
coordinates'' and the one-coordinate self-interaction $cH(X)$, not the coupled case. (2) \emph{Mean cancellation} $\E\Re(S_0\bar\alpha)=\E\Re(S_0\beta)=0$: automatic for
the chaos problem (deterministic $\alpha,\beta\in\Hc10$-coefficients, centered $S$), so not restrictive for K; it is what makes the content \emph{bilinear} and killable by a
supporting multiplier. (3) \emph{Collinearity} $\alpha=\kappa\bar\beta$ in dimension $\ge2$: genuinely restrictive; noncollinear $A{+}B$ is the open eight-parameter target below.
(4) \emph{Conditionally proper residual} (no $\sum P_j\bar X_j$): restrictive for general $q_0$. (5) \emph{No even sectors, no $\nu$, one profile}: as before.
(6) Theorem~\ref{thm:kjoint-junta} spends the full $10^{-4}$ budget by itself.

\paragraph{Comparison with \code{junta-and-b} (iii).} That statement needed $\alpha^{\top}\beta=0$, $\E\Re\langle S_0,\alpha\rangle=0$ and a conditional phase-spread hypothesis, and
gave deficits $\|\alpha\|^2/200+281\|\beta\|^2/400$. Theorem~\ref{thm:kjoint-complex} drops $\alpha^{\top}\beta=0$ and the phase spread entirely in one driver (and in any dimension for
collinear directions) but returns only a slack $+8\cdot10^{-5}$, no deficit; (iii) covered the orthogonal case $\alpha^{\top}\beta=0$ in any dimension under phase spread. The two
are incomparable in general dimension; for the one-driver and collinear cases the new theorem is the right one and (iii) should no longer be quoted as the joint statement.

\subsection{Two obstructions}\label{sec:kjoint-obstr}
\paragraph{The phase relaxation has intrinsic positive slack (\st{P} GPT, exact identities replayed).} The junta proof maximises the relative phase of the $B$ term separately at
each radial pair $(T,R)$, which replaces the honest Wick amplitude $c(R-2)/\sqrt2$ in the mixed term by $c|R-2|/\sqrt2$. Expanding around the Gaussian optimum ($\alpha=0$,
$g^2=w_*>0$, $w_*$ interior because $\varepsilon\sqrt\pi>2a+b$) to second order in $(c,\beta)$: the pure $c^2$ term vanishes by stationarity, and
$\Lambda_{\max}(c,0,\beta,\sqrt{w_*})-S=Kc\beta+B\beta^2+o(c^2+\beta^2)$ with $K=-16e^{-2}\E_T[Tu(T)F''_{w_*}(u(T))]>0$ (because $F''<0$ and $\frac12\E[R|R-2|]=4e^{-2}$),
whereas the honest mixed moment is $\frac12\E[R(R-2)]=0$. Along $\beta=tc$ the relaxed envelope exceeds $S$ at order $c^2$. \emph{Consequence}: no refinement of this verifier
proves exact K ($\eta=0$) on the junta; it can only prove $K_\eta$ with $\eta$ at least the relaxation gap, and a zero-slack proof must keep the Wick--$B$ phase coupling. The
mechanism is not specific to the profile (it occurs for $L=aZ$) and is not a counterexample to K (the J1 scan shows positive honest margins).

\paragraph{The two faces cannot be merged by remote centering alone (\st{P} GPT; identity verified here \st{C}).} For $Q_c=cH(X)+S_0X+g(S_0)G$ with real $S_0$,
$\E S_0=0$, $V=S_0^2+g^2$:
\[
\E|Q_c|^2=\E V+c^2,\qquad
\E|Q_c|^4-2(\E|Q_c|^2)^2=2\Var(V)+4\sqrt2\,c\,\E S_0^3+20c^2\E S_0^2+42c^4 .
\]
We re-derived the underlying deterministic identity $\E|Q|^4=2V^2+4\sqrt2cS^3+c^2(4V+20S^2)+32\sqrt2c^3S+44c^4$ exactly (own polynomial expansion over $\mathbb Q[\sqrt2,i]$ with
Gaussian moments, \code{kjoint/mine/cumulant\_exact.py}, difference $0$), and checked the two-point case by Monte Carlo ($4\cdot10^6$ samples, common random numbers:
$\kappa=-2.07\cdot10^{-5}$ vs claimed $-2.12\cdot10^{-5}$ at $c=0.002$; $-3.89$ vs $-3.91\cdot10^{-5}$ at $c=0.004$). With the skew two-point law $S_0\in\{r,-2r\}$ (prob.\ $2/3,1/3$),
$g^2=w_*-S_0^2$, every conditional variance equals $w_*$, so $\E J(0)=S$ exactly, and $\partial_c\E J(0)=-2\sqrt2r^3f''(w_*)>0$ (GPT: $0.00645$ at $r=0.499\sqrt{w_*}$; our MC
slope $0.0058\pm0.0005$, sign confirmed \st{C}); the convex tangent gives $\E J(c)\ge S+Ac-c^2/\varepsilon>S$, excess $\varepsilon A^2/4\approx7.9\cdot10^{-6}$ \st{F}. Since
$\kappa(c)=-8\sqrt2cr^3+40c^2r^2+42c^4<0$ for small $c$, the construction is \emph{not} cubic chaos and does not refute K or $K_{10^{-4}}$; it refutes any bridge of the form
``centered remote coefficient + arbitrary correlated proper buffer'' and any $g$-independent multiplier $\lambda(c)\Re S_0$. A merger of the Wick face and the Gaussian-factor
face must retain chaos restrictions (fourth cumulant $\ge0$), or fix the buffer and use the necessary multiplier $\partial_c\lambda(0,g)=-\sqrt2(w_*-g^2)f''(w_*)$.

\subsection{Analytic by-products}
\emph{Power phase ordering} (\st{P}): for $J_p(x;m,n,g)=\E|x+me^{i\Psi}+ne^{-i\Psi}+gG|^p$ the perpendicular orientation maximises all moments $1\le p\le2$ and the parallel one all
$p\ge2$ (equality at $p=2$); an exact example ($x=1/2$, axes $1,1/2$, no buffer) shows that neither squared radius dominates the other in convex order, so the ordering must not
be promoted to a stop-loss statement. \emph{Eight-parameter target} (\st{P}, exact algebra): in any dimension the proper Gaussian-factor problem with simultaneous $A,B$ depends only
on $t=\|S\|^2+g^2$, $r=\|\alpha\|^2$, $s=\|\beta\|^2$, $p=\Re\langle S,\alpha\rangle$, $k=\sum S_j\beta_j$, $\ell=\sum\alpha_j\beta_j$ (variance $t+2p(T-1)+r(T-1)^2+sT^2/2$,
pseudocovariance $\sqrt2T(k+\ell(T-1))$), feasible iff $t_\perp\ge0$, $r_\perp\ge0$, $p_\perp^2\le t_\perp r_\perp$ (Schur complement of the $3\times3$ Gram cone), realised by two drivers
$(S_1+\alpha_1A)X_1+\sqrt sB\bar X_1+(x+dA)X_2+hG$; Theorem~\ref{thm:kjoint-complex} is the face $d=0$; the compact tail $V\ge10$ is dimension-free. Replacing the orthogonal factor
$(x+dA)X_2$ by a constant buffer is \emph{not} valid (\code{IMPROPER\_A\_TRANSFER\_OBSTRUCTION.md}).

\subsubsection{The global theorem (\st{P} GPT; algebra re-derived here; numerically tested \st{C})}\label{sec:kfull-global}
\begin{theorem}[fixed-buffer norm comparison]\label{thm:kfull}
Let $L\overset d=\sqrt{R_0}\,G_0$ be a proper Gaussian variance mixture (every profile $Z(a+b|Z|^2)$, $a>0$, $b\ge0$, is one), $X,G$ independent standard proper Gaussians,
$S$ an independent centered complex variable with $\E|S|^2=v<\infty$, $g\ge0$ deterministic, $c\in\R$, $k=c^2+v>0$, $f(w)=2\E|L+\sqrt wG|=\sqrt\pi\,\E\sqrt{R_0+w}$. Then
\[\begin{gathered}
2\E|L+cH(X)+SX+gG|\le f(g^2+k)-c^2D(k,g),\\
 D(k,g)=\frac{\sqrt\pi k}{16}\E\frac{4(R_0+g^2)+k}{(R_0+g^2+k)^{5/2}}\ \ge\ \frac{\sqrt\pi k}{16(M+g^2+k)^{3/2}},\quad M=\E|L|^2,
\end{gathered}\]
hence $\Lambda(cH(X)+SX+gG)\le S-c^2D(k,g)$ for every $\varepsilon$, every amplitude, every dimension and law of $S$, no fourth moment. Corollary: for
$Q=cH(X)+SX+T\bar X+CX^2/\sqrt2+gG$ with $(S,T,C)$ arbitrarily dependent, only $\E S=0$, $k=c^2+\E|S|^2+\E|T|^2+\E|C|^2$:
$\Lambda(Q)\le S-\sqrt\pi k(c^2+2\E|C|^2)/[16(M+g^2+k)^{3/2}]$. Conditional version: $c,g$ $\mathcal F$-measurable, $\E[S\mid\mathcal F]=0$.
\end{theorem}
\emph{Proof, checked step by step.} (i) With $X=\sqrt Re^{i\varphi}$, circularity of $L+gG$ gives $2\E|L+Q|=\E_{R,S}P_g\bigl(R|c(R-2)/\sqrt2+S|^2\bigr)$, $P_g(u)=2\E|L+\sqrt u+gG|$.
(ii) $P_g$ is concave in $u$ because, conditionally on $R_0$, it is the Rice mean $F_{R_0+g^2}(u)$, concave by the Laplace representation (this is exactly the ``concavity in the squared
radius'' of \S\ref{sec:why}, item~1, with the visible variable itself supplying the Gaussian smoothing, which is why the mixture property of $L$ is essential: for a general circular $L$ and
$g=0$ the step fails). Jensen over $S$ at fixed $R$ gives $\le\E_RP_g(R[v+c^2(R-2)^2/2])$ since $\E S=0$ kills the cross term; equality holds iff $|c(R-2)/\sqrt2+S|^2$ is a.s.\
constant given $R$, i.e.\ for $S=\pm i\sqrt v$: the imaginary two-point law is the exact worst centered law at fixed variance (real two-point laws are strictly worse for the adversary).
(iii) $h(t)=\E_RP_g(R[k+t((R-2)^2/2-1)])$ is concave on $[0,k]$ ($U_t\ge0$ affine), $h(0)=f(g^2+k)$, $h(c^2)$ is the bound of (ii), and by exponential integration by parts
$I_{j+1}=(j+1)I_j+kI_j'$ ($I_j=\E[R^jP_g'(kR)]$, $I_1=F'$): $h'(0^+)=\frac12I_3-2I_2+I_1=kf''(g^2+k)+\frac12k^2f'''(g^2+k)=-D(k,g)$ (we re-derived (13)--(15) by hand; the signs and
the factor $16$ are right). The tangent at $0$ bounds the concave $h$. (iv) Domination: $0\le P_g'(u)\le u^{-1/2}$, $|P_g''|\le u^{-3/2}/2$, $|P_g'''|\le\frac34u^{-5/2}$, uniform in the
buffer, justify $h'(0^+)$ and the boundary terms also at $g=0$. (v) For the corollary the three $X$-frequencies $1,-1,2$ of $SX$, $T\bar X$, $CX^2$ are orthogonal in the phase average, so
the conditional mean square is $R|c(R-2)/\sqrt2+S|^2+R|T|^2+R^2|C|^2/2$; a two-parameter concave interpolation with $u$-derivative $\frac k2f''=-E_C$ gives the extra deficit. \emph{We find
no gap.} The one hypothesis that carries everything is the Gaussian-mixture structure of $L$ (complete monotonicity of $v\mapsto e^{-H^{-1}(v)}$, $H(u)=u(a+bu)^2$, proved by a
derivative induction in \code{GAUSSIAN\_MIXTURE\_CORE.md}; our numerical second differences of $u\mapsto2\E|L+\sqrt u|$ at $g=0$ are all $\le0$ \st{C}).

for which the Gaussian integration by parts of Lemma~\ref{lem:ibp}, the coefficient map $\mathcal B_q$ and the OU-resolvent identities apply at each $s$ with explicit derivatives
($h(\rho)=e^{-s\rho}$, $h'=-se^{-s\rho}$, \dots). This is the natural exact-profile tool for (T$_0$): it replaces sup-norm bounds on $F_{uu\bar u}$ by $\mu_D$-weighted bounds on
$s^k e^{-s\rho}$, i.e.\ exactly the ``law-weighted third derivative'' that \S\ref{sec:wct-remains} identified as the single loss; the cubic-model constants (Theorem~\ref{thm:W}) are recovered

the radial variable and the Malliavin fluctuations, and warns that replacing that joint law by a Gaussian radial average is not justified.
