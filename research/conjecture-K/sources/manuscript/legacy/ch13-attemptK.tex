\subsection{The natural proof strategy, and why it fails}
After the coordinate reduction (\S\ref{sec:coordred}) the shared-driver $A$-sector is a problem in two explicit coordinates: $r_A=c\,w_2$ and, conditional on the other
coordinates $w''$, the residual is a polynomial of degree $\le3$ in $(w_2,\bar w_2)$ whose coefficients are chaos elements of $w''$. This suggests an induction over
coordinates: \emph{peel} one coordinate at a time by replacing its contribution to the residual by an independent Gaussian of the same conditional variance.
\begin{definition}[peeling lemma P, candidate]
Let the residual depend on one remote Gaussian $w$ through a polynomial with random coefficients independent of $(Z,w)$, plus visible mixed terms in $w$. Then the two-sign payoff
does not decrease when the $w$-dependence is replaced by an independent Gaussian driver with the same conditional variance profile.
\end{definition}
If P held, every $G\in\Hc21(\C^n)$ would be dominated by a Gaussian variance mixture, and Jensen (concavity of the Gaussian benchmark in the variance) would give K.
GPT's theorems are all of this equal-variance type, so P is the natural conjecture. \textbf{P is false.} Test (\code{peel.py}): $G_{\rm sh}=\sigma(aH(w_2)+\sqrt{1-a^2}W)+\alpha(T-1)w_2$
(shared driver) against $G_{\rm ind}=\sigma(aH(w_2)+\sqrt{1-a^2}W)+\alpha(T-1)Z'$ ($Z'$ independent; same conditional variance profile $\sigma^2(1-a^2)+|\alpha|^2(T-1)^2$ around the same
junta core), both with $W,Z'$ integrated in closed form. Over $a\in\{0.3,0.6,1\}$, $\sigma\in\{0.14,0.2,0.3\}$, $|\alpha|\in\{0.05,0.15,0.3\}$ and eight phases:
$\Lambda(G_{\rm sh})-\Lambda(G_{\rm ind})$ ranges from $-3.6\cdot10^{-2}$ (phase $\pi$) to $+1.96\cdot10^{-2}$ (phase $0$, $a=1$, $\sigma=0.3$, $|\alpha|=0.3$); at phase $\pm\pi/2$ it is
$\approx-10^{-4}$. So sharing the driver \emph{helps} the adversary by a large amount when $r_A$ is phase-aligned with the junta, and the equal-variance Gaussianisation is not an
upper bound. (K itself holds at every one of the 216 points: the independent-driver value is already $10^{-2}$--$8\cdot10^{-2}$ below $S$ and absorbs the gain.) Consequently
\emph{no proof of K can proceed sector by sector or coordinate by coordinate at equal variance}; the content gain of a sector must be weighed against the deficit of the residual it
couples to. This confirms lower-GPT's current direction (a direct comparison keeping content and deficit together).

\subsection{Why the content is third order: the influence-matrix picture}
Both sides are controlled by the fluctuation of the carré du champ. With $U=|q|^2=\sum_kU_k$ (chaos parts), $W-\sigma^2=\frac23U_2+\frac13U_4$ and $U_2=\sum_{k,l}M_{kl}{:}\bar w_kw_l{:}$
with $M=\Gamma+\Gamma'^{\top}$ the \emph{influence matrix} ($\Gamma_{kl}=\E[\partial_kq\,\overline{\partial_lq}]$). The deficit's leading term is the pairing of the fluctuation with $U$
itself, $\mathrm{Cov}(W,U)=\frac23\|U_2\|^2+\frac13\|U_4\|^2>0$ (the fourth-moment inequality in Stein form), and the Stein cuts bound the whole law deviation by
$\Var W+\frac12\E|C|^2\le\kappa/3$. The content's leading term is the pairing of the same fluctuation with $\bar w_jq$: $\langle w_j,U_2q\rangle=2\sum_{k,l}t_{jkl}M_{kl}=2(t\cdot M)_j$,
the tensor contracted with the influence matrix (this is the $B_j$ term of \autoref{ch:L2}). A Cauchy--Schwarz with the full norm of $t$ gives $\sum_j|(t\cdot M)_j|^2\le\frac{\sigma^2}2\|M\|_{HS}^2
\lesssim\sigma^6x^2$: second order in $x$, the Theorem-4 regime. The truth is third order because $M$ is supported on the influential directions and the mass of $t$ on those directions is
itself controlled by $M$ ($\Gamma_{kk}$ is the weight of $t$ on direction $k$): on the junta family $M\sim a^2$ on one direction where $t\sim a$, so $t\cdot M\sim a^3\sim x^{3/2}$. A proof of
the content inequality at the right order is therefore a \emph{weighted} tensor inequality (weights $\Gamma_{kk}$), of which L2 is the pure-cubic prototype; its constant is
dimension-dependent (\autoref{ch:L2}), which is the warning sign for any constant-chasing approach. The saturation of the bounded profiles handles $x\gtrsim0.5$ and is a separate,
finite-dimensional estimate.

\subsection{Verdict}
K was not proved. Established tonight: the coordinate-by-coordinate strategy is dead (P false, with a large explicit counterexample at aligned phase); the problem is
irreducibly a joint statement about content and deficit of the same residual; on the junta family the mixed gain is at most $16\%$ of the $q$-only deficit, so the conjecture has
ample room; and the leading-order mechanism of the content is the contraction of the chaos tensor with its own influence matrix, a weighted inequality whose sharp constant
(L2) grows with the dimension. The route that remains open and sufficient is the content inequality above with the true (Stein-cut) deficit, or GPT's direct comparison.
