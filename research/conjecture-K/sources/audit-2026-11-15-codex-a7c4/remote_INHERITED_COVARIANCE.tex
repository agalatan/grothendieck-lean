\paragraph{The extra universal input \st{P}.}
Use $U,X,r,P,Q,\chi$ from the separate-budget proof, so
$P=\E[XU]$, $Q=\E[rU]$ and $2P+Q=\chi$.
For a proper unit complex third-chaos polynomial $q=I_3(f)+iI_3(g)$,
where real symmetric cubic tensors satisfy
$\|f\|^2=\|g\|^2=1/12$ and $\langle f,g\rangle=0$, put
\[
 K_{ij;kl}=\sum_a f_{ija}g_{kla},\qquad K^{\mathsf T}_{ij;kl}=K_{kl;ij}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:covariance-order-20261022}
 P-Q=648\|K-K^{\mathsf T}\|^2,\qquad
 \frac\chi3-Q=432\|K-K^{\mathsf T}\|^2\ge0.
\end{equation}
Here is a finite-sum derivation. Write $A=\|f\otimes_1g\|^2$,
$B_2=\|f\otimes_2g\|^2$ and $D=\langle K,K^{\mathsf T}\rangle$.
The twenty placements of the first cubic tensor among six slots have
intersection multiplicities $1,9,9,1$. Hence
\[
 6!\|\operatorname{Sym}(f\otimes g)\|^2
 =36(\|f\|^2\|g\|^2+9A+9B_2+\langle f,g\rangle^2),
 \qquad \|\operatorname{Sym}(f\otimes_2g)\|^2=(B_2+D)/2.
\]
The degree-two Wick-product coefficient is
$\binom32^2 2!=18$. If $F=I_3(f)$ and $H=I_3(g)$, the OU product rule
on $FH$ therefore gives
\[
 \E F^2H^2-\E[FH\Gamma(F,H)]
 =36\|f\|^2\|g\|^2-36\langle f,g\rangle^2+324(A-D).
\]
Polynomial Gaussian integration by parts and the definitions give
$P-Q=4\{\E F^2H^2-\E[FH\Gamma(F,H)]\}-(\E F^2+\E H^2)^2$.
The scalar terms cancel by proper normalization; since
$\|K-K^{\mathsf T}\|^2=2(A-D)$, this proves
\eqref{eq:covariance-order-20261022} in arbitrary finite dimension.

\[

\[
