## 2. Mathematical Proof Chain (`P`)

### 2.1 Minimax Linearized Joint-Kantorovich Bound (`JM1`)

**Theorem 2.1.** *Let $q \in \mathcal{H}_{2,1}(\mathbb{C}^n)$ have energy $u = \|q\|_2^2 > 0$, fourth cumulant $\delta(q) = \chi u^2 > 0$ with $\chi \in (0, \chi_{\mathrm{hi}}]$, and directional derivative energies $d_1(e) = \|\partial_e q\|_2^2$, $d_2(e) = \|\bar{\partial}_e q\|_2^2$ along any unit vector $e \in \mathbb{C}^n$. For any bounded measurable $h : [0, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$, any $\beta \ge 0$, $\gamma > 0$, and $\lambda_K \in \mathbb{R}$,*
$$\frac{\|c[h](q)\|_2^2}{\delta(q)} \le \left(\frac{\beta^2}{3} + \lambda_K\right)u + M_{\Lambda(\chi_{\mathrm{hi}})}\!\left(\frac{\beta^2}{3} + \lambda_K,\; \frac{\beta^2}{4\gamma} + \lambda_K\right) + \sup_{\|e\|_2 = 1} \mathbb{E}\Bigl[\tau_e H_{\beta,\gamma,\lambda_K}(|q(Z)|^2)\Bigr],$$
*where $\tau_e := |\langle Z, e\rangle|^2 \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1)$, $V = |q(Z)|^2$,*
$$H_{\beta,\gamma,\lambda_K}(v) := \left(\frac{3}{2} + \gamma\right) v k'(v)^2 - \beta v k'(v) - \lambda_K v,$$
*and for any $(c_1, c_2) \in \mathbb{R}^2$,*
$$M_{\Lambda(\chi)}(c_1, c_2) := \max_{\substack{0 \le d_1 \le 2 u,\; 0 \le d_2 \le u \\ d_1^2 + d_2^2 \le \frac{\chi}{2} u^2}} (c_1 d_1 + c_2 d_2) \le \min\!\Bigl(2 u (c_1)_+ + u (c_2)_+,\; u\sqrt{\tfrac{\chi}{2}}\sqrt{(c_1)_+^2 + (c_2)_+^2}\Bigr)$$
*(sharpened further on $2 < \chi < 10$ by splitting $(c_1)_+ = p_1 + q_1$ or $(c_2)_+ = p_2 + q_2$ with $p_i \ge 0$, $q_i \ge 0$).*

*Proof.* Choose a unit vector $e \in \mathbb{C}^n$ with $\|c[h](q)\|_2 = |\langle c[h](q), e\rangle|$. By the joint first-chaos content inequality $M_1(e)$ ([CONTENT_LEMMA_D5.md](../degree5-capped-2026-11-16/CONTENT_LEMMA_D5.md) §11.6),
$$\frac{|\langle c[h](q), e\rangle|^2}{\delta(q)} \le \frac{3}{2}\mathbb{E}[\tau_e V k'(V)^2] - \frac{3}{4(u + d_1)}\bigl|\mathbb{E}[\tau_e V k'(V)] - \mathbb{E}[\overline{\bar{\partial}_e q}\,y_e q k'(V)]\bigr|^2.$$
For every $\xi \ge 0$ and $\zeta > 0$, combining $-|A - B|^2 \le \xi^2 - 2\xi A + 2\xi|B|$ ($A = \mathbb{E}[\tau_e V k'(V)] \in \mathbb{R}$) with Cauchy–Schwarz and AM–GM $|B| \le \sqrt{d_2}\sqrt{\mathbb{E}[\tau_e V k'(V)^2]} \le \frac{\sqrt{d_2}}{2\zeta} + \frac{\zeta\sqrt{d_2}}{2}\mathbb{E}[\tau_e V k'(V)^2]$ gives
$$\frac{|\langle c[h](q), e\rangle|^2}{\delta(q)} \le \frac{3\xi^2}{4(u + d_1)} + \frac{3\xi}{4\zeta(u + d_1)}\sqrt{d_2} + \mathbb{E}\!\left[\tau_e\left(\left(\frac{3}{2} + \frac{3\xi\zeta\sqrt{d_2}}{4(u + d_1)}\right)V k'(V)^2 - \frac{3\xi}{2(u + d_1)} V k'(V)\right)\right].$$
Substituting $\xi = \frac{2(u + d_1)}{3}\beta$ and $\zeta = \frac{2\gamma}{\beta\sqrt{d_2}}$ (when $\beta > 0$ and $d_2 > 0$; by continuity at $\beta = 0$ or $d_2 \to 0^+$, where the $d_2$-term vanishes) gives:
- $\frac{3\xi}{2(u + d_1)} = \beta$,
- $\frac{3\xi\zeta\sqrt{d_2}}{4(u + d_1)} = \gamma$,
- $\frac{3\xi^2}{4(u + d_1)} = \frac{\beta^2}{3}(u + d_1)$,
- $\frac{3\xi}{4\zeta(u + d_1)} \sqrt{d_2} = \frac{\beta^2}{4\gamma} d_2$.
Adding and subtracting $\lambda_K \mathbb{E}[\tau_e V] = \lambda_K(u + d_1 + d_2)$ (since $y_e q = y_e \diamond q + \bar{\partial}_e q$ gives $\mathbb{E}[\tau_e |q|^2] = \|y_e \diamond q\|_2^2 + \|\bar{\partial}_e q\|_2^2 = u + d_1(e) + d_2(e)$ on $\mathcal{H}_{2,1}(\mathbb{C}^n)$) yields
$$\frac{|\langle c[h](q), e\rangle|^2}{\delta(q)} \le \left(\frac{\beta^2}{3} + \lambda_K\right)(u + d_1) + \left(\frac{\beta^2}{4\gamma} + \lambda_K\right) d_2 + \mathbb{E}\bigl[\tau_e H_{\beta,\gamma,\lambda_K}(V)\bigr].$$
By `Conjecture K` `6.3.31–6.3.32(c)`, $(d_1(e), d_2(e)) \in \Lambda(u, \chi) \subseteq \Lambda(u, \chi_{\mathrm{hi}})$ ($d_1^2 + d_2^2 \le \lambda_{\max}(J_1)^2 + \lambda_{\max}(I_2)^2 \le \frac{1}{2}\delta(q) = \frac{\chi}{2} u^2$), so maximizing the linear form over $\Lambda(u, \chi_{\mathrm{hi}})$ proves the inequality. Every sub-decomposition $c_1 \le (c_1)_+ = p_1 + q_1$, $c_2 \le (c_2)_+ = p_2 + q_2$ ($p_i, q_i \ge 0$) satisfies $c_1 d_1 + c_2 d_2 \le 2 u p_1 + u p_2 + u\sqrt{\chi_{\mathrm{hi}}/2}\sqrt{q_1^2 + q_2^2}$ by Cauchy–Schwarz on $d_1^2 + d_2^2 \le \frac{\chi_{\mathrm{hi}}}{2} u^2$. $\square$

### 2.2 Exponential Kantorovich Duality for $\mathbb{E}[\tau_e X]$

**Lemma 2.2.** *For $\tau_e \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1)$, any real random variable $X = H_{\beta,\gamma,\lambda_K}(V)$, knots $0 = t_0 < t_1 < \dots < t_M$, nondecreasing thresholds $x_1 \le \dots \le x_M$, prefix sums $K_m := \sum_{j=1}^m (t_j - t_{j-1}) x_j$ ($K_0 := 0$), and $\varepsilon_K > 0$,*
$$\mathbb{E}[\tau_e X] \le \sum_{m=1}^{M-1} x_m\bigl(e^{-t_{m-1}} - e^{-t_m}\bigr) + x_M e^{-t_{M-1}} + \frac{e^{-t_M}}{\varepsilon_K} + \mathbb{E}\bigl[\Phi_{\mathrm{Kant}}(X)\bigr],$$
*where $\Phi_{\mathrm{Kant}}(x) := \max_{0 \le m \le M}(t_m x - K_m) + \frac{\varepsilon_K}{2}(x - x_M)_+^2$ is convex and nondecreasing on $\mathbb{R}$, and for any $x \le \overline{H} \ge 0$ satisfies $\max_{0 \le m \le M}(t_m x - K_m) \le t_M \overline{H} + \max\bigl(0, -\min_{1 \le m \le M} K_m\bigr)$.*

*Proof.* Define the step function $\psi_0(\tau) := \sum_{m=1}^{M-1} x_m \mathbf{1}_{[t_{m-1}, t_m)}(\tau) + x_M \mathbf{1}_{[t_{M-1}, \infty)}(\tau)$. For any $x \in \mathbb{R}$, pick $m^* \in \{0, \dots, M\}$ with $x_{m^*} \le x \le x_{m^*+1}$ ($x_0 := -\infty, x_{M+1} := +\infty$). Since $(x_j)$ is nondecreasing, $x - \psi_0(s) \ge 0$ on $[0, t_{m^*})$ and $x - \psi_0(s) \le 0$ on $(t_{m^*}, t_M]$, so for every $\tilde{\tau} := \min(\tau, t_M) \in [0, t_M]$,
$$\tilde{\tau} x - \int_0^{\tilde{\tau}} \psi_0(s)\,ds = \int_0^{\tilde{\tau}} \bigl(x - \psi_0(s)\bigr)\,ds \le \int_0^{t_{m^*}} \bigl(x - \psi_0(s)\bigr)\,ds = t_{m^*} x - K_{m^*} \le \max_{0 \le m \le M}(t_m x - K_m).$$
Meanwhile, by Fubini's theorem for $\tau \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1)$, $\mathbb{E}\bigl[\int_0^{\min(\tau, t_M)} \psi_0(s)\,ds + (\tau - t_M)_+ x_M\bigr] = \int_0^{t_M} \psi_0(s) e^{-s}\,ds + x_M e^{-t_M} = \sum_{m=1}^{M-1} x_m(e^{-t_{m-1}} - e^{-t_m}) + x_M e^{-t_{M-1}}$, and for $\tau > t_M$, Young's inequality gives $(\tau - t_M)(x - x_M) \le \frac{(\tau - t_M)^2}{2\varepsilon_K} + \frac{\varepsilon_K}{2}(x - x_M)_+^2$ with $\int_{t_M}^\infty \frac{(\tau - t_M)^2}{2\varepsilon_K} e^{-\tau}\,d\tau = \frac{e^{-t_M}}{\varepsilon_K}$. Finally, if $x \le \overline{H}$ with $\overline{H} \ge 0$, then for every $0 \le m \le M$, $t_m x - K_m \le t_m \overline{H} - K_m \le t_M \overline{H} + \max(0, -\min_{1 \le m \le M} K_m)$ since $0 \le t_m \le t_M$. $\square$

### 2.3 Cone-$\mathcal{K}$ + Coupled-Gram Rational Stein Duality and Kummer Rice Polygon

Write $U := |q|^2 / u \ge 0$, and let $(v(Z), r(Z))$ denote the normalized radial Stein coordinates
$$v := \frac{\Gamma(q, \bar{q})}{3 u} - 1, \qquad r := \frac{\operatorname{Re}(\Gamma(q, q)\bar{q}^2)}{3 u |q|^2} \quad (r := 0 \text{ when } q = 0),$$
where $\Gamma(f, g) := \sum_{j=1}^n (\partial_j f\,\bar{\partial}_j g + \bar{\partial}_j f\,\partial_j g)$ is the bilinear Ornstein–Uhlenbeck carré du champ. Since $\Gamma(q, \bar{q}) = \sum_{j=1}^n (|\partial_j q|^2 + |\bar{\partial}_j q|^2) \ge 0$ and $|\Gamma(q, q)| = 2\bigl|\sum_{j=1}^n \partial_j q\,\bar{\partial}_j q\bigr| \le \Gamma(q, \bar{q})$ by $2|a b| \le |a|^2 + |b|^2$, $(v, r) \in \mathcal{K} := \{(v, r) \in \mathbb{R}^2 : 1 + v \ge 0,\; |r| \le 1 + v\}$ pointwise, and:
1. **Normalized moments & sixth moment:** $\mathbb{E}[1] = 1$, $\mathbb{E}[U] = 1$, $\mathbb{E}[v] = 0$, $\mathbb{E}[U^2 - 2 U v - U r] = 2$, $2 + \chi_{\mathrm{lo}} \le \mathbb{E}[U^2] \le 2 + \chi_{\mathrm{hi}}$, $\mathbb{E}[U^3] \le 6 + 9\chi_{\mathrm{hi}} + \frac{4045313}{100000}\chi_{\mathrm{hi}}^{3/2}$ (`P1` §1.3), and $\mathbb{E}[\sqrt{U}] \le \frac{2287}{2500}$ (`Conjecture K` `3.7`).
2. **Bihomogeneous $(2, 1)$ gradient identities & Coupled-Gram cuts (`Grothe/Lower/Gram.lean`):**
   $\mathbb{E}[U r - \frac{1}{3} U^2] \le -\frac{2}{3}$, $\mathbb{E}[-U r] \le 0$, $\mathbb{E}[v^2 - \frac{2}{3} U v] \le 0$, $\mathbb{E}[r^2 - \frac{2}{3} U r] \le 0$, and for every Coupled-Gram parameter $s_j \ge 2$, $\mathbb{E}\bigl[v^2 - \frac{s_j + 5}{9} U v + \frac{s_j + 1/2 - 3/s_j}{9} U r\bigr] \le 0$.
3. **Rational Stein identities (`Grothe/Lower/Stein.lean`):** For each pole $c_m > 0$ and $f_m(U) = (1 + U/c_m)^{-1}$, $\mathbb{E}[U f_m(U) - (1 + v)(f_m(U) + U f_m'(U)) - r U f_m'(U)] = 0$.
4. **Exact Cone-$\mathcal{K}$ quadratic conjugate:** For any $\lambda_S > 0, \mu_S > 0$ and $(A, B) \in \mathbb{R}^2$,
   $$\Psi_{\mathcal{K}}(A, B; \lambda_S, \mu_S) := \sup_{(v, r) \in \mathcal{K}}\bigl(-2 A v - 2 B r - \lambda_S v^2 - \mu_S r^2\bigr) = \begin{cases} \dfrac{A^2}{\lambda_S} + \dfrac{B^2}{\mu_S}, & \mu_S(\lambda_S - A) \ge \lambda_S |B|, \\[6pt] 2 A - \lambda_S + \dfrac{(\lambda_S - A + |B|)_+^2}{\lambda_S + \mu_S}, & \text{otherwise,} \end{cases}$$
   which is convex on $\mathbb{R}^2$ and hence maximized over any interval rectangle $[A_{\mathrm{lo}}, A_{\mathrm{hi}}] \times [B_{\mathrm{lo}}, B_{\mathrm{hi}}]$ at its four corners.
5. **Kummer + Cauchy–Schwarz rational Rice polygon:** Since $\rho(x) = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2} e^{-x}\,{}_1 F_1(\frac{3}{2}; 1; x) = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}\,{}_1 F_1(-\frac{1}{2}; 1; -x)$ has $\rho'(x) = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{4} e^{-x}\,{}_1 F_1(\frac{3}{2}; 2; x) > 0$ and $\rho''(x) = -\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{16} e^{-x}\,{}_1 F_1(\frac{3}{2}; 3; x) < 0$ for all $x \ge 0$ ($\rho''(0) = -\frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{16}$), every tangent line at a rational node $x_k \ge 0$ ($0 \le x_k \le 70$, 25 nodes) rounded outward to dyadic rationals $(a_k, b_k)$ with $a_k \ge \rho_{\mathrm{hi}}(x_k) - \rho'_{\mathrm{lo}}(x_k) x_k$ and $b_k \ge \rho'_{\mathrm{hi}}(x_k) > 0$ satisfies $\rho(x) \le a_k + b_k x$ for all $x \ge 0$. In addition, by Cauchy–Schwarz and AM–GM, $\rho(x) \le \sqrt{\mathbb{E}|Z + \sqrt{x}|^2} = \sqrt{x + 1} \le \frac{m^2 + 1}{2m} + \frac{x}{2m}$ for every $m > 0$ ($m \in \{12, 32, 100, 250\}$, 4 nodes), so $\rho(x) \le \min_{1 \le k \le 29}(a_k + b_k x)$ on $[0, \infty)$.
6. **Slice concavity elimination:** On each slice $m_1 = \mathbb{E}[V k'(V)] \in [a_r, b_r]$, convexity of $m_1 \mapsto m_1^2$ gives $m_1^2 \le (a_r + b_r) m_1 - a_r b_r$, so $\widehat{\omega}_h(q) \ge \frac{3 a_r b_r}{4 u} + \mathbb{E}\bigl[\frac{3}{2} V k'(V)^2 - \frac{3(a_r + b_r)}{4 u} V k'(V)\bigr]$.
