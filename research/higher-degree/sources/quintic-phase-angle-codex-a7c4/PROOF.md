Let $f,g$ be unimodular, $h=(f+g)/2$, $k=(f-g)/2$. Rotate the first-chaos projection of $h$ to $tZ$, where $Z$ is a standard proper complex Gaussian. Put $T=|Z|^2$, $K=\|k\|_2^2$, $D=1-K-t^2=\|h-tZ\|_2^2$, $A=\|P_{2,1}k\|_2^2$. For $t>0$,

$$A+t^2\le b\left(1+D/t^2\right),\qquad b=4277/5000.$$

Work in the frame $Z/|Z|$, and let $k_r,k_s$ denote the real and imaginary components in this frame. Since $\mathrm{Re}(\bar h k)=0$ and $|k|\le1$, pointwise $t|Z|\,|k_r|\le |h-tZ|$. Set $w=(3+T)/4$, so $\mathbb Ew=1$, and $B_T=\mathbb E(T/w)$. Orthogonality of the first projection gives $\mathbb E w|h|^2\ge t^2/B_T$ by Cauchy–Schwarz against $Z/\sqrt w$. Now put $q=P_{2,1}k/\sqrt A$ when $A>0$. Real Cauchy–Schwarz in the two components gives

$$ A=(\mathrm{Re}\,\mathbb E\bar q k)^2\le \left[\mathbb E(w|k|^2+Tk_r^2)\right] \left[\mathbb E(q_s^2/w+q_r^2/(w+T))\right]. $$

The first factor is at most $1-t^2/B_T+D/t^2$ because $|h|^2+|k|^2=1$. For $A=0$ the same reasoning with any unit cubic yields the theorem from $B_T\le b$ and $t^2\le B_T\mathbb E w|h|^2\le B_T$.

For the second factor use the standard independent-coordinate complex Hermite basis with normalized remote coefficients and sum of sector energies one. Write $F=1/w$, $G=1/(w+T)$. The $00,10,01,21$ prices are respectively bounded by $\mathbb E(F+G)/2$, $B_T$, $\mathbb ET(F+G)/2$ and $\mathbb ETF(T-2)^2/2$. These use $F\ge G$ independently of the complex phases. The $11/20$ block has diagonal prices $\mathbb E[(F+G)(T-1)^2]/2$ and $\mathbb E[(F+G)T^2]/4$. The off-diagonal coefficient has modulus at most $|\mathbb E[(F-G)(T-1)T]|/(2\sqrt2)$ times the product of normalized remote norms by Cauchy–Schwarz. Both row sums, including the absolute off-diagonal, are below $b$, as are the other four prices. Since normalized sector energies sum to one, the second factor is at most $b$. With $B_T\le b$ this proves the claim.
