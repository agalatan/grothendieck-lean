- The two symmetries used by the coverage check follow from the basis: under z → −z the six odd basis functions (coordinates 6–11) change sign, L → −L and (A, B) → (−B, −A); under z → conj z the functions 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11 go to minus their conjugates, the others to their conjugates, and (A, B) → (conj A, conj B). Ψ_ν is invariant under both, the Gaussian measure too, Q is block diagonal with these blocks and a lies in block 0. So J_s is invariant under the sign changes of coordinates {6,…,11} and of {3,4,5,8,9,11}; they act on (τ2, τ3) as (−τ2, −τ3) and (τ2, −τ3).
- Chords: for fixed m, s ↦ J_s(m) = F(m + s a) − mᵀQm is convex (F is a Gaussian integral of the convex function Ψ_ν of an affine argument), so Ψ = sup_m J_s is convex. A piece quadratic bounds sup over its region for every s, because the majorant is global in y = m + s a and the constraints and multipliers do not involve s. No table uses the Lipschitz rule or a `none` source.
- Anchored records are replayed on the full circle (4 × 64 cells) with the anchored generator's own price choice extended by symmetry; this is a different integration from the quarter-grid one of `nucert.py`.
- Side observation (`redundancy.py`, older layouts): 335 of 12,181 live route-M pieces are not needed for coverage, because the mirror image of another piece already covers their region (ring boxes at −τ3).
