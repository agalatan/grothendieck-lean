\section{A global upper bound from a signed resolvent certificate}
For a real polynomial, let $\|P\|_{\mathcal A}$ denote its absolute
coefficient sum. Haagerup's Gaussian phase kernel is
\[
 h(z)=\frac\pi4\sum_{k\ge0}r_kz|z|^{2k},\qquad
 r_0=1,\quad r_{k+1}=r_k\frac{(2k+1)^2}{4(k+1)(k+2)}.
\]
The Gaussian identity, tensor realization, complete residual estimate, and
matrix transfer are proved in Lean. The resulting bound has no external
analytic or numerical premises:
\[
 \boxed{K_G^{\mathbb C}\le\frac{1404909409}{1000000000}=1.404909409.}
\]

\subsection{A finite inverse certificate for the entire signed tail}
Let $A,T,R$ be real polynomials, with $\|A\|_{\mathcal A}\le1$, and suppose
\[
 \|1-R(1-A)\|_{\mathcal A}\le\eta<1,
 \qquad \|RT\|_{\mathcal A}\le B.
\]
For every polynomial $S$, the identity
$S=(1-R(1-A))S+R(1-A)S$ gives
\[
 (1-\eta)\|S\|_{\mathcal A}\le\|R(1-A)S\|_{\mathcal A}.
\]
Apply this to $S_n=\sum_{i<n}TA^i$. Since
$(1-A)S_n=T-TA^n$, submultiplicativity gives
\[
 \|S_n\|_{\mathcal A}\le\frac{2B}{1-\eta}.
\]
If $a_i\ge0$ decreases with $i$, finite summation by parts now yields
\[
 \left\|\sum_{i<n}a_iTA^i\right\|_{\mathcal A}
 \le a_0\frac{2B}{1-\eta}.
\]
Indeed, writing each weighted sum as the last weight times $S_n$ plus
$\sum_{i<n-1}(a_i-a_{i+1})S_{i+1}$ makes the weights telescope to $a_0$.
No infinite inverse or analytic continuation is invoked.

The Haagerup weights have decreasing first differences
$\Delta r_k=r_k-r_{k+1}\ge0$, since
\[
 \Delta r_k=r_k\frac{2k+7/4}{(k+1)(k+2)}
\]
and both factors decrease. Also $r_k\to0$, by summability.
Writing $W_n(a)=\sum_{i<n}a_{K+i}TA^i$, a second summation by parts gives
\[
 (1-A)W_n(r)=r_KT-AW_n(\Delta r)-r_{K+n}TA^n.
\]
If $\|RA\|_{\mathcal A}\le C$, the preceding Abel estimate on
$W_n(\Delta r)$ implies
\[
 \|W_n(r)\|_{\mathcal A}\le
 \frac{B}{1-\eta}\left(r_K+r_{K+n}+
 \frac{2C\Delta r_K}{1-\eta}\right).
\]
For $A=XP^2$ and $T=X^KP^{2K+1}$, each fixed coefficient prefix is
already determined by finitely many terms. Fix that prefix and let
$n\to\infty$, so the boundary term $r_{K+n}$ vanishes. Taking the
supremum over finite absolute coefficient sums yields
\[
 \left\|\sum_{k\ge K}r_kX^kP^{2k+1}\right\|_{\mathcal A}
 \le\frac{r_KB}{1-\eta}
 \left(1+\frac{2C}{1-\eta}\frac{\Delta r_K}{r_K}\right).
\]
This coefficient-local argument requires neither an infinite polynomial
inverse nor an exchange of two series.

\subsection{A short combined seed and exact data}
When $\|P\|_{\mathcal A}\le1$ and $\|P^4\|_{\mathcal A}\le q$, a short
combined product suffices to price $B$. If $s\le2K+1$ and
$\|RP^s\|_{\mathcal A}\le b$, then
\[
 \|RT\|_{\mathcal A}\le bq^{\lfloor(2K+1-s)/4\rfloor}.
\]
This follows by dividing the remaining exponent by four. The norm of
$RP^s$ is evaluated as a single polynomial, preserving cancellation between
$R$ and $P^s$.

The repository stores every rational coefficient in
\texttt{data/haagerup-resolvent/certificate.json}; the Lean data are generated
byte for byte from that file. Here $\deg P=14$, $\deg R=64$, and
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 K=1024,\qquad s=17,\qquad a=P(0)=
 \frac{1020379367568975}{1125899906842624},\\
 q=\frac{998903}{10^6},\quad b=\frac{249}{500},\quad
 \eta=10^{-12},\quad\gamma=\frac{637}{1000}.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Lean proves $\|P\|_{\mathcal A}\le1$ by exact rational arithmetic.
The native certificate checks the fourth-power, inverse-defect,
combined-seed, and finite-head budgets by outward integer arithmetic at
128-bit precision.
It also checks $r_K(K+1)(2K+1)\le\gamma$. The native Abel budget is
\[
 \frac{2b\gamma q^{508}}{(1025)(2049)(1-\eta)}
 <\frac{174}{10^9}.
\]
The exact resolvent list also proves $\|R-1\|_{\mathcal A}+\eta\le2$,
so $\|RA\|_{\mathcal A}\le2$ by $RA=(R-1)+(1-R(1-A))$.
At $K=1024$, $\Delta r_K/r_K=8199/4206600$. Therefore the second Abel
estimate bounds the normalized tail by
\[
 \frac{174}{10^9}\left(\frac12+
 \frac{2(8199/4206600)}{1-\eta}\right)
 <\frac{877}{10^{10}}.
\]
The full finite polynomial
$P\sum_{k<1024}r_k(XP^2)^k-a$ has degree below $29696$, and all its
coefficients are checked. Its norm is at most $95/10^9$. Hence
\[
 \|h\circ p-(\pi a/4)\operatorname{id}\|_{\mathcal A}
 \le\frac\pi4\frac{1827}{10^{10}},\qquad p(t)=tP(t^2).
\]
The proved residual transfer and a proved lower enclosure of $\pi$ give
\[
 K_G^{\mathbb C}\le\frac4{\pi(a-1827/10^{10})}<1.404909409.
\]
An independent directed FLINT replay checks the complete finite polynomial
and seed; exact rational arithmetic checks the inverse defect and power
budget. These external replays audit the data; the Lean theorem has no
hypotheses requiring their results.

The obstruction for simpler preprocessors remains exact: if
$|P(-1)|=\|P\|_{\mathcal A}=1$, then $\|P^n\|_{\mathcal A}=1$ for every
$n$, by evaluation at $-1$ and submultiplicativity. Breaking that common
boundary phase permits a power contraction. The resolvent certificate
additionally retains cancellation \emph{between} consecutive composed
powers. The sharper conditional endpoints are separate and unchanged.
